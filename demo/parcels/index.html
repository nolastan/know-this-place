Lots instead of dots

Three working prototypes of drawing each building as its actual lot, from the city's parcel outlines (DataSF acdm-wktn, 227,968 active parcels, which collapse to 154,972 lots once condo units share their building's lot). 26,426 of our pages matched a lot: 25,190 by APN, 1,236 by their point, 49 not placed.

  1. A. Lots over a basemap — the homepage map as it is, but past zoom 15 each dot cross-fades into its lot outline. Shown over a keyless OpenFreeMap basemap because the Mapbox token only works on knowthis.place and localhost:8517; the layers are the same code in Mapbox GL JS. Jump to the Castro.
  2. B. Lots without a basemap — no tile server. The map is drawn from four files we build ourselves: the city's land, every lot (fetched about 1 km at a time as you pan), street centrelines, and street names on them. Where lots meet, the gap between blocks is the street, so the grid draws itself. Jump to Jackson Square.
  3. C. A locator band with no JavaScript — the strip at the top of an address page, drawn at build time as inline SVG instead of a Mapbox static image. The building's lot is filled; every documented neighbour is a plain link to its page. 4548 18th St · 600 Montgomery · 999 Green · 3 Boynton Ct · 1000 Valencia
