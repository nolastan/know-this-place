  1. 1880

  2. November 5, 1970

    A two-storey building, about 40 feet tall, held a store on the ground floor and two flats above, on an irregular 2,539-square-foot lot also fronting 18th and Storrie Streets.

    San Francisco City Planning Commission minutes, San Francisco Public Library (Internet Archive)
  3. December 3, 1970

    The City Planning Commission refused to rezone the lot from R-3 to R-4 for a seven-unit apartment building to replace the old building, finding it would be spot zoning.

    San Francisco City Planning Commission minutes, San Francisco Public Library (Internet Archive)
  4. February 4, 1971

    The Board of Supervisors had upheld on appeal the City Planning Commission's disapproval of reclassifying the property from R-3 to R-4.

    San Francisco City Planning Commission minutes, San Francisco Public Library (Internet Archive)
