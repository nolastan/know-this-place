Merchant page prototypes

Three ways to make a page work as the landing page for a business that has no website of its own, each tried on three dry cleaners. Demos only: static copies of the real pages, not wired into the build.

  • A · Address page, business first. A "Trading here now" card under the heading, and business openings in the timeline. No new URL.
  • B · A page for the business, one level under its address (/4548/c-h-cleaners/).
  • C · A page for the storefront, whoever trades there (/2162/storefront/).
