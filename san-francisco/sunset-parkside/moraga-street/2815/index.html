Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

StyleStreamline Moderne

An example of the Streamline Moderne style: flat roof, rounded corners, glass block, porthole windows, applied speedlines. It was the first modern style offered to modest-income San Francisco house buyers, and fewer than one Sunset District facade in ten used it.
