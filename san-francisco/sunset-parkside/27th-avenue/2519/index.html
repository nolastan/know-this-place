  1. 1908

    Built by the Parkside Realty Company as one of 62 near-identical cottages put up across seven blocks to bring the first residents into the new district — about 800 square feet on one storey, two bedrooms and a bath, sold with a choice of six façade treatments over a single plan. A 2007 walking survey found all but one of the group still standing, almost all of them since raised over a basement garage.

    Parkside District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2008
