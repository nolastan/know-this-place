  1. 1900s

    Developed by Sol Getz & Son, the realty firm that graded and sold much of the north-western Oceanside. Getz would level a block, lease the flat lots on terms requiring the tenant to build within a set period, then sell the property to that tenant within the year.

    Oceanside Historic Context Statement, adopted 2012
