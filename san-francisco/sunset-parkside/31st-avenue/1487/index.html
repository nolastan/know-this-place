Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

StyleArt Deco

Art Deco detailing: stepped roofline, ziggurat forms, bold geometry and no historicist ornament. Art Deco houses are very rare in San Francisco; the Depression cut the style short.

Modernistic Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

StyleArt Deco
Builder as surveyedL.L. & M.L. Wold
Year built as surveyed1937

An Art Deco tract house built in 1937 by L.L. & M.L. Wold, illustrated three times in the statement. It shows bold geometric elements and a vertical emphasis, but the statement judges it too restrained to be individually eligible under Criterion C/3 — it could contribute to a district alongside more detailed examples.
