Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

StyleTudor Revival

An example of the Tudor Revival style: steeply pitched forward-facing gable, applied half-timbering, ganged casement windows in square surrounds. The Sunset District has no Tudor Revival tracts; the style appears one house at a time among other revival fronts.
