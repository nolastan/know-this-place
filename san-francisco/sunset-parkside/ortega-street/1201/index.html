Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

Survey findingIndividually significant
StyleMission Revival

Built as the Infant Shelter and now the San Francisco Conservatory of Music to designs by Louis Christian Mullgardt — an institutional Mission Revival building that keeps a high level of integrity, with red tile roofs, shaped parapets in stucco, an unusual bell tower at the entrance and carved stonework around the entry.

The assessor dates the building to 1975, after this photograph was taken. Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. Submit an update
