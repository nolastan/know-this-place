Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

Stylebarrel front Mediterranean Revival

The first house Henry Doelger built in the Sunset District. Later the same year he began his first whole tract on this block, a solid run of barrel front Mediterranean Revival houses between Judah and Kirkham Streets with alternating parapet forms, put forward as a candidate for its own historic district survey.
