Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

Bought by the California Veterans' Welfare Board shortly after it was finished. The board ran state-funded amortized mortgages for World War I veterans a decade before the FHA did the same nationally, and owned 52 houses in the Sunset District in 1937 alone.
