  1. 1936

    A new Francis Scott Key Elementary School was built here with Public Works Administration funding after the 1933 school bond, replacing a school two blocks away that had been judged a fire risk. Edward Eames, William Mooser and Douglas D. Stone designed it in concrete and terra cotta in the PWA Moderne style: academic building 1936, kindergarten wing 1939, auditorium 1940.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  2. 2023

    Recommended for city landmark designation in 2023, on historical, aesthetic and artistic grounds.

Francis Scott Key Elementary School, built in 1938 and named as one of the San Francisco schools designed with Art Deco detailing.

The city dates the school's buildings differently in two of its own statements: the statement on modernistic styles gives 1938, while the New Deal statement has the academic building in 1936, the kindergarten wing in 1939 and the auditorium in 1940. The assessor's roll gives 1935. Not yet documented: the architect and builder, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale.
