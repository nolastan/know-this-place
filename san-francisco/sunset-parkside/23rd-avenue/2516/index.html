  1. 1920

    The deed recorded here set a floor of $2,500 on the house to be built — one of the building restrictions the Parkside Realty Company borrowed from the residence parks. The permit taken out for the construction put its value at $1,500. W. E. Collins was the architect.

    Parkside District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2008
  2. before 1925

    Picked out by a 2007 walking survey of the Parkside as a house predating the stucco “Sunset” row house that filled the district from the 1920s. Most houses on that list are larger Craftsman-style buildings put up by the Parkside Realty Company before 1925; the survey was informal and its authors called it incomplete.

    Parkside District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2008
