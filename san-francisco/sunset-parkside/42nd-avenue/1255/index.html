  1. 1911

    One of a row of fifteen Craftsman houses Alonzo Harrington built along this block between 1911 and 1913 — one of only two long rows put up in the neighbourhood before 1915. The row is largely intact, and this house has been restored, with earlier unsympathetic modernisations reversed.

    Oceanside Historic Context Statement, adopted 2012
