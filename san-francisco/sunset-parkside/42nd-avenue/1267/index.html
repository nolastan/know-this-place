Developed and built in 1913 by Alphonso Harrington: a Dutch Colonial Revival house with a gambrel roof, brick and sash windows, contributing to the eligible Alphonso Harrington 42nd Avenue Historic District. Its massing and detail are standard for the style, which suits it better as a contributor than as an individually significant building.
