  1. 1924

    The San Francisco Protestant Orphanage moved to the Parkside and built here on the “cottage” plan then replacing the single dormitory — six residential cottages around an administration building, meant to raise children in something closer to family groups. The campus is now the Edgewood Center for Children and Families.

    Parkside District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2008
