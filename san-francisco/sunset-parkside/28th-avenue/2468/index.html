  1. before 1925

    Picked out by a 2007 walking survey of the Parkside as a house predating the stucco “Sunset” row house that filled the district from the 1920s. Most houses on that list are larger Craftsman-style buildings put up by the Parkside Realty Company before 1925; the survey was informal and its authors called it incomplete.
