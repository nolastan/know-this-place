Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

StyleStreamline Moderne

One of five adjacent Streamline Moderne houses put up together in 1938, one of only a handful of such groupings in the Sunset District, where the style was almost always scattered singly among revival fronts. When the district was surveyed in 2012 only one of the five still showed its original design.
