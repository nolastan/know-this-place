Modernistic Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

StyleStreamline Moderne
Year built as surveyed1938

One of five adjacent, nearly identical Streamline Moderne houses built in 1938 by the Golden Gate Investment Company. This one has been heavily altered — a new window opening and sash, a new garage door, brick cladding at the ground storey and the glass block accents removed — leaving only the speed lines and chimney stack of the original design. The statement says it would have qualified as significant as built, but its compromised integrity renders it ineligible; of the five, just one retains its integrity.
