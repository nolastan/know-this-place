  1. 1908

    Built by the Parkside Realty Company as one of 62 near-identical cottages put up across seven blocks to bring the first residents into the new district — about 800 square feet on one storey, two bedrooms and a bath, sold with a choice of six façade treatments over a single plan. All but one of the group was still standing a century later, almost all of them raised over a basement garage in the meantime.

    Parkside District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2008
  2. 1930s

    The Parkside's first circulating library was run privately out of this cottage, before the district had a branch of its own.

    Parkside District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2008
