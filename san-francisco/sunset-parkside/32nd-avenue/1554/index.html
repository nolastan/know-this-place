Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement (2013)

StylePueblo

Pueblo influence: boxy massing under a flat roof, stucco, and projecting vigas imitating the roof beams of adobe building. The style is a southwestern one and was almost never applied to San Francisco tract houses.
