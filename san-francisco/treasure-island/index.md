# Treasure Island

The city's Treasure Island analysis neighborhood: the flat man-made island
built for the 1939 Golden Gate International Exposition, and the natural
Yerba Buena Island it is joined to. Coverage begins with the first building
completed in the island's redevelopment.

## Streets documented so far

- [Avenue Of The Palms](avenue-of-the-palms/) — 1 building.

## Adjacent neighborhoods

The island touches no neighborhood by land. The city's Analysis Neighborhoods
boundary file gives it one neighbour, [Bayview Hunters
Point](../bayview-hunters-point/), across open water to the south; the only
ground route on or off is the Bay Bridge, which reaches the city at [South of
Market](../south-of-market/) and Alameda County the other way.
