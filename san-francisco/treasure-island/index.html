Treasure Island

The city's Treasure Island analysis neighborhood: the flat man-made island built for the 1939 Golden Gate International Exposition, and the natural Yerba Buena Island it is joined to. Coverage begins with the first building completed in the island's redevelopment.

Adjacent neighborhoods

The island touches no neighborhood by land. The city's Analysis Neighborhoods boundary file gives it one neighbour, Bayview Hunters Point, across open water to the south; the only ground route on or off is the Bay Bridge, which reaches the city at South of Market and Alameda County the other way.
