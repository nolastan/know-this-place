Year built as surveyed1969

The cross-plan Annex A addition to the Jewish Home for the Aged campus by Howard A. Friedman, with board-formed concrete and brick exteriors and circulation cores expressed at the sides and ends of the plan. The statement judges it short of the sculptural quality of the best Brutalism and not individually significant.
