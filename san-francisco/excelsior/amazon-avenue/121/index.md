# 121 Amazon Avenue

A six-room single-storey house of 1930 in the Excelsior, recorded by published
guides as Jerry Garcia's first home.

## The building

- Built 1930 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1930)
- 1 unit, 6 rooms, 2 baths, 1 story; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 1,482 sq ft; lot 2,265 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-1; Supervisorial District 11
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Jerry Garcia as
living here until 1947, when he was five and his father died. The guide
separately places the household at [87 Harrington
Street](../../../outer-mission/harrington-street/87/) for the five years that
followed. Neither claim carries an underlying source.

## Permit history

No building permits are on file. The DBI dataset reaches back to the
mid-1970s and returns nothing for this address — an absence of records, not
evidence that nothing was done.

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **3 January 2018**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $828,337 and improvements at $354,999 — $1,183,336 total. The
land is worth more than twice what sits on it, which is the ordinary pattern
for a small single-storey house on an Excelsior lot.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, and any primary-source
confirmation of the Garcia residency or its end date.
