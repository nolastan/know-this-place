# Excelsior

A working-class residential district in the city's south-east, laid out in the early 20th century and named, like its streets, after countries and cities of the world.

## Streets documented so far

- [Amazon Avenue](amazon-avenue/) — A 1930 house recorded as Jerry Garcia's first home.
- [Moscow Street](moscow-street/) — 1 building.
- [Russia Avenue](russia-avenue/) — 1 building, built 1915.
