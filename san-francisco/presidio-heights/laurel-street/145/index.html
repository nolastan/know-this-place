  1. 1920

    Originally the Hillard, designed by Alfred Henry Jacobs at the corner of Washington and Laurel streets. A $350,000 Class A building announced in 1919 and featured in the Architect and Engineer that September; a whole floor was given over to staff rooms reachable by telephone and a service elevator. A metal and glass marquee shelters the entrance.

    Large Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
