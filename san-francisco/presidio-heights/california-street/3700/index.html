  1. 1906

    The hospital buildings of 1914, 1929, 1954 and 1966 replaced earlier buildings on the block that had survived the earthquake and fire.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Children's Hospital of San Francisco: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, 1979.
  2. 1914

    Building C of Children's Hospital, on the northeast corner of California and Cherry Streets, was completed.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Children's Hospital of San Francisco: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, 1979.
  3. 1914

    Building B, fronting California Street, was completed; a further section followed in 1929.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Children's Hospital of San Francisco: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, 1979.
  4. 1929

    A second section of Building B was completed fifteen years after the first.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Children's Hospital of San Francisco: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, 1979.
  5. 1954

    The taller of the hospital's concrete buildings, fronting Sacramento Street, was completed; its companion followed in 1966.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Children's Hospital of San Francisco: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, 1979.
