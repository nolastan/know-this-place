  1. 1933

    The Jewish Community Center built here in 1933, a privately funded building in the PWA Moderne style. It was demolished in 2000.

    The Jewish Community Center of San Francisco, where members of the newly formed Congregation Sha'ar Zahav joined regular Friday services in its early years.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
