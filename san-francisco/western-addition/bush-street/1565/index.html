  1. 1923

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a public garage. Inverness Garage No. 1, a public garage for 18 years. Its paired metal vehicle doors with full-length glazing are the last of their kind on the avenue.
