  2. 1930

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a tire shop. Tire dealers have held the building for much of its life — recorded here 1932–1937, 1941–1942 and 1951–1964, selling Firestone tires 1951–1953 and Goodyear 1954–1964.
