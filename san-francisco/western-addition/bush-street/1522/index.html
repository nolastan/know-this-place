  1. 1916

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a parts store/repair shop. Brick-built as an automobile supplies store, with a row of now-rare pivoting wooden windows in the second storey.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
