Bethel A.M.E. Church occupies two adjoining parcels, 970 and 916 Laguna Street. This page covers 970, the address the congregation uses.

Earlier record

  1. 1969–1973

    The Bethel A.M.E. Church buildings at 916–970 Laguna Street were constructed.

    San Francisco Heritage
  2. 1862

    The congregation bought a frame building on Powell Street between Jackson and Pacific for $5,500, and met on that site until the 1940s.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 2, Winter 2021
  3. 1860s

    The congregation held supper-dances at Assembly Hall, at Post and Kearny Streets — supper with the clergy, dancing after they left.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 2, Winter 2021
  4. 1852–1854

    The Reverend Thomas M. D. Ward moved his congregation into a carpenter shop on Scott Street, refitted for religious services, until he could buy a larger building on Powell Street.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 2, Winter 2021
