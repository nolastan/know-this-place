  1. 1940s

    The Bethel African Methodist Episcopal congregation took over the former Seventh Day Adventist Church here — one of the mainline Protestant churches the 1950 Sanborn maps show supplanted by African-American congregations.

    Market & Octavia Historic Context Statement, adopted 2007
  2. August 24, 1964

    Photographed, showing the Bethel A.M.E. Methodist Church.

    San Francisco Subjects Photograph Collection (SFP 162), San Francisco Public Library
  3. 1969

    Bethel A.M.E. bought a disused church on this site in the late 1950s and, when it proved too cramped, replaced it with a new building put up between 1969 and 1973. Identified in 2024 as eligible for local landmark, California Register or National Register designation.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024

Bethel A.M.E. Church, clad in gridded concrete and metal panels, its Late Modernism carried in the geometric interplay of sculptural forms and vertical planes. The architect is not recorded.
