StylePostmodernism
Architect as surveyedBeverly Willis
Year built as surveyed1978

The Margaret Hayward Playground building by Beverly Willis, one of the earliest known built examples of architectural Postmodernism in San Francisco: a playful stage-set form with a Palladian doorway and a blue and dusty pink colour scheme. Demolished in 2019.
