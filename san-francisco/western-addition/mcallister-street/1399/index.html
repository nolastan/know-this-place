Earlier record

  1. Nov 2017

    The Third Baptist Church Complex was designated an Article 10 individual landmark by Ordinance 226-17, for its association with the congregation's role in the advancement of African Americans in San Francisco, with the Reverend Frederick Douglas Haynes Sr., and as one of the first Midcentury Modern ecclesiastical buildings in the Western Addition.

    Planning Code Article 10, Appendix A
  2. 1959

    The cross that stands atop the bell tower was added.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  3. 1956

    The youth and classroom building, which houses the gymnasium on its second floor, was designed by architect Alfred W. Johnson and built as an addition.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  4. 1952

    The congregation moved to the Western Addition and commissioned architect William F. Gunnison to design the sanctuary and the detached bell tower that fronts McAllister Street.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  5. 1932–1971

    The Reverend Frederick Douglas Haynes Sr. served as pastor. He ran for the San Francisco Board of Supervisors in 1945.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  6. 1908

    The congregation built a church at Hyde and Clay Streets on Nob Hill, where it remained for 44 years.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  7. 1896

    The congregation moved from Dupont Street to a building at Bush and Powell Streets, which the 1906 earthquake and fire destroyed.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  8. 1855

    The congregation took the name Third Baptist Church, as the third Baptist communion founded in the city and to be an inclusive church without a racial designation.

    SF Planning landmark designation report, 2017
  9. 1852

    The congregation was founded as the First Colored Baptist Church of San Francisco, the first African American Baptist congregation west of the Rocky Mountains. Its first building stood on Dupont Street, now 1640–1644 Grant Avenue.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 2, Winter 2021
