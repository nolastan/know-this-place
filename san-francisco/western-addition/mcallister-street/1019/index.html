Friendship Village by Bulkley & Sazevich, in the Western Addition A-2 redevelopment area, which kept a Victorian-era facade as a gateway into its central courtyard. The complex has the massing, flat roof and regular fenestration of a Late Modernist building, with shingle siding nodding to the Third Bay Tradition. The old facade survives, now closed off by a security gate.
