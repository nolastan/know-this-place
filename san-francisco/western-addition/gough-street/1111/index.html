The Cathedral of St. Mary of the Assumption by Pier Luigi Nervi, Pietro Belluschi, John Michael Lee, Paul A. Ryan and Angus McSweeney - called one of the most iconic buildings in the city and an excellent example of the Expressionist mode of Late Modernism. It appears eligible under Criterion C/3, with the plaza and the main interior space, including the parabolic concrete piers, organ, ceiling and stained glass, significant in their own right.
