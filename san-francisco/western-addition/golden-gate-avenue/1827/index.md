# 1827 Golden Gate Avenue

A twelve-unit apartment building of 1926, seismically retrofitted under the
city's soft-storey programme in 2017–19.

## The building

- Built 1926 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1926)
- 12 units, 30 rooms, 12 baths, 3 stories; assessor class "Apartment 5 to 14
  Units"
- Building area 9,513 sq ft; lot 4,125 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RM-1; Supervisorial District 5
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district

The assessor files the parcel under North Panhandle; the city's analysis
neighbourhood layer places it in Western Addition. This site follows the
analysis neighbourhood.

## 1974

A published guide to notable San Francisco addresses lists this among several
buildings it connects with the 1974 kidnapping of Patty Hearst by the
Symbionese Liberation Army. It gives no dates for this address and cites no
underlying source. The other addresses it names include [286–288 Precita
Avenue](../../../bernal-heights/precita-avenue/286/).

## Permit history

Eleven permits are on file, 1992–2023; two are street-space filings at a
nominal valuation. The largest by far is the mandatory soft-storey seismic
retrofit — $87,000, filed in 2017 and signed off in April 2019 after a revision
for field conditions.

- **2023** — fire alarm upgrade to SF Fire Code 1103.7.6.1 ($30,453 revised) —
  [permit 202308073877](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202308073877)
- **2021** — replace water-damaged kitchen cabinets and upgrade kitchen wiring
  in two apartments ($30,000) —
  [permit 202101263299](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202101263299)
- **2019** — revision to the retrofit for field conditions ($5,000) —
  [permit 201901180697](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201901180697)
- **2017** — mandatory soft-storey seismic retrofit under AB-107 / IEBC A4
  ($87,000) —
  [permit 201705166680](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201705166680)
- **2000** — install vinyl siding not visible from the street ($9,000),
  expired —
  [permit 200005018683](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200005018683)
- **1995** — six vinyl windows in existing frames ($2,235) —
  [permit 9516305](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9516305)
- **1993** — 12 windows in existing frames ($3,550) —
  [permit 9306973](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9306973)
- **1992** — 14 vinyl windows ($5,290) —
  [permit 9213512](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9213512)
- **1992** — convert the mechanical room to a laundry room ($1,500), expired —
  [permit 9211169](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9211169)

Between 1992 and 1995 the building's windows were replaced in three separate
filings, 32 windows in total.

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **31 August 1989**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $873,900 and improvements at $735,914 — $1,609,814 total.
