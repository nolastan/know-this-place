  2. 1906

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a retail; auto repair and sales 1. Built as a temporary clothing store for Roos Brothers while downtown was rebuilt after the earthquake and fire. It is the only one of Van Ness Avenue's temporary post-fire commercial buildings still standing.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  3. 1910

    Given over to automobile uses from 1910: an auto repair shop 1910–1916, new car sales 1917–1922, and used-car salesrooms for 34 years between 1923 and 1964.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
