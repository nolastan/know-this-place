  2. 1926

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. Dated 1926–1927. Designed by Bernard Maybeck with Powers and Ahnden as associated architects, for the Packard dealership of Earle C. Anthony. The most theatrical of Auto Row's showrooms, and one of the two with the most elaborate interiors.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
