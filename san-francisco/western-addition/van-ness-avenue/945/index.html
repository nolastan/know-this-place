  1. 1937

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. Designed by John E. Dinwiddie in the Art Deco and Streamlined Moderne manner, for the Chevrolet dealership of Ernest Ingold. It was the last auto showroom built on Van Ness Avenue. A Chevrolet dealership into the 1960s, and a Chevrolet dealership under a second name after that.
