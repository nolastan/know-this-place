  1. 1917

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto shop and garage. The survey dates the work to 1917 and 1925. The first story was designed by Willis Polk and Company; the same firm added the second story in 1925, a year after Polk's death. It has profiled cornices, a chamfered corner at Eddy Street and a wooden classical entrance in the cornice. Three businesses at a time in its early years: auto repair shops from 1917, an auto painting shop 1918–1932, and a public garage 1919–1942. Later the whole building was one dealer's service shop.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
