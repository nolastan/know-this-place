  2. 1948

    Frank Lloyd Wright's only building in San Francisco, designed 1948 as the V.C. Morris gift shop; a brick-clad retail storefront whose arched doorway echoes Richardsonian Romanesque precedent. Designated City Landmark No. 72 in 1975, at the age of 26.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
  3. 1949

    Frank Lloyd Wright's remodel of 1948-49 for the V. C. Morris Gift Shop, which set itself deliberately against the open-front storefronts of its day: a closed front, with the display kept for those who came inside.

    Neighborhood Commercial Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
