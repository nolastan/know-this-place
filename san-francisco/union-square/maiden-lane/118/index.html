  1. 1909

    The Lloyd Building was completed in 1909 to plans drawn in late 1906 by the architect M. J. Lyon, with the John Ince Construction Co. as contractor: a twentieth-century classical revival commercial block on a lot forty feet square, brick walls with wood framing, faced in white glazed brick. It was cleared for the building of 2001.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 116-124 Maiden Lane: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 98.833E, 1999.
