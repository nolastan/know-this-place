Public art

  • Hothouse (Blue) — Teresita Fernandez
    Glass, cabochon mirrors; Louis Vuitton entrance; artwork is accessible Monday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., and Sunday 12:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
    Installed 2008. Hothouse (Blue) is a permanent artwork by Teresita Fernández (born 1968, Miami, American), commissioned by Louis Vuitton and unveiled at its San Francisco Union Square store with a reception and dinner with the artist on June 2, 2008. The city's inventory credits Teresita Fernandez; the source cited here gives Teresita Fernández.
