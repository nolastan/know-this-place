Crocker Tower, now Post Montgomery Center, by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. It has the rectangular form of a typical Late Modernist high-rise but introduces pattern and ornament to its polished granite facade; with the Galleria it marks the arrival of Postmodernism downtown.
