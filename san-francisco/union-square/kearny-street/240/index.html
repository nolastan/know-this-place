  1. 1979

    The Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage rated the Marston Building "B" in the 1979 downtown survey it published as Splendid Survivors — the second of its four grades, for buildings it judged individually important for their architecture, their history and their setting. The Department of City Planning's 1976 Architectural Inventory did not rate it.
