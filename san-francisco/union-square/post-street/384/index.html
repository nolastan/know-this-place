  1. 1924

    The Fitzhugh Building was designed in 1922 and finished in the middle of 1924 to the design of James and Merritt Reid — a ten-storey tower 137 feet high fronting Post Street, built as medical and dental offices, with a one-storey extension to the north.

    Saks Fifth Avenue, Union Square Project: Final Environmental Impact Report, 1978
  2. January 20, 1976

    Listed on the National Register of Historic Places on 20 January 1976, after the Landmarks Preservation Advisory Board had twice declined to recommend it for city landmark status and then voted 4 to 3 to recommend the National Register listing.

    Saks Fifth Avenue, Union Square Project: Final Environmental Impact Report, 1978
