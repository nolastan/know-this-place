  1. 1909

    Built for the Argonaut Club, an early Jewish club formed at a time when antisemitism kept Jews out of the city's other private clubs. Unusually for a private club, it has shopfronts at street level.

    Citywide Historic Context Statement: Clubs and Social Halls (1848–1989), 2026
