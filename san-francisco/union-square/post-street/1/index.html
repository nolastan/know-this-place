Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

Architect as surveyedSasaki, Walker & Associates
Year built as surveyed1969

The sunken plaza by Sasaki, Walker & Associates, its tiered steps with chamfered corners wrapping a BART entrance. When new, the sunny steps filled with office workers at lunchtime - an early local demonstration of the activated urban space that later guided the city's open-space rules.
