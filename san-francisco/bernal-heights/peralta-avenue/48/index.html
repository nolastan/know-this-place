Earthquake Shacks Theme Document (2021)

Survey findingNot eligible for the National Register or the California Register

The building matches a Type C earthquake relief shack in scale, massing and form, but later alterations removed the board-and-batten siding, the galvanized metal chimney and the redwood shingle roof, leaving too little to carry the association.
