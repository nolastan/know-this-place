# Bernal Heights

A hilly residential district south of the Mission, built up from the late 19th century with small houses on modest lots.

## Streets documented so far

- [College Avenue](college-avenue/) — 1 building, built 1928.
- [Cortland Avenue](cortland-avenue/) — 1 building, built 1910.
- [Crescent Avenue](crescent-avenue/) — 1 building, built 1900.
- [Mullen Avenue](mullen-avenue/) — A 1,000 sq ft house of 1906, recorded as Carlos Santana's home during high school.
- [Precita Avenue](precita-avenue/) — Four units of 1905 at the foot of the hill, a 1974 Patty Hearst address.
- [Virginia Avenue](virginia-avenue/) — 1 building, built 1910.
