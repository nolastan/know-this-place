# 286–288 Precita Avenue

Four units over three storeys on a 1,875-square-foot lot at the foot of Bernal
Heights — one parcel carrying both street numbers, and the smallest lot
documented on this site.

## The building

- Built 1905 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1905)
- 4 units, 13 rooms, 4 baths, 3 stories; assessor class "1 Flat &
  Dwelling-1 Parcel"
- Building area 2,771 sq ft on a lot of 1,875 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-2; Supervisorial District 9
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district
- The parcel spans 286 and 288 Precita Avenue; the article that prompted this
  page gives the address as 288

## 1974

A published guide to notable San Francisco addresses lists this among several
buildings it connects with the 1974 kidnapping of Patty Hearst by the
Symbionese Liberation Army. It gives no dates for this address and cites no
underlying source. The other addresses it names include [1827 Golden Gate
Avenue](../../../western-addition/golden-gate-avenue/1827/).

## Permit history

Four permits are on file, and all three of the older ones sit in the 1980s.
The sequence starts with fire damage: in June 1984 a permit was filed to repair
the back of the building after a fire, and it expired. A larger job to prepare
all the walls for sheetrock and insulate, filed seven weeks later, was
cancelled. Whatever finally satisfied the city came in 1987, on a $1,000
permit whose stated scope was simply to bring the building into code.

- **2003** — repair the rear stairs ($2,000) —
  [permit 200312313252](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200312313252)
- **1987** — bring the building into code ($1,000) —
  [permit 8700548](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8700548)
- **1984** — prepare all walls for sheetrock and install insulation
  ($28,000), cancelled —
  [permit 8408616](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8408616)
- **1984** — repair fire damage at the back of the building ($4,500),
  expired —
  [permit 8406611](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8406611)

## Value

The 2025 roll assesses land at $73,943 and improvements at $147,907 —
$221,850 total, with no sale on the current roll. Improvements are carried at
exactly twice the land value, which is the reverse of the usual San Francisco
pattern and follows from the long-held assessment base.
