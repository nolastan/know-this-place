Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StylePostmodernism
Architect as surveyedRobert Mittelstadt
Year built as surveyed1975-1979

A Bernal Heights duplex by Robert Mittelstadt, named as the first example of Postmodern residential architecture in San Francisco: the gabled form of a typical rowhouse in unfinished wood siding, a bay window capped by a projecting fanlight, a porthole window, wood lattice where a Victorian would carry ornament, and four Doric columns hanging from the ceiling of the double-height upper unit. Published on the cover of Global Architecture's GA Houses 11. Now painted, with a replaced garage door, but otherwise intact and found eligible for the California Register.
