  1. 1900

  2. 1935–1941

    The Works Progress Administration excavated and graded the site, built 75 feet of rubble wall and paved 16,000 square feet of playground behind the Bernal Heights branch library.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  3. 1936

    The Bernal Heights branch library, 1936, by Frederick H. Meyer, a Public Works Administration project.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
  4. 1940

    One of only two substantial buildings the Works Progress Administration put up for the city, this $41,709 branch library was designed by the veteran architect Frederick H. Meyer in a blend of Classical Revival and Mission Revival motifs and completed in 1940, with an adjoining playground. Its main reading room has decorative ceiling stencilling by unrecorded artists.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
