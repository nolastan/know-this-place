# 205 Mullen Avenue

A 1,000-square-foot single-storey house of 1906 on the north slope of Bernal
Heights — the smallest building documented on this site.

## The building

- Built 1906 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1906)
- 1 unit, 6 rooms, 1 bath, 1 story; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 1,000 sq ft; lot 1,750 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-2; Supervisorial District 9
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Carlos Santana as
living here while attending Mission High School. No dates are given and no
underlying source is cited.

## Permit history

Four permits are on file, 2005–2024.

- **2024** — repair dry rot in a section of rear wall framing and remove wood
  in contact with subarea soil, per structural pest report 240619A107
  ($4,700). Filed five days after the recorded sale and signed off six days
  later —
  [permit 202408078161](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202408078161)
- **2017** — revision to the remodel: strip drywall and insulation for
  additional electrical work in the living room, dining room and bedrooms
  ($5,000) —
  [permit 201704053215](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201704053215)
- **2017** — new kitchen, and new 2×8 ceiling joists at 14 inches on centre
  sistered to the existing 2×4s to carry recessed lighting ($24,000) —
  [permit 201701106802](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201701106802)
- **2005** — remove an illegal unit in the basement ($23,000), expired —
  [permit 200507056828](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200507056828)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **2 August 2024** — the most
recent of any address documented here. Because the sale reset the base, the
2025 figures track market value more closely than most: land $1,120,000,
improvements $480,000, $1,600,000 total. Both are round to the nearest
$10,000.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, whether the basement unit the
2005 permit proposed removing was ever removed, and any primary-source basis
for the Santana residency.
