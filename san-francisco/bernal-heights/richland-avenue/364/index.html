Earthquake Shacks Theme Document (2021)

Survey findingEligible for the National Register and the California Register under Criterion A/1 (Events); not eligible under Criterion C/3

A Type C earthquake relief shack, later raised to insert a garage beneath it. It keeps its redwood board-and-batten siding, pitched gable roof, galvanized metal chimney and a plan of about 15 by 25 feet; the raising and the garage are why it was found ineligible under the architecture criterion.
