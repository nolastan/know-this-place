Architect as surveyedJonathan Bulkley
Year built as surveyed1964

A house by local architect Jonathan Bulkley that originally showed several Third Bay Tradition features, including references to vernacular barn architecture and unfinished wood siding. It has since been remodelled with conventional windows, roof eaves and stucco siding, and the statement finds it no longer an eligible historic resource.
