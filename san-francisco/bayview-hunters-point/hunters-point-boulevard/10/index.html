India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Recorded as the yard of Skyscraper Crane and Rigging Co.: a large irregular parcel behind a tall steel fence, two-thirds of it asphalt, holding a gable-roofed concrete-block and corrugated-steel warehouse with a frame office wing facing Hunters Point Boulevard, and behind the warehouse a two-story frame dwelling of the 1950s that the survey thought may have been moved to the site, being older than the recorded construction date. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
