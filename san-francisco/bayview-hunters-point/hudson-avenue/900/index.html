India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Survey findingContributor to the India Basin Boat Yards, a district the survey put forward as eligible for the California Register under Criteria 1 and 3
Eligible districtIndia Basin Boat Yards (proposed; period of significance 1893–1935)
Parcel as surveyedBlock 4629A, Lot 010

A partly submerged parcel listed in a 1947 insurance appraisal as the site of Anderson & Cristofani's "West Repair Ways – Winch House, Storage & Boiler" and the "West Outfitting Dock and New repair ways." The West Repair Ways were demolished after 1950 and that part of India Cove filled; what remains is the West Outfitting Dock, one marine railway and one concrete boat ramp.
