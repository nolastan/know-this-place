Earlier record

  1. 1907–1914

    One of a row of three originally identical post-quake duplexes at 1773–75, 1779–81 and 1785 McKinnon Avenue, built when speculators put up larger multiple-family dwellings on the blocks closest to Third Street and Butchertown.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010

The 2010 survey's inventory table gives this parcel the address 1773–75 Newcomb Avenue; the same survey's text, EAS and the assessor all place it on McKinnon Avenue.
