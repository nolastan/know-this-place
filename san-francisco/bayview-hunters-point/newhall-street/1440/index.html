  1. 1886

    All Hallows Church built

    All Hallows Roman Catholic Church was built at Newhall Street and Palou Avenue for the neighbourhood's growing Irish Catholic population, who had until then worshipped in the chapel of the Mt. St. Joseph Orphan Asylum. It is the oldest Catholic church in the district.

    Kelley & VerPlanck Historical Resources Consulting, Bayview-Hunters Point Area B Survey: Town Center Activity Node — Historic Context Statement, prepared for the San Francisco Redevelopment Agency, adopted February 11, 2010
  2. November 25, 1888

    “The imposing Catholic Church”

    A Morning Call account of a stage trip south along Railroad Avenue picked out “the imposing Catholic Church” among the freshly painted cottages and fir groves of South San Francisco.

    Kelley & VerPlanck Historical Resources Consulting, Bayview-Hunters Point Area B Survey: Town Center Activity Node — Historic Context Statement, prepared for the San Francisco Redevelopment Agency, adopted February 11, 2010
