Earlier record

  1. 1899–1900

    The Sanborn maps of 1899–1900 show this house and 1510 Palou Avenue among the rural-type farmhouses standing on the larger garden lots of the old South San Francisco Homestead & Railroad Association tract, east of Railroad Avenue. With the Hittell House at 1547 Oakdale Avenue, it is one of the two dwellings in the Town Center that fit the Folk Victorian type most closely.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
