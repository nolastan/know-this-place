  1. 1906

    Built in 1906 in the Mediterranean Revival style, with arched window openings, multi-light windows, applied ornament, pent roof forms, a tower element, crenellations and Spanish clay tiles at the upper storey. The storefront was remodelled around the 1980s and keeps its recessed vestibule, bulkhead and historic transom.

    Neighborhood Commercial Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
