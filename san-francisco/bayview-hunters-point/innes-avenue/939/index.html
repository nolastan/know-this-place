India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

A one-story-over-basement stucco dwelling set near the middle of its lot behind a concrete stair and a one-story garage, its front terminating in an Art Moderne-inspired stepped parapet with stucco "speed lines." The report's property-type chapter names it as an example of the simplified Moderne five-room-over-garage dwelling built here after the Navy took over the Hunters Point shipyard in 1939. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
