Earlier record

  1. circa 1875

    The Dircks Residence was built — one of three structures surviving from the earliest era of wooden boat building at India Basin.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
  2. 1868

    Johnson J. Dircks, born in the Netherlands, was the first known boat builder to move to Hunters Point, establishing a yard at the corner of 5th (Evans) Avenue and L (Lane) Street. He moved all of his operations to a site next to William Stone's on 9th (Innes) Avenue in 1876.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
