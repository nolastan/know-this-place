The survey's record for 1064 Innes Avenue attributes five dwellings on the north side of the 1000 block — 1064, 1068, 1080, 1086 and 1092 — to the speculative builder George M. Moore, who bought the lots in March 1938 shortly after the block was graded from Hawes Street to Ingalls Street. This was the first of the five completed, on a 75-by-100-foot lot that also held the site of 1068 Innes Avenue. The survey names no architect.
