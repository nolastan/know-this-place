  1. 1937

    Built.

  2. May 1937

    Paul Runge, an engineer, built the one-and-a-half-story frame dwelling for himself and his wife at a cost of $3,000, on a 50-by-100-foot lot that also held the site of 993 Innes Avenue.

    India Basin Survey and Context Statement, adopted 2008
  3. April 1942

    The 50-by-100-foot lot was divided in two and 993 Innes Avenue sold off.

    India Basin Survey and Context Statement, adopted 2008
  4. July 1943

    A second unit was permitted in the lower part of the dwelling and let, with the upper unit, to war workers at the Hunters Point Naval Shipyard.

    India Basin Survey and Context Statement, adopted 2008
