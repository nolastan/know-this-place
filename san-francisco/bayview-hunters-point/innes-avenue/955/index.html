India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Parcel as surveyedBlock 3653, Lot 014

A two-story-over-garage single-family dwelling of 1956, three bays wide, with balconies on the upper floors. The form's identifier field prints Block 3653; its locational-data field and the assessor both give Block 4653. The survey also lists 955 Innes Avenue as a second address for Our Lady of Lourdes at 410 Hawes Street, which is a different parcel across the avenue. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
