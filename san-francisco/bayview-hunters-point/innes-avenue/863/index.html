India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Year built as surveyed1939
Parcel as surveyedBlock 4654, Lot 022

A one-story-over-garage stucco dwelling on a 31-foot lot, with a projecting gable-roofed garage, a shed-roofed porch and an Art Moderne stepped parapet. The city's address register still points this number at Lot 014B, a parcel the assessor has retired; the survey's Lot 022 is the parcel that carries the address now. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
