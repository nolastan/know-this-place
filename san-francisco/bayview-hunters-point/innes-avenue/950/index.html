India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Recorded as Surfside Liquors: a one-story-over-garage stucco building whose garage bay was infilled with a storefront, with a plaster cartouche above the window. One of two mixed commercial and residential buildings the survey counted in India Basin. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
