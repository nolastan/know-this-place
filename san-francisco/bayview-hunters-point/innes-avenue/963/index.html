India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Survey findingAppears ineligible for the California Register and the National Register — not closely connected to the families of the boat-building industry and not architecturally significant — but one of the oldest dwellings in the neighbourhood and warranting special consideration in local planning

StyleVernacular

Recorded as the Wilmer and Hilda Wilson Residence and built by day labour; the report's narrative instead calls it the Jorgensen cottage. A one-story hipped-roof cottage clad in California novelty siding, standing on a high scored concrete retaining wall with a stair up from the pavement. The survey names no architect.
