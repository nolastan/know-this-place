A two-story-over-garage stucco dwelling with a barrel-vaulted roof and a barrel-vaulted garage bay. The inventory form dates it to 2003 from Department of Building Inspection records; the report's narrative names 869 Innes Avenue as a 1938 survivor of the five-room-over-garage "junior five" built here for shipyard workers. The assessor's roll gives no year built at all. The city's address register still points this number at Lot 014A, a parcel the assessor has retired. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
