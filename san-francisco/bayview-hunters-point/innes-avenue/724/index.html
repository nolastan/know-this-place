India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Address as surveyed724 Innes Avenue (form address 724–732 Innes Avenue)
Parcel as surveyedBlock 4644, Lot 003B

A two-story wood-frame apartment building with a compound hip and gable roof, its front in face brick and vertical wood siding and its second floor overhanging the first. The west wall looks onto a small courtyard shared with the apartment house at 736–746 Innes Avenue. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
