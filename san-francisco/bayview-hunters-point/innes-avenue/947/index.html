India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

A one-story-over-garage stucco dwelling with a projecting garage bay, a tripartite window with an ornamental balcony, a recessed "tunnel" entrance and a false mansard parapet — the same plan as 943 Innes Avenue next door but one. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
