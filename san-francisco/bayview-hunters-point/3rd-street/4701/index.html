Earlier record

  1. 1966

    The Bayview Neighborhood Community Center moved in from its first home at 1201–05 Mendell Street.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
  2. 1954

    Gene K. Walker's Hunters Point Project Committee asked the San Francisco Housing Authority for $12,000 to buy the building and rehabilitate it as a community recreation centre. The bid failed, but it drew citywide attention to the conditions African American residents were living in on Hunters Point ridge.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
  3. Oct 24, 1888

    The Opera House opened to the public with a performance of John W. Sherman's Phantasma.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
  4. Oct 1884

    Local Masons built the South San Francisco Opera House as a community centre and lodge house.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010

The building is addressed 1601 Newcomb Avenue in its landmark designation and in every survey of it; the assessor and EAS carry the corner parcel as 4701–4705 Third Street. The assessor dates the building 1900; the 2010 survey dates it 1884. Most pre-1906 buildings in the district carry a default assessor date of 1900, because the city's building records were destroyed in the 1906 earthquake and fire.
