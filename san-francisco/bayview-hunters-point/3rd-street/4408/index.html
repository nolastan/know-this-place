DBI holds 30 permits for this parcel, most of them tenant improvements on individual floors. The timeline shows the 24 largest by stated cost, plus the earliest on file; the DBI query in the sources below returns all 30. 1 of them are nominal $1 street-space or sidewalk permits. 12 expired or cancelled permits are not shown. The 2010 survey gives conflicting accounts of this parcel. Its inventory table lists 4408–42 Third Street as an Italianate commercial block, and its narrative counts 4408–42 among the nineteenth-century mixed-use buildings shown on the 1899–1900 Sanborn maps, while a later passage describes 4408 Third Street as a Streamline Moderne building of 1946. The assessor dates the parcel 1913.
