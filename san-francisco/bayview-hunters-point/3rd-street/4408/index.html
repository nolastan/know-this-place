The 2010 survey gives conflicting accounts of this parcel. Its inventory table lists 4408–42 Third Street as an Italianate commercial block, and its narrative counts 4408–42 among the nineteenth-century mixed-use buildings shown on the 1899–1900 Sanborn maps, while a later passage describes 4408 Third Street as a Streamline Moderne building of 1946. The assessor dates the parcel 1913.
