  1. 1930

    Built as the Link Belt Co. plant: a Mediterranean-style masonry office building, a concrete pattern shop, and a steel-frame, steel-clad machine shop at the rear under a sawtooth roof. It stands in the former Western Development Co. tract, subdivided around 1925 for manufacturing.

    Kelley & VerPlanck Historical Resources Consulting, Bayview-Hunters Point Area B Survey: Town Center Activity Node — Historic Context Statement, prepared for the San Francisco Redevelopment Agency, adopted February 11, 2010
