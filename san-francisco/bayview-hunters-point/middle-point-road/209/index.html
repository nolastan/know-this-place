India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

A one-story-over-garage stucco dwelling with an arched entry porch, an extruded gable-roofed bay window and a false cross-gabled roof in red clay tile, built about 1940 on ground cut from the corner lot at 201 Middle Point Road. The report's property-type chapter names it as a surviving example of the five-room-over-garage plan built to Federal Housing Administration guidelines for shipyard workers. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
