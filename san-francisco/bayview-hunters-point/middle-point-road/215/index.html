India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

A one-story-over-garage stucco dwelling with a recessed "tunnel" entrance, a square bay window and a false cross-gabled roof, built about 1940 on ground cut from the corner lot at 201 Middle Point Road. Middle Point Road was Ingalls Street when the house went up. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
