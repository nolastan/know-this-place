India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Survey findingAppears ineligible for the National Register and the California Register under every criterion — built as rental housing, unconnected to the boat-building community, and of low integrity
Status code6Z
StyleUtilitarian

Built on a 75-by-100-foot lot at the corner of Innes Avenue and Ingalls Street, the street since renamed Middle Point Road, and facing the 1000 block of Innes while that block was still ungraded. About 1940 the lot was cut into three and 209 and 215 Middle Point Road built on the remainder. The survey names no architect.
