  1. 1977

    Dewey Crumpler painted The Fire Next Time on the exterior of the Joseph P. Lee Recreation Center here in 1977 and The Fire Next Time II on the adjacent gymnasium in 1984; the first was removed in a remodelling and a plaque commemorates it.

  7. 2011

    The Burnett Child Development Center here was renamed in 2011 for the first Black woman to serve as a principal in the city's schools, after a campaign to remove the name of California's first governor, who had sought to bar African Americans from settling in the state.
