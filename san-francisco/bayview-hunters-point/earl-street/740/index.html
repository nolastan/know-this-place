India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Address as surveyed5 Earl Street; 700 Innes Avenue
Parcel as surveyedBlock 4644, Lot 002

A two-story wood-frame mixed-use building, stucco below and rustic channel siding above, five bays along Earl Street, recorded in 2007 with its storefront and upper windows boarded up after a fire. One of two mixed commercial and residential buildings the survey counted in India Basin. The city carries neither number the survey used for it: the parcel is addressed 740 Earl Street today. The survey recorded every building in its area built in or before 1962 on a Primary Record, and evaluated the pre-1939 ones on Building, Structure and Object Records; a Primary Record carries no status code and no eligibility finding.
