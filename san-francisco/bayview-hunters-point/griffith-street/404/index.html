India Basin Survey (adopted 2008)

Survey findingContributor to the India Basin Boat Yards, a district the survey put forward as eligible for the California Register under Criteria 1 and 3
Eligible districtIndia Basin Boat Yards (proposed; period of significance 1893–1935)
Address as surveyed900A Innes Avenue
Parcel as surveyedBlock 4646, Lot 002

A 25-by-75-foot lot holding the two oldest purpose-built boat-yard buildings on the site. What reads as one board-and-batten building is two structures, built in the 1890s and standing on the 1899–1900 Sanborn map — a tool shed and engine house, and at its east end a water-tank house whose tank armature stood until 2005. East of them is a wood-frame ship's pilot house taken off a boat about 1930 and turned into the yard office. The district record dates the yard's period of significance from the building of this structure, which it addresses as 900A Innes Avenue.
