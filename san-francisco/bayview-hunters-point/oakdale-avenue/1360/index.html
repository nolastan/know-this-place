1360 Oakdale Avenue

Bayview Hunters Point · San Francisco, CA 94124

  • School building

104,000 sq ftLot area
  1. 1970

    George Washington Carver Elementary School, built as part of the New Hunters Point Community — a $33.9 million HUD-funded redevelopment that produced 2,000 homes, schools, churches, parks and a community centre.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
