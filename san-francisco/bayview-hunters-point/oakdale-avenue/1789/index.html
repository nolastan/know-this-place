  1. 1930

    The Maltese Club of San Francisco opened its headquarters here under the directorship of Francis Grech; the crowd at the opening was so large that people had to be turned away. The club kept a Maltese band and a theatrical group that performed in Maltese, and did philanthropic work. Its enrolled membership passed 750 by 1935. The building, heavily altered, is now Saint Paul Tabernacle Baptist Church.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
