  1. 1927

    Built as the L & E. Emanuel Inc. store fixture factory complex, a large three-storey concrete industrial building on an L plan. It stands in the Hudson Tract, farmland still cultivated in vegetable gardens on the 1914 Sanborn maps, subdivided for industry from about 1915 and built out in the 1930s.

    Area B Survey context statement, 2010
