Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

Architect as surveyedMichael Manwaring
Year built as surveyed1978

Graphic designer Michael Manwaring installed large concrete Helvetica letters here in 1978 as a sign for the India Basin Industrial Park, commissioned on a small budget by the Redevelopment Agency. The sign was removed for the construction of the new Southeast Community Center and is in storage.
