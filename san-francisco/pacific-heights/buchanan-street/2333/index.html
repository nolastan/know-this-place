CPMC Pacific Heights, designed by Stone, Marraccini & Patterson with Roselyn Lindheim - one of the medical complexes that make Brutalism most visible in the city's institutional buildings. An earlier competition-winning proposal for the Pacific Medical Center took a Progressive Architecture award in 1972; the building was constructed to a different design.
