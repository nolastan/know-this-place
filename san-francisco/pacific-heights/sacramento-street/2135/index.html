    The first of the large Pacific Heights apartment buildings that occupied its architect for the next twenty years — elegant, highly decorated Classical Revival with a slight French inflection.

    The Lafayette Apartments — five storeys over a basement, standing across from Lafayette Park, and one of only two large apartment buildings the city's citywide survey identified as predating the 1906 earthquake.
