  1. 1891

    Three flats in the Stick Eastlake manner stood here from late 1891 until the school next door expanded over the site — wood frame under a front-gabled roof behind a gabled parapet, with paired two-storey bay windows, a bracketed cornice and three doors in a recessed porch, designed by Michael J. Welsh, an architect credited with more than a hundred San Francisco buildings, who trained in the office of his brother Thomas J. Welsh.

    Drew School Addition, 2901 California Street/1831-1835 Broderick Street DEIR, SF Planning Dept., 2008
  2. 1911

    The Drew School, founded in 1908, moved to this corner of Broderick and California in 1911, a century before the building now on the parcel.

    Drew School Addition, 2901 California Street/1831-1835 Broderick Street DEIR, SF Planning Dept., 2008
