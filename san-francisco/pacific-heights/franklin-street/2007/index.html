Survey findingIndividually architecturally significant
StyleQueen Anne
Year built as surveyed1886

Judged individually significant as a detached single-family Queen Anne residence for its unusual massing — multiple cross gables, a round tower, cutaway and box-bay windows — and its range of ornament, including broken pediments, dentils, floral carving and turned porch posts.
