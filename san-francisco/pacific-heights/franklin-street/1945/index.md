# 1945 Franklin Street

A five-storey house of 1900 on Franklin Street, with fourteen rooms, an
internal theatre floor, and a permit record that runs to a $3.7 million
fire-damage repair completed in 2025.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 1 dwelling, 14 rooms, 7 baths, 5 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 6,957 sq ft; lot 3,781 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-2; Supervisorial District 2
- Historic status: CEQA Category A — a historical resource on the strength of
  a survey result. Not a designated landmark; in no historic district
- Several permits reference a "theatre floor" inside the house

## Notable residents

The prompting article associates a public figure with this address in terms
suggesting occasional, ongoing occupancy. Under this site's privacy rules,
claims phrased as current or continuing occupancy are omitted, so no name is
recorded here.

## Permit history

Forty-eight permits are on file, 1982–2024 — many administrative
final-inspection filings, street-space permits and nominal revisions. The
substantive record has two threads: a long remodel through the 2010s that
touched the theatre floor, the dormers and the baths, and a fire. In February
2021 a permit was filed to secure the back of the building and patch damage
caused by fire-department water; the full repair that followed, mostly at the
rear and including charred siding replaced over new sheathing, was valued at
$3.7 million and closed out in March 2025.

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **19 March 2019**. The 2025 roll
totals $3,300,000 but reports land and improvements at the identical figure of
$1,650,000 each — the total is usable, the split is not, and it predates the
completion of the fire repair.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder; when and how the fire started; what the theatre
floor is and when it was built; and whether the 2014–15 remodel permits, still
recorded as issued, were completed.
