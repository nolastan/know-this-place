# 2080 Washington Street

The Spreckels Mansion — a 34-room house of 1911 filling a 45,006-square-foot
Pacific Heights block, and a designated San Francisco landmark. The largest
building and the largest lot documented on this site.

## The building

- Built 1911 per the assessor; the prompting article gives 1912
- 1 dwelling, 34 rooms, 9 baths, 3 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 14,966 sq ft; lot 45,006 sq ft
- Class-B (non-combustible) construction — unlike the wood frame of nearly
  every other building documented here
- Zoning RH-1 / RH-2; Supervisorial District 2
- Recorded in the SF Planning survey under the name **Spreckels Residence**

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A / A* — a historical resource and an **Article 10 individual
  landmark**, the only individually landmarked building documented on this
  site
- The prompting article states this is San Francisco Landmark No. 197,
  designated 9 June 1990. The city's per-parcel dataset confirms the Article 10
  individual-landmark status but does not carry the landmark number or date, so
  those are the article's figures

## A current-resident claim, omitted

The article names a current resident of this house. This site does not name or
allude to current occupants — whether or not the information is published
elsewhere — so that claim is left out. Past, dated residency tied to a
published source could be documented here in future.

## Permit history

Eight permits are on file, 1990–2004; one is a sidewalk filing. The record
clusters in 1990–91: a $600,000 kitchen-and-two-bath remodel, the re-hanging of
the iron driveway gate, and — in the wake of the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake —
a proposal to brace the parapet with steel angles, which expired.

- **2001** — reroofing ($93,829), expired —
  [permit 200107053022](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200107053022)
- **1991** — brace and secure the parapet with a system of steel angles
  ($125,000), expired —
  [permit 9103665](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9103665)
- **1990** — re-install the iron gate at the front entrance driveway ($5,000) —
  [permit 9023908](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9023908)
- **1990** — remove non-structural partitions only ($2,000) —
  [permit 9022425](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9022425)
- **1990** — remodel the kitchen and two baths with miscellaneous electrical,
  plumbing and cabinetry ($600,000) —
  [permit 9022121](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9022121)

## Value

The 2025 roll assesses land at $3,607,497 and improvements at $8,274,212 —
$11,881,709 total, the highest total documented on this site. Improvements are
carried at more than twice the land value, the reverse of the usual Pacific
Heights split and a measure of the building itself. No sale appears on the
current roll.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder; confirmation of the landmark number and designation
date against the city's landmark ordinance; and the discrepancy between the
1911 and 1912 build dates.
