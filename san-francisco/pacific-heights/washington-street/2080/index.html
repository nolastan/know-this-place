Historic status

One published account gives San Francisco Landmark No. 197, designated 9 June 1990. The city's per-parcel historic dataset confirms the Article 10 individual-landmark status and the Category A / A* rating, but carries neither the landmark number nor the designation date, so those two figures remain unverified against the landmark ordinance itself.
