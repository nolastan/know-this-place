Victorian Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2022)

Survey findingIndividually architecturally significant
StyleRichardsonian Romanesque
Year built as surveyed1894–1896

A four-storey detached house and one of the few known Richardsonian Romanesque buildings in the city, judged individually significant for its multiple round towers, hipped roof with lower cross gables, classical columns and pilasters, and banded windows.
