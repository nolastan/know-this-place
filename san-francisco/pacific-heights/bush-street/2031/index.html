  1. 1924

    Built.

  2. 1926

    Purpose-built premises for the Kinmon Gakuen, or Golden Gate School, a Japanese language school that had first enrolled pupils in 1911 and taught ikebana, Japanese instruments and etiquette alongside the language.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  3. 1942

    One of the places in the city where San Francisco's Japanese community was ordered to register and report for processing in 1942 before wartime removal.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  4. 1942

    The Booker T. Washington Community Service Center leased this building in 1942 and stayed until 1952. It had housed the Kinmon Gakuen Japanese language school until the incarceration of the city's Japanese American population that year.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  5. 1945

    The Booker T. Washington Center moved into the building during the war and, with the American Friends Service Committee, ran a men's hostel here for those returning from the internment camps; the language school had reopened by 1949.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009

    The Redevelopment Agency photographed the property.

    Redevelopment Agency Records (SFH 371), San Francisco Public Library
