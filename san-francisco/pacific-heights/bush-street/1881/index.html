  1. 1895

    Congregation Ohabai Shalom, formed in 1862, built its temple here in 1895 to a design by the architect Moses J. Lyon, in an eclectic style drawing on Moorish and Eastern European precedent. It is one of only three 19th-century synagogues left standing in California.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  2. 1934

    Bought in 1934 by the Soto Zen congregation as the synagogue's membership dwindled. The building housed the Japanese Zen community for more than forty years, and Shunryu Suzuki taught zazen here to students from outside the community in the 1960s and 1970s; the congregation withdrew in the 1970s to build new quarters nearby.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  3. 1950s

    Sokoji Temple in Japantown, where poets and others were introduced to Zen Buddhism in the 1950s and 1960s; the San Francisco Zen Center grew out of that meeting.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  4. August 20, 1964

    Photographed, showing the Zen Soto Mission.

    San Francisco Subjects Photograph Collection (SFP 162), San Francisco Public Library
  5. July 1975

    Offered for sale by the Redevelopment Agency in July 1975 as the Soto Zen Mission, at a minimum bid of $39,600, with priority given to holders of Residential Certificates of Preference — property owners who had lost buildings to eminent domain.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009

The city's Japantown context statement gives 1881 Bush Street for the temple Congregation Ohabai Shalom built in 1895 and 1831 Bush Street for the same congregation's synagogue on the evidence of the 1893 and 1899 Sanborn maps. It does not reconcile the two numbers. Not yet documented: the developer, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale. The 1990 unreinforced-masonry survey dates the building to 1920; the assessor's roll gives 1895. Submit an update
