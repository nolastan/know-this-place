  1. 1865

    Henry Casebolt, a blacksmith who became a carriage and street car manufacturer and later controlled the Sutter Street Railroad, built this house and owned the whole block bounded by Pierce, Vallejo, Scott and Green Streets.

    Robert Bardell, “The Presidio & Ferries Railroad,” The Argonaut: Journal of the San Francisco Historical Society, vol. 29, no. 2 (Winter 2018), pp. 6–33
  2. March 11, 1868

    House nearly completed

    The Daily Alta California reported Henry Casebolt's house on Pierce Street nearly completed.

    Robert Bardell, “The Presidio & Ferries Railroad,” The Argonaut: Journal of the San Francisco Historical Society, vol. 29, no. 2 (Winter 2018), pp. 6–33
  3. 1874

    A house here served as the St. Alexander Nevsky Orthodox chapel from 1874 to 1881, between Vallejo Street and what later became Green Street.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
  4. October 1964

    The Junior League of San Francisco's Historic Sites Committee photographed the property.

    Junior League of San Francisco Here Today building research files (SFH 611), San Francisco Public Library
