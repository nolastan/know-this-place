San Francisco Pacific Heights

Pacific Heights

An affluent district on the ridge north of Japantown, known for large houses and mansions with views across the bay.

Streets documented so far

  • Steiner Street
    The house known as the exterior of the film Mrs. Doubtfire.
  • Franklin Street
    A five-storey 1900 house with an internal theatre floor and a 2025 fire-damage repair.
  • Washington Street
    The Spreckels Mansion, an Article 10 individual landmark.
  • Pierce Street
    The Casebolt House, an Article 10 individual landmark built for the blacksmith and street car manufacturer Henry Casebolt.
