1998 Broadway

Pacific Heights · San Francisco, CA 94123

  • 82-unit building
  • Zoned RM-2
  • District 2
  • Historic status: unevaluated

1998 Broadway is an 82-unit condominium tower built in 1964, its units held as 82 separate parcels — lots 038 through 119 on block 0567 — and numbered 402 through 1607 across twelve residential floors, with no thirteenth, above three levels of garage.

This page's parcel is lot 038, unit 402, so the assessed value and last sale shown below are that unit's and not the building's; a 2025 alteration permit describes the building as fifteen stories.
