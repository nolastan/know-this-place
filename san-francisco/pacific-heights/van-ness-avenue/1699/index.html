  2. 1919

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. Dated 1919 and 1923. Designed by Sylvain Schnaittacher, with a tall ground-floor arcade in an Italian Renaissance manner: the northern three bays in 1919, the southern two added in 1923. It was the showroom for the Paige, later Graham-Paige, motor car.
