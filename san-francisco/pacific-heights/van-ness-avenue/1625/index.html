  1. 1919

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. Dated 1919–1920. Faced with a brick veneer — the only showroom on the avenue so clad — with an ell running back to the side street for vehicle entry.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  2. 1920

    Several automobile dealers held the building in turn, each for a fairly short time, between 1920 and 1938.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
