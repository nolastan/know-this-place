  1. 1912

    An auto showroom designed by the O'Brien Brothers stood on the site, a Cadillac showroom 1913–1921 and then a Buick showroom 1921–1950 — the headquarters of the owner of the racehorse Seabiscuit. Lytle Buick, Berl Berry Ford and Harvey Motors followed. It was demolished in the 1980s.
