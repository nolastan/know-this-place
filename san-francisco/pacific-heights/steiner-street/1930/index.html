  1. 1932

    A Pacific Telephone & Telegraph building of 1932 in the Art Deco style, three storeys high — one of a series of nearly identical Art Deco utility buildings the company put up across San Francisco. A large modern addition has since been made to it.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
