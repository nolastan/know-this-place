# 2640 Steiner Street

A seven-room house of 1906 in Pacific Heights, known from published guides as
the exterior used in the 1993 film *Mrs. Doubtfire*.

## The building

- Built 1906 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1906)
- 1 unit, 7 rooms, 2 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 2,578 sq ft; lot 2,378 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-1; Supervisorial District 2
- Historic status: CEQA Category A — a historical resource on the strength of
  a survey result. Not a designated landmark; in no historic district

## As a filming location

A published guide to notable San Francisco addresses names this as the house
used in *Mrs. Doubtfire*, a film associated with Robin Williams. This is a
filming-location claim about the building's exterior, not a statement about who
has lived here.

## Permit history

Seventeen permits are on file, 1984–2021; six are street-space filings at a
nominal valuation. The record thins to almost nothing in the 1990s — three
permits filed between 1990 and 1998 all expired — and picks up again with three
substantial remodels in the 2000s and 2010s.

- **2021** — convert the basement beside the garage into a bedroom, bath,
  laundry and family room; lower the floor for ceiling height; replace an
  exterior door and three windows ($225,000) —
  [permit 202105240899](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202105240899)
- **2017** — partial second-floor remodel; add a half bath; replace the
  basement furnace and add an attic furnace; replace selected single-glazed
  windows in kind ($40,000) —
  [permit 201704285175](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201704285175)
- **2005** — kitchen remodel; add a half bath in the kitchen pantry
  ($100,000) —
  [permit 200502075012](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200502075012)
- **1998** — reroofing ($31,500), expired —
  [permit 9817226](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9817226)
- **1991** — repair a damaged wall at the front of the basement ($50,000),
  expired —
  [permit 9109700](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9109700)
- **1990** — window seat, kitchen window, counter tops, closets and a skylight
  ($10,000), expired —
  [permit 9017959](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9017959)
- **1984** — remove wood and concrete steps ($3,900) —
  [permit 8410355](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8410355)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **10 November 2016**. The 2025
roll assesses land at $3,371,498 and improvements at $1,693,972 — $5,065,470
total. Land is worth roughly twice the building, the usual Pacific Heights
pattern.
