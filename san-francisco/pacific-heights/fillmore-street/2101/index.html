  2. 1911

    The Preston Apartments, designed by the Reid Brothers, at the corner of California and Fillmore streets — one of the rare large apartment buildings of the Reconstruction era built outside the burned district, with a commercial ground floor beneath its flats.

  28. 2024

    Evaluated in the city's citywide survey of large apartment buildings as a good Renaissance Revival example keeping the fire escapes, central lightwell and rectilinear massing of its type, already identified as significant for its association with the Reid Brothers as Architects of Merit, and likely significant under Criterion A/1 for standing on Fillmore Street as that corridor became one of the city's primary business districts after 1906.

    Large Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
