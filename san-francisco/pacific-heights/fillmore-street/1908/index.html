  1. September 1956

    The San Francisco chapter of the National Council of Negro Women opened its headquarters here in September 1956 and ran its Citizenship Education Project from the building — a two-phase voter registration and education drive with a sample voting machine installed on the premises. Identified in 2024 as eligible for local landmark, California Register or National Register designation.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
