  1. before 1942

    An Art Deco commercial building that held the Yokohama Art Goods Store before the war and a dentist's offices in the 1950s.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  2. 1945

    Dr. Carlton B. Goodlett, the physician who bought and ran the Sun-Reporter and led the local NAACP, opened his medical practice here in 1945. Identified in 2024 as eligible for local landmark, California Register or National Register designation.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  3. 1970

    A restaurant occupied the ground floor here beneath a dental office on the upper storey, both run by the same Fillmore dentist. As of 2019 the building remained in the family and the restaurant space had not been remodelled.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
