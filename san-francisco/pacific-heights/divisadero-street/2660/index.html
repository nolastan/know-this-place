  1. 1938

    A house designed by John Ekin Dinwiddie, and the only residence he built in San Francisco. A blend of the International Style and the Second Bay Tradition.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  2. 1938

    The Roos House, 1938, and John Ekin Dinwiddie's only house in San Francisco: Cubist-influenced geometry, ribbon windows, a canted bay, and an unusual siding of wooden dowels that reads as corrugated metal.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
  3. 1965

    A residential garden laid out in 1965 by the landscape architect Thomas D. Church, who designed more than 150 gardens in San Francisco and is widely counted the founder of Modern California landscape design.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
