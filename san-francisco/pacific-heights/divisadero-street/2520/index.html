Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

Survey findingIndividually significant and district contributor
StyleTudor Revival
Year built as surveyed1934

A Tudor Revival residence of 1934 with front-facing gables; tall, narrow, multiple-pane windows; masonry accents; large chimneys; entries with Tudor arches; decorative half-timbering and stucco cladding. Its integrity and its full expression of the style would qualify it both individually and as a district contributor.
