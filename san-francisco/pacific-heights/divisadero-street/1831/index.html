  1. 1967

    The Third Hand Store opened in one of these storefronts in the summer of 1967, reportedly the first vintage clothing shop in San Francisco, stocked from the castoffs the era's scavengers turned up.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
