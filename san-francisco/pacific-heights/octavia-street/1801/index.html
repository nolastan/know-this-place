  1. 1939

    St Francis Xavier Mission, a Catholic Japanese American parish founded in a small Buchanan Street building in 1912, had moved to this corner by 1939, into a new building designed by the architect H.A. Minton to reflect the church's Eastern and Western connections.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
