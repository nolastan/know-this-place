  1. before 1942

    The First Filipino Christian Church, a small congregation, formed here next door to the Nichiren Buddhist church, and was active from before the war until the redevelopment years. The same structure stands with a remodelled facade.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009

    Bought in 1976 by the Japanese Community Youth Council from the San Francisco United Methodist Mission; a Filipino Methodist congregation had used it before that.
