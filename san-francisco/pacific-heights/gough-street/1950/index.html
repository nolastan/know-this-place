1950 Gough Street is a condominium building whose 35 apartments are held as 35 separate parcels — lots 065 through 099 on block 0624 — numbered 101 through 507, seven to a floor across five floors.

This page's parcel is lot 065, unit 101, so the assessed value and last sale shown below are that unit's and not the building's.
