Address as surveyed2799 Pacific Avenue and 2498 Divisadero Street

The Ellinwood Residence built in 1894 to designs by J. Eugene Freeman — an exuberant and unusual Colonial Revival house with an entry portico, symmetrical bays, oval windows and a hipped roof.
