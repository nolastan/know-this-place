  3. 1934–1936

    Émigré artist Victor Arnautoff painted three lunettes in the library reading room of what was then the California School of Fine Arts, later the San Francisco Art Institute.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
  4. 1950–1965

    The city's survey names the San Francisco School of Fine Arts, now the San Francisco Art Institute (San Francisco Landmark No. 85) at this address among the bars, cafes, bookshops and clubs of the North Beach Beat scene, whose period of significance it sets at about 1950 to 1965.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

StyleBrutalism
Architect as surveyedPaffard Keatinge-Clay
Year built as surveyed1966-1970

The San Francisco Art Institute addition by Paffard Keatinge-Clay, one of the best local examples of Brutalism. The building's local landmark designation does not explicitly recognise the addition, though the National Register nomination does. David Ireland's Smithsonian Falls, Descending a Staircase for P.K. was installed in its stairwell in 1987, and the founding members of the Organization of Women Architects were photographed on its roof in 1973.

The Modern design context statement dates the Art Institute addition to 1968-69 in one appendix and 1970 in another. Not yet documented: the developer, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale. Submit an update
