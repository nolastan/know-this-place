Year built as surveyed1967

The Cannery, a former warehouse and fruit cannery reworked by architect Joseph Esherick with landscape architect Thomas Church and graphic designer Marget Larsen - strategic incisions and insertions producing a complex, playful sequence of spaces with colourful wayfinding. Larsen's graphic star signs survive, repainted from their original green, pink and orange to white.
