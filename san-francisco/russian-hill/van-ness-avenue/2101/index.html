  1. 1965

    The Center for Special Problems opened here in 1965 under the San Francisco Department of Public Health, supporting transgender people with discussion groups, counselling and hormone prescriptions and issuing identity cards signed by a public health doctor that matched a person's gender identity; it reportedly hired the first openly gay city employee. Identified as potentially eligible for landmark designation.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
