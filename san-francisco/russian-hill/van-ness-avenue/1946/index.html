  1. 1920

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. Oakland and Duesenberg cars, among other makes, were sold here from 1920 to 1930. Two wooden vehicle doors survive on the Jackson Street side — among the only pre-war vehicle doors left in the corridor.
