StyleNew Formalism, Home Savings Style
Architect as surveyedMillard Sheets
Year built as surveyed1977

A Home Savings & Loan Association branch bank by Millard Sheets and his collaborative design studio, its mosaic mural using local motifs including the skyline and the Golden Gate Bridge. The statement finds the monumental massing, stone cladding, gold trim and mosaic art make it eligible under Criterion C/3 as an example of the Home Savings Style and of Sheets's work.
