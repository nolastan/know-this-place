San Francisco Russian Hill

Russian Hill

A steep district above North Beach and the waterfront, known for its cable-car grades and dense mix of flats and single houses.

Streets documented so far

  • Taylor Street
    Three flats of 1907 recorded as where Joe DiMaggio grew up.
  • Francisco Street
    A 1914 corner house beside the Powell–Hyde cable car line; tied to Nicolas Cage.
