  1. 1930

    Built as a commercial building for Bimbo’s 365 Club, to the design of Miller and Pflueger, one of the modern architects the city's survey identifies as working in North Beach.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1931

    Built.

  3. 1931

    Bimbo's 365 Club traces its business here to 1931; the city's survey lists it among North Beach's legacy bars, restaurants and businesses, a concentration it records as the highest in the city.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  4. 1940s

    The Art Deco Bal Tabarin nightclub, designed by Timothy Pflueger, drew wartime crowds here; the building is now Bimbo's 365 Club.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  5. September 5, 1962

    The Office of the Assessor-Recorder photographed the property for tax assessment. Bimbo's was at the address.

    Assessor-Recorder Photographs (SFP 23), San Francisco Public Library
