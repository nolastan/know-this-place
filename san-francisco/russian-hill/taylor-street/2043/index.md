# 2043–2047 Taylor Street

Three flats over three storeys of 1907, on a 1,907-square-foot lot on the north
slope of Russian Hill. One parcel carrying both street numbers.

## The building

- Built 1907 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1907)
- 3 units, 19 rooms, 3 baths, 3 stories; assessor class "Flats & Duplex"
- Building area 4,608 sq ft; lot 1,907 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RM-1; Supervisorial District 3
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district
- The parcel spans 2043 to 2047 Taylor Street; the article that prompted this
  page gives the address as 2047

## Neighbourhood

The article places this address in North Beach. Both the assessor and the
city's analysis neighbourhood layer put it in Russian Hill, and this site
follows the city data.

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records this as where
Joe DiMaggio grew up. No dates are given and no underlying source is cited. The
same guide gives [2150 Beach Street](../../../marina/beach-street/2150/) as his
address during his marriage to Marilyn Monroe.

## Permit history

Four permits are on file, 2005–2019, and the only substantial one is the
garage.

- **2019** — revision to the garage permit: driveway grading for a minor
  sidewalk encroachment —
  [permit 201903054411](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201903054411)
- **2019** — widen the garage door, open the garage space, enclose the garage
  light wells, and provide a new rated corridor access way ($89,000 estimated,
  revised down to $50,000) —
  [permit 201901140291](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201901140291)
- **2010** — reroofing ($6,500) —
  [permit 201006255301](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201006255301)
- **2005** — repair the back stair in kind, under 50 percent ($1,000) —
  [permit 200504210600](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200504210600)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **10 September 2018**; the garage
work followed four months later. The 2025 roll assesses land at $1,394,398 and
improvements at $1,282,508 — $2,676,906 total.
