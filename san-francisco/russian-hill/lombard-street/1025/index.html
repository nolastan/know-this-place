1025 Lombard Street

Russian Hill · San Francisco, CA 94109

  • Built 1951
  • 6-unit building
  • Zoned RH-3
  • District 3

1025 Lombard Street is a building divided into six condominium units, each its own parcel — lots 033 through 038 on block 0071 — numbered 1 through 6, and running between 950 and 1,150 square feet apiece.

The assessor records one story against every one of the six, a unit-level figure rather than the building's, and the unit numbers carry no floor; no story count for the building is stated here.

This page's parcel is lot 036, unit 4, so the assessed value and last sale shown below are that unit's and not the building's.
