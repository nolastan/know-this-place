  1. 1877

    A one-story Italianate house at the rear of the lot, little changed apart from the shingles later added to its front. Its cornice is molded without brackets, and fluted triglyphs sit between the panels of the frieze just below it.

    The West Slope of Russian Hill: A Historical Context and Inventory of Historic Resources, 2006, revised 2009
  2. 1908

    Shingled flats by the architects Wright, Rushforth and Cahill, built by the contractor N. A. Trubeck at the front of a lot that already held an older cottage.

    The West Slope of Russian Hill: A Historical Context and Inventory of Historic Resources, 2006, revised 2009
