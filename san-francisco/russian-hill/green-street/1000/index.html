  1. 1951

    Designed by Oleg N. Ivanitsky and R. G. Watanobe. Advertisements and articles of the day called it the first high-rise apartment building of its type erected in San Francisco since the 1930s, and the city's citywide survey treats it as the start of the post-war wave of tall apartment construction on Russian Hill and Nob Hill.
