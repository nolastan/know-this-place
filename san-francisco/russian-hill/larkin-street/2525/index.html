  1. 1877

    An Italianate house stood on this site from 1877, one of a row of four along the block, with pediments over its paired windows and door. It was demolished in the 1920s to make way for the apartment building here now.

    The West Slope of Russian Hill: A Historical Context and Inventory of Historic Resources, 2006, revised 2009
  2. 1878

    A one-story-plus-raised-basement Italianate house stood on this site from 1878. It was substantially altered in 1894 and again in 1917, ending as a two-story house with classical trim, and came down in 1927.

    The West Slope of Russian Hill: A Historical Context and Inventory of Historic Resources, 2006, revised 2009

    Built.

  4. 1927

    A mid-rise reinforced-concrete apartment building, one of the three tallest on these blocks and the work of a partnership that specialised in the type. Its ornament survives intact apart from window alterations.

    The West Slope of Russian Hill: A Historical Context and Inventory of Historic Resources, 2006, revised 2009
