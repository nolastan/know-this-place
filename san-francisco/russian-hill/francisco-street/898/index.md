# 898 Francisco Street

A thirteen-room house of 1914 on the corner of Francisco and Hyde, on the
Powell–Hyde cable car line. The most valuable parcel documented on this site,
and the only one whose historic status comes from a project-driven evaluation.

## The building

- Built 1914 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1914)
- 1 unit, 13 rooms, 6 baths, 3 stories per the assessor; a 2014 permit
  describes a four-storey house
- Building area 6,305 sq ft; lot 5,832 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-1(D); Supervisorial District 3
- A corner parcel: a 2001 permit to rebuild a storm-destroyed fence names the
  Hyde Street side of Francisco Street

## Historic status

CEQA Category A — a historical resource. Uniquely among the addresses
documented here, the determination comes from a **Historic Resource Evaluation
Response**, which Planning issues in response to a specific project, rather
than from a survey sweep or district membership. It is not a designated
landmark and lies in no historic district.

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Nicolas Cage as a
resident, and notes the corner position and the cable car line. No dates are
given and no underlying source is cited.

## Permit history

Sixteen permits are on file, 1989–2022. The 1989 cluster is five small
bathroom and kitchen jobs, most of which expired and were renewed together in
1996. The outlier is 2014: a $950,000 permit — revised to $1,500,000 — to
expand the two-car garage and add to the rear. It remains issued, not
completed.

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **23 December 2021**. The 2025
roll assesses land at $5,619,096 and improvements at $3,746,064 — $9,365,160
total, the highest of any address documented here.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder; whether the house has three storeys or four, as the
assessor and a 2014 permit disagree; whether the 2014 garage and rear addition
were built; and any dates for the reported residency.
