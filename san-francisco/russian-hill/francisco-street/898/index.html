A corner parcel at Francisco and Hyde. A 2001 permit to rebuild a storm-destroyed fence names the Hyde Street side, confirming the corner frontage.

How Category A was reached

This parcel's Category A status comes from a Historic Resource Evaluation Response — a determination Planning issues in response to a specific project — rather than from a survey sweep or district membership. It is not a designated landmark and lies in no historic district.
