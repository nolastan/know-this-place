# Golden Gate Park

The park between Stanyan Street and the Great Highway, filed as its own
neighborhood because the city's analysis neighborhoods treat it as one. Its
places are the ones the Recreation and Park Department's facility inventory
names inside the park — gardens, lakes, museums, windmills, stadiums — each
with a page of its own; the land under them is one parcel, whose assessor and
permit record is its page on Fulton Street.

## Streets documented so far

- [Fulton Street](fulton-street/) — 1 building; 1 in the Golden Gate Park Historic District.
