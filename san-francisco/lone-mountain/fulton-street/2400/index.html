San Francisco Lone Mountain / USF Fulton Street 2400

2400 Fulton Street

Lone Mountain / USF · San Francisco, CA 94118

  • Built 1904
  • Single-family, 19 rooms
  • 3 stories
  • Zoned RM-1
  • Historic resource — CEQA A

Facing Golden Gate Park across Fulton Street. Jefferson Airplane and Grace Slick bought the house in 1968 for around $70,000 and used it as home and workplace for about ten years.
