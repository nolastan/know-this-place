  1. 1912-1914

    The State Armory and Arsenal was built of clinker brick in rough red and brown, its walls curving outward towards the ground, with long slit windows and four octagonal corner towers, in the manner of a mediaeval fortress.

    San Francisco Planning Department, Valencia Gardens Hope VI Public Housing Project: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 2002.0809E, 2003.
  2. 1912-1914

    Built by the contractors McLeran & Peterson to the design of the architects Woollett & Woollett, who had presented the plans in December 1911.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
  3. 1914

    Built.

  4. 1978

    The armory was listed on the National Register of Historic Places as an outstanding example of the metropolitan armories built in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. It is San Francisco Landmark No. 108.

    San Francisco Planning Department, Valencia Gardens Hope VI Public Housing Project: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 2002.0809E, 2003.
