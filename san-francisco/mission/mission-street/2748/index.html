    Built in 1920 and remodelled twice: the left-hand storefront about the 1930s as an exceptional piece of Streamline Moderne, with large curved-glass show windows and a deeply recessed vestibule, and the right-hand one about the 1950s in the Midcentury Modern manner, with an angled vestibule and canted display windows.
