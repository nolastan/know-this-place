  1. 1937

    Remodelled over 1937 and 1938 into the Streamline Moderne: the corners rounded off, continuous windows run around them, the rustic wood cladding covered over with smooth enamel iron panels, speed lines laid in near the roofline, and the entrance rebuilt with a recessed vestibule, terrazzo paving and a marquee tower.

Modernistic Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

StyleStreamline Moderne
Year built as surveyed1937

A rectangular-plan commercial building completely remodelled in 1937-38 in the Streamline Moderne idiom: the corners rounded, continuous corner windows installed, rustic wood cladding covered with smooth enamel iron panels, speed lines applied near the roofline, and the entrance rebuilt with a recessed vestibule, terrazzo paving and a marquee tower.
