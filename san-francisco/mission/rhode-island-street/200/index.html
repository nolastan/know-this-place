Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

Survey findingEligible as a district contributor
StyleEarly 20th Century American Commercial
Year built as surveyed1912

The J.I. Case Threshing Co. building of 1912, designed by G. Albert Lansburgh. Built to fit a triangular lot, its massing is slightly irregular, but it keeps brick cladding, a tripartite facade and a flat roof, and would be eligible as a district contributor.
