Inner Mission North Context Statement, adopted 2004

DistrictRamona Street
Survey findingContributor

Contributor to the Ramona Street National Register eligible district (29 Ramona Street). Criterion C: a 1911–1923 middle-class subdivision of 36 properties, almost all built with an integrated ground-floor garage (“auto basement”) — the survey calls this among the earliest known examples of the automobile built into residential design on a national scale, roughly a decade before the practice became common. Integrity is high.
