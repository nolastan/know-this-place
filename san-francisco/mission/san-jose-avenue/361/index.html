Early Settlement Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2025)

StyleFolk Victorian
Year built as surveyedc. 1865

A one-and-a-half-storey single-family wood-framed house named as one of the few intact Folk Victorian examples left in San Francisco. Its full-width front entry porch with sawn decorative brackets is what marks the style, and it could be found eligible under Criterion C/3.
