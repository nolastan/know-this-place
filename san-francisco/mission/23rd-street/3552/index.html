  2. before 1906

    A pre-1906 neighborhood commercial building keeping segmented display windows — small panes divided by wood muntins, the storefront glazing that plate glass displaced in the 1880s and 1890s. The city's preservation planners put it forward for landmark designation as one of the rare intact examples of its kind.
