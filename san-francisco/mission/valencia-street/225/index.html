  1. 1969

    The American Indian Center took the building, and held it until 1989 — a rare space given over to the cultural heritage and wellbeing of the city's American Indian community, and a resource for the surrounding Mission District.

    Citywide Historic Context Statement: Clubs and Social Halls (1848–1989), 2026
  2. 1980s

    The American Indian Center, which hosted the roving lesbian-of-colour dance party Colors and benefit dances for the Gay Latino Alliance, and out of whose art workshop Gay American Indians was founded in 1975.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
