Year built as surveyed1995

Bethel Temple, designed by Mark Lechowski of Allen-Drever-Lechowski for a Swedish American congregation. Its highly expressed steel frame is a Late Modernist trope, but the double-coding of the mountain-range roof puts it in the Postmodern tradition. The church was later renamed Bethel Christian Church.
