  1. 1912

    The S. C. Johnson floor wax building, cited with 1563 Mission Street as one of the more substantial industrial buildings of the decade after 1909.

    Market & Octavia Historic Context Statement, adopted 2007
  2. 1912

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. A showroom for the Jeffrey Auto Sales Company, makers of the Rambler, for a few years from its completion.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
