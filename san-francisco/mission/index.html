Mission

A large, dense district around the old Mission Dolores, mixing Victorian and Edwardian flats with a strong Latino community and, on Liberty Hill, a protected historic district.

Adjacent neighborhoods

The Castro adjoins west across Dolores and Church Streets, Noe Valley south-west over the hill, and Bernal Heights south beyond Cesar Chavez Street. Potrero Hill is east across Potrero Avenue, South of Market north-east past Division Street, and Hayes Valley north-west across Market Street.
