Inner Mission North Context Statement, adopted 2004

DistrictHidalgo Terrace
Survey findingContributor

Contributor to the Hidalgo Terrace California Register eligible district (15 Hidalgo Terrace). Criteria 1 and 3: a small cul-de-sac developed 1919–1925 on land deeded to the city in 1916 by Adrienne and Nellie Thompson, its stucco-clad row houses gated by two matched three-story apartment buildings at Dolores Street. Integrity is high.
