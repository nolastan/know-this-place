  1. 1918

    A stone meditation grotto dedicated to Our Lady of Lourdes was built here by the Sisters of Mercy, on the grounds of St. Catherine's Home and Training School. It is the last of the many such grottoes the order built in San Francisco.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, San Francisco General Hospital Mental Health Skilled Nursing Facility: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, 1990.
