Early Settlement Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2025)

StyleGreek Revival
Year built as surveyedc. 1854

A Tanforan Cottage, San Francisco Landmark No. 67 — one of the two best-known Greek Revival residences in the city. Altered, but still showing the style in its flattened window crowns, substantial window frames, squared porch columns, Chippendale balustrades, keystone pediment and low-pitch roof.
