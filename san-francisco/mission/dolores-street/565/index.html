Mission Dolores Neighborhood Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Address as surveyed569 Dolores Street

The anarchist Alexander Berkman lived here for about a year until 1916, publishing the monthly magazine The Blast; he was among those suspected, though never arrested, after the Preparedness Day bombing of July 22, 1916.
