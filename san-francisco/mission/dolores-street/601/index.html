Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Address as surveyed601–605 Dolores Street

Listed as the Norwegian Lutheran Church, one of the Scandinavian and Finnish institutions of the Upper Market area that the statement inventories. The congregation stayed in the South of Market area until the 1930s, when it moved here. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
