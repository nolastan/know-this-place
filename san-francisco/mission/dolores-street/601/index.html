    Mission Congregational Church

    Listed as 3689 19th Street in the Junior League of San Francisco's Here Today survey of 1968. The red brick Gothic Revival church was designed by the Reverend Francis W. Reid and completed in 1910 for the Mission Congregational Church, facing Mission Dolores Park across from the rebuilt Youths' Directory, and was later known as the Norwegian Lutheran Church and then the Golden Gate Lutheran Church. A second passage gives it as 1910 and 1931, both by Reid.

    Mission Dolores Neighborhood Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

The congregation stayed in the South of Market area until the 1930s, when it moved here.
