Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StyleNew Modernism
Architect as surveyedPeter Pfau Architects
Year built as surveyed1999

Green Glen, a 1947 industrial building converted for office use by Peter Pfau Architects, named after the linen service that once operated here. The intervention sets a new Modernist approach against an existing, modest Modernist industrial design.
