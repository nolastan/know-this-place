  1. 1887

    The Sanborn map shows this block cut through by Juniper Street and built up with dwellings of various sizes, stables, a coal and wood yard and the Milwaukee Brewery.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Costco Wholesale: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 90.070E, 1991.
  2. 1929

    The Sanborn map of 1913, updated to 1929, shows the block after the fire: the Milwaukee Brewery rebuilt on part of it, and around it dwellings, storage and offices, a motor garage and assorted industry.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Costco Wholesale: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 90.070E, 1991.
  3. 1991

    None of those buildings were left. The block was a surface parking lot and a police pound for impounded vehicles.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Costco Wholesale: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 90.070E, 1991.
