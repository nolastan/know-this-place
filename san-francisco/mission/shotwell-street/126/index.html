Contributor to the South Van Ness Avenue–Shotwell–Folsom Streets (SVN-S-F) California Register eligible district (126-128 Shotwell St). Criterion A: an isolated pocket of pre-1906 buildings left unharmed by the earthquake and fire, near a larger surviving group at 573 South Van Ness Avenue. Integrity is moderate; the survey says the district is not likely to qualify for the National Register.
