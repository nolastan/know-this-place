Inner Mission North Context Statement, adopted 2004

DistrictGuerrero Street Fire Line
Survey findingNon-contributor

Non-contributor to the Guerrero Street Fire Line National Register eligible district (222-224 Clinton Park). Criteria A and C: one of the few groups of buildings in the survey area that are front-line survivors of the 1906 earthquake and fire, embodying balloon-frame and early platform-frame construction with high-artistic-value ornamentation. Integrity is high; two buildings are non-contributors.
