Address as surveyed2731-2735 Folsom Street

The Gaughran House designed by Albert Pissis — a residential Beaux Arts building with intricately moulded balconies, a rusticated ground floor, a tripartite composition and arched openings, judged individually significant. The document's year for this building is 1900, which is what the assessor's roll gives the parcel, so it is not an independent date.
