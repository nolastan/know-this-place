  7. 2010

    The ARC Studios and Gallery, headquarters of the Kearny Street Workshop since 2010. Founded in 1972 in the basement of the International Hotel, the workshop is the oldest Asian Pacific American multidisciplinary arts organisation in the country.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
