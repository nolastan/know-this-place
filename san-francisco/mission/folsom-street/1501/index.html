  1. 1929

  2. 1966

    FeBe's leather bar opened here in 1966 and made Folsom Street the anchor of the city's leather scene; it displayed a sculpture by artist Mike Caffee recasting Michelangelo's David in motorcycle garb, and by the late 1970s hosted the Mr. SF Leather contest that became the International Mr. Leather competition.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  3. 1966

    A Taste of Leather, a leather shop, occupied this address from 1966 to 1978. This was the shop's first location, upstairs from the bar.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
