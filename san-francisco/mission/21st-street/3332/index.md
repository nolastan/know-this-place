# 3332–3334 21st Street

A two-flat building of about 1900 on Liberty Hill, inside the Liberty Hill
Historic District. Published guides record it as Mel Blanc's childhood home.
One parcel carrying both street numbers.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 2 flats, 14 rooms, 3 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Flats & Duplex"
- Building area 4,344 sq ft; lot 2,875 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 9
- The parcel spans 3332 and 3334 21st Street

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A / A* — a historical resource, and within an **Article 10
  historic district**
- Liberty Hill Historic District, period of significance **1860s–1906**;
  California Register eligible, and Article 10 **listed**
- Because the district is Article 10, this parcel carries local landmark
  protection

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records this as Mel
Blanc's childhood home, giving his birth on 30 May 1908 and a move away when he
was about five. The guide notes the house still stands as a private residence.
No underlying source is cited.

## Permit history

Seven permits are on file, all clustered in 1987–1994; there is no recorded
work since. The 1987–89 run reads as one project: reinforce the east-side
foundation and replace the kitchen, remodel a family room, and remove a column
with a new beam — with a larger family-room construction permit withdrawn along
the way.

- **1994** — restore accessory use of basement habitable space to the
  dwelling ($1,999 revised) —
  [permit 9401032](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9401032)
- **1993** — reroofing ($4,000) and termite repair ($3,620) —
  [9317658](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9317658),
  [9314138](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9314138)
- **1988** — add a beam to remove a column in the rec room ($1,000) —
  [permit 8817180](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8817180)
- **1988** — remodel the family room ($12,000); a companion permit to build a
  new family room ($25,000) was withdrawn —
  [8805837](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8805837),
  [8806911](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8806911)
- **1987** — reinforce and augment the east-side foundations; replace the
  kitchen ($30,000) —
  [permit 8717518](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8717518)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **30 June 1995**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $285,532 and improvements at $403,118 — $688,650 total.
Improvements are carried above land, unusual for San Francisco and a product of
the long-held assessment base.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, when the building was divided
into two flats, and any primary-source confirmation of the Blanc residency.
