California Historical Resource Status Codes: a 1 or 2 means listed or formally determined eligible, a 3 means the survey judged it eligible, a 5 means locally significant, a 6 that it was found ineligible, and a 7 that it was not evaluated. The survey rates the architecture 3 of 5. The 2009 context statement gives the status code 3CD; the 2011 survey data table gives 6L.

The survey's data table dates the building to 1918; the context statement's appendix dates it to Ca. 1925. Not yet documented: the year the building went up, the architect and builder, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale. Submit an update
