  1. 1893

    Built by the Market Street Railway as a steam powerhouse, heavy timber framed and brick, generating electricity first from coal and later from oil. The oldest surviving non-residential building in the Showplace Square survey area.

    Showplace Square / Northeast Mission Historic Resource Survey, adopted 2011
  2. 1895

    Enlarged.

    Showplace Square / Northeast Mission Historic Resource Survey, adopted 2011
  3. 1907

    During the 1907 streetcar strike, one of the earliest outbreaks of violence in the district took place outside the powerhouse doors, when armed strikebreakers fired on strikers.

    Showplace Square / Northeast Mission Historic Resource Survey, adopted 2011
  4. Sep 2003

    This 1 story masonry building will be seismically reinforced & plumbing, electrical brought up to code.

California Historical Resource Status Codes: a 1 or 2 means listed or formally determined eligible, a 3 means the survey judged it eligible, a 5 means locally significant, a 6 that it was found ineligible, and a 7 that it was not evaluated. The survey rates the architecture 5 of 5. The 2009 context statement gives the status code 3CB; the 2011 survey data table gives 3S, 3CB. California Register criteria cited: 1, 3.
