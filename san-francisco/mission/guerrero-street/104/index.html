Inner Mission North Context Statement, adopted 2004

DistrictGuerrero Street Fire Line; Inner Mission Commercial Corridor
Survey findingContributor

Contributor to the Guerrero Street Fire Line National Register eligible district (104-114 Guerrero). Criteria A and C: one of the few groups of buildings in the survey area that are front-line survivors of the 1906 earthquake and fire, embodying balloon-frame and early platform-frame construction with high-artistic-value ornamentation. Integrity is high; two buildings are non-contributors. Contributor to the Inner Mission Commercial Corridor locally significant area (104-114 Guerrero). Formerly called the 16th Street Commercial Corridor. Criterion C: San Francisco's largest concentration of residential-over-commercial and small-scale commercial buildings, spanning both pre-1906 and post-disaster construction, dated c. 1870s–1931.
