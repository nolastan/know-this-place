  2. 1952

    The 585 Club, a bar from 1952 until 1961; its owner's decision in 1960 to refuse police payoffs and to convene other bar owners here led to the extortion prosecutions known as the Gayola Scandal, the first time in American history that bar owners accused police of extortion in this way.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
