Architect as surveyedMario Gaidano, architect; Byron Niskian, engineer
Year built as surveyed1969

The Donatello Hotel by architect Mario Gaidano with engineer Byron Niskian: ornamental concrete panels, emphasised verticality and a projecting flat roof, with travertine planters at the base. The statement reads it as Late Modernist with New Formalist influences.
