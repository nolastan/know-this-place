  1. 1966

    Gene Compton's Cafeteria, part of a local chain, occupied this corner of Turk and Taylor; in August 1966 transgender women and young men fought back against police here over several nights, an act of resistance that preceded the Stonewall riots by three years and led to lasting institutional change in the city.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  2. 1980

    The Ram's Head, one of a circuit of Tenderloin bars frequented by members of Gay American Indians.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
