    Built as the Beverly Hotel, stores and rooming house with 37 rooms and 25 baths, engineered by A. Knelling. Two storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, stucco facade, scored wall, cornice, two-part commercial composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.
