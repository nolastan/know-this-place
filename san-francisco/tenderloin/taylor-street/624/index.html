  1. 1872

    The Bohemian Club, founded in 1872 by journalists, artists and musicians, settled at this address after earlier clubhouses on Sacramento and Pine Streets.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  2. 1909

    The Bohemian Club, designed by architect Loring P. Rixford, his best-known San Francisco work.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  3. 1912

    The Bohemian Club built its first purpose-designed clubhouse here, in the Italian Renaissance Revival style, after the 1906 earthquake. It was demolished in 1933.

    Citywide Historic Context Statement: Clubs and Social Halls (1848–1989), 2026
  4. 1934

    The club replaced its 1912 clubhouse on the same site, in a transitional Art Deco and Art Moderne style. Repeating owl heads and relief sculptures of men at the dramatic and artistic arts run across the street facades.

    Citywide Historic Context Statement: Clubs and Social Halls (1848–1989), 2026
