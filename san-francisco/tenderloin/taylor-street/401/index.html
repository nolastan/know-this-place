  1. 1923

    Built as the Hotel Californian, mid-priced hotel with 250 rooms with baths, to the design of the architect Edward E. Young. Sixteen storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, decorative panels, beltcourses, balconies, parapet, three-part vertical composition with extension, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation with Spanish Colonial revival extension.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
