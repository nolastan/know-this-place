  1. 1923

    Built as the Chateau Marian Apartments, stores and apartment building with 70 2- room units, to the design of the architect John C. Hladik. Seven storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
