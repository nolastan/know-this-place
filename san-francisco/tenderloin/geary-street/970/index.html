  1. 1922

    Built as the Gray Moor Apartments, apartment building with 39 2-room units, to the design of the architects Griewank and Buckley. Five storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
