  1. 1923

    Built as apartment building with twenty 2- and 3-room units, to the design of the architect Samuel C. Heiman. Five storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
