  1. 1911

    Built as store and rooming house with 19 rooms and 6 baths, built by the contractors Ruegg Brothers. Three storeys over a basement, brick structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
