    The New Orleans trumpeter Bunk Johnson performed in the basement of the CIO Building here from July 1943 to July 1944, at the invitation of the labour leader Harry Bridges; the room was renamed the HJS Chamber for the Hot Jazz Society. The building is gone.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
