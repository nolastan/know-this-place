  1. 1924

    Built as the Sovereign Apartments, apartment building with 33 2- and 3-room units, to the design of the architects Baumann and Jose. Five storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
