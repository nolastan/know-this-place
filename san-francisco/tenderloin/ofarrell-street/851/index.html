  1. 1908

    Built as the Blanco's Hotel and Restaurant, mid-priced hotel with 46 suites each with bath converted to apartments with 27 2-room units, to the design of the architects Righetti and Kuhl. Four storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
