  1. 1910

    Built as the Hotel Eureka, store and apartment house with 32 one-and 2-room units, to the design of the architect C.A. Meussdorffer. Six storeys over a basement, brick structure, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
