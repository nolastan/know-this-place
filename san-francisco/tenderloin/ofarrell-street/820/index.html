  1. 1915

    Built as apartment building with 54 2-room units, to the design of William Wilde. Four storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, stucco facade, 3-story galvanized iron bay windows and cornice, rusticated base, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
