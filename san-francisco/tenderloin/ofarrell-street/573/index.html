  1. 1927

    Built as the El Capitan Apartments, stores and apartment building with 49 2-room units, to the design of the architect H.W. Bott. Twelve storeys over a basement, steel frame and reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Gothic ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
