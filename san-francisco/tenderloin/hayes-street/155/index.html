  1. 1925

    The Automobile Association of California's Beaux Arts office building at 150 Van Ness Avenue, remodelled in 1959 and linked by skybridge to the association's new nine-storey building on Hayes Street.

    Market & Octavia Historic Context Statement, adopted 2007
  2. 1925

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as the California State Automobile Association; altered in 1969. Designed by George Kelham.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  3. 1959

    Built as a nine-storey office building for the Automobile Association of California, linked by skybridge to the association's remodelled 1925 building at 150 Van Ness Avenue.

    Market & Octavia Historic Context Statement, adopted 2007
  4. 1959

    The Automobile Association Inter-Insurance Bureau building, 1959, by Albert F. Roller. A 1968 California State Automobile Association office on the street shows the projecting mullions that divide a Midcentury Modern facade's massing.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
  5. 2010

    Identified in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor but not formally evaluated on a state historic resource form.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
