  1. 1916

    Built as the Cameo Apartments, apartment building with 17 2-room units, to the design of the architects Rousseau and Rousseau. Five storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
  2. 1922

    Built as apartment building with 132- and 3-room units, built by the contractor J. Greenbach. Three storeys, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Gothic ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
