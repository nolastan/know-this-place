  1. 1914

    Built as the Hotel Clark, a rooming house with 153 rooms and 127 baths, to the design of the architect Henry H. Meyers. Brick structure, two-part vertical composition.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
  2. 1950

    The exterior was remodelled in the Moderne style, with stylized fluted pilasters and a cove cornice.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
