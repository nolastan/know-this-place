  1. 1926

    Built as the Eddy Arms Apartments, apartment building with 41 2- and 3-room units, engineered by William Helbing Company. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure with stucco facade, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.
