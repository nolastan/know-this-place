  1. 1906

    Built as rooming house with 109 rooms and 54 baths, to the design of the architect Albert Pissis. Four storeys over a basement, brick structure with terra cotta trim, decorative brickwork, two-part commercial block composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
