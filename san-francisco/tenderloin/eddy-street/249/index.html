  1. 1924

    Built as the Standard Apartments, apartment building with 54 2-room units, to the design of the architects William Helbing Company. Six storeys over a basement, steel and reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
