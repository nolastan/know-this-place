  1. 1923

    Built as the Traymore Apartments, apartment building with 24 2- and 3-room units, to the design of the architect David C. Coleman. Five storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
