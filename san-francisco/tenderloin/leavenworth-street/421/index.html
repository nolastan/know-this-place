  1. 1913

    Built as apartment building with 48 2- and 3-room units, to the design of the architects Rousseau & Rousseau. Five storeys over a basement, steel frame and reinforced brick structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
