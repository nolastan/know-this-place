Modernistic Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

StyleArt Deco
Architect as surveyedAndrew H. Knoll
Year built as surveyed1931

A small-scale Art Deco building of 1931 by Andrew H. Knoll, once part of a theatre-supply district. A contributor to the National Register and California Register Uptown Tenderloin Historic District and an Article 11 Category 1 building; its storefront has been altered, but the statement calls it an exuberant example, with a stepped facade, vertical emphasis and floral decoration.
