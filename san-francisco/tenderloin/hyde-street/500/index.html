  1. 1924

    Built as the Frontenac Apartments, apartment building with 100 rooms and 48 baths, to the design of the architects Baumann and Jose. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
