  1. 1926

    Built as the Ben Hur Apartments, apartment building with 69 2-room units, built by the builder Louis Johnson. Seven storeys over a basement, steel frame and reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.
