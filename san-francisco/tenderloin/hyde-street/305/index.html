  1. 1925

    Built as the Princess Pat Apartments, stores and apartments with 39 2-room units, to the design of the architects Helbing Company. Six storeys over a basement, steel frame and reinforced concrete structure, three-part vertical composition, Spanish Colonial Revival.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
