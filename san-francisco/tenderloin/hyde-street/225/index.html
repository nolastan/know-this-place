  1. 1927

    Built as the Hotel LaSalle, mid-priced hotel with 128 rooms and 128 baths, converted to apartments. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure with stucco facade, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.
