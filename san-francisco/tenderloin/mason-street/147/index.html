Intertwined

Johanna Poethig's hand-cut glazed tile ribbon runs from the entrance up one side of the building to the roof, carrying open hands and a welcome whose wording was settled with the GLIDE community. The building was developed for formerly chronically homeless residents by the GLIDE Economic Development Corporation and the Tenderloin Neighborhood Development Corporation.

The city's public-art inventory files this work under “157 Mason”, a street number the city's address registry does not hold.
