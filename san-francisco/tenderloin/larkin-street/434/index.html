  1. 1923

    Built as the Congress Court Apartments, store and apartment building with 26 2-room units. Five storeys over a basement, steel frame and reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
