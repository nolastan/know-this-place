  1. 1860

    The first buildings on this block went up in the late 1860s, and by 1886 about half the block was developed.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, San Francisco Courts Building: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1994.
  2. 1929

    The City leased this lot, empty in a photograph of the Civic Center taken in 1926, to the Standard Oil Company for a service station.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, San Francisco Courts Building: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1994.
  3. 1930

    Built.

  4. 1977

    The service station closed, after forty-eight years on the lot.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, San Francisco Courts Building: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1994.
  5. 1986

    The former service station was demolished and the surface car park put in.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, San Francisco Courts Building: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1994.
