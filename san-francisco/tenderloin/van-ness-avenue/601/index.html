Opera Plaza by Jorge de Quesada with John Carl Warnecke and landscape architect Anthony Guzzardo - a mixed-use complex of retail, offices and flats around a multi-level central courtyard that seems to take its cue from Embarcadero Center, and the conservative end of commercial Late Modernism.
