  1. 1913

    714 Van Ness Avenue

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an used car salesroom.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  2. 1915

    700 Van Ness Avenue

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an used car salesroom. Designed by William Knowles. Its ground-floor arcade was built to show off automobiles.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  3. 1919

    700 Van Ness Avenue

    After a year selling new cars, the ground floor was a used-car salesroom for more than 36 years; a Chevrolet dealer sold used cars here 1938–1960. The second floor was first a business college and then, 1952–1962, a physical culture and yoga studio.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
