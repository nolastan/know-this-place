  1. 1919

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an used car salesroom. Used-car salesrooms occupied the building from its completion through at least 1964 — the only building in the corridor so used from the very beginning. It has lost its pent roof and its original window sash on the Van Ness side, keeping both on the Ellis Street side.
