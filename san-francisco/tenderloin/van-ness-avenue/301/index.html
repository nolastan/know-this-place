  3. 1932

    The War Memorial Complex, designed by G. Albert Lansburgh.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  4. 1978

    The War Memorial Opera House, where a memorial service for Harvey Milk was held in November 1978; the San Francisco Gay Men's Chorus sang here in San Francisco Opera performances in 1988.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  5. 1984

    The War Memorial Veterans Building, administered since 1921 by a trust for the benefit of the city's veterans and the base of the city's American Legion posts; the Alexander Hamilton Post 448, the only gay and lesbian post affiliated with the American Legion, was founded in 1984 and listed its office and monthly meetings here. A designated San Francisco landmark (No. 84).

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
