  1. 1906

    Residential buildings stood on the site until the 1906 earthquake and fire destroyed them; the 1913 Sanborn map shows the Van Ness frontage still vacant.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, 1000 Van Ness Avenue Mixed Use Development/Theater Complex: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 94.618E, 1996.
  2. 1920

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. Dated 1920–1921. Designed by Weeks and Day for the Cadillac dealership of Don Lee. It was monumental in scale and luxurious in detail, and pulled the centre of the showroom trade several blocks south.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  3. 1921

    Cadillac dealerships occupied the building into the 1960s. Its integrity survived its conversion into a cinema.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  4. 1926

    The radio station KFRC broadcast from the building for thirty-one years, from 1926 until 1957.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, 1000 Van Ness Avenue Mixed Use Development/Theater Complex: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 94.618E, 1996.
  5. March 14, 1964

    Two hundred demonstrators organised by the local NAACP chapter picketed the Don Lee Cadillac dealership here on 14 March 1964, part of the Auto Row employment campaign; 107 were arrested. Seven of the dealership's 258 employees were Black.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
