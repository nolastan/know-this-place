  1. 1913

    Built as the Klimm Apartments, apartment building with 20 1-, 2- and 3-room units, to the design of the architects Salfield and Kohlberg. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, galvanized iron cornice and stucco facade, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
