  1. 1911

    Built as the Hetty Apartments, apartment building and stores with 32 2-room units, to the design of the architects Salfield and Kohlberg. Five storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure with galvanized iron bay windows and cornice, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
