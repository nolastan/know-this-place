  1. 1915

    Built as the Verona Apartments, apartment building with 27 2-room units, to the design of the architects Smith and Stewart. Four storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, stucco facade, 3-story bay windows with tile roofs, Spanish parapets, two-part vertical composition, Mission Revival Style.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
