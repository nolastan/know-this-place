  1. 1962

    The Glide Foundation established the Glide Urban Center here in 1962, a community organising centre operating out of Glide Memorial Church; it later housed the offices and symposia of the Council on Religion and the Homosexual.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  2. 1964

    Glide Memorial Methodist Church helped create the Council on Religion and the Homosexual in 1964, after opening a dialogue with the city's homosexual activists about the young hustlers of the Tenderloin. The Council devoted itself to combating homophobia inside mainline churches.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
