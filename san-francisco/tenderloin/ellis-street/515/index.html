  1. 1923

    Built as the Senator Hotel, mid-priced hotel with 114 rooms, to the design of the architects Baumann and Jose. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure with stucco facade, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
