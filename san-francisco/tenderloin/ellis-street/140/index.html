  1. 1855

    Jonathan Kittredge, owner of the Phoenix Iron Works, built a two-and-a-half-storey gabled Carpenter Gothic house on the north side of Ellis Street between Powell and Mason for his Christmas Eve marriage. The homestead included an artesian well, a small backyard orchard and a cow.

    The Argonaut, vol. 30 no. 2, Winter 2020
  2. Jan 1987

    The sidewalk slab rebuilt.

    Complete Permit 8700547 $$$ $12,000
  3. Nov 2001

    Unreinforced-masonry seismic retrofit of the hotel and its ground-floor retail, with accessibility work.

    Complete Permit 200111163350 $$$ $1,000,000
  4. Sep 2006

    A new entrance on Ellis Street and a new sidewalk, a new stair from the ground floor to the basement, and a change of occupancy, with new walls, kitchen, restrooms and HVAC.

    Complete Permit 200609223060 $$$ $463,000
  5. Sep 2010

    Meeting, breakfast and fitness rooms built in vacant street-level space, with a new steel structure creating an additional storey between the first and second levels to carry hotel offices.

    Issued Permit 201009291867 $$$ $350,000
  6. Dec 2012

    An interior remodel of 17,315 sq ft across the first basement and first floor, changing the use to include new assembly space.

    Expired Permit 201212126025 $$$ $1,200,000
  7. Sep 2014

    The aluminium storefront system replaced, and a steel and glass canopy added above the lobby entrance.

    Complete Permit 201409116097 $$$ $128,000
  8. Jan 2019

    The basement restaurant renovated — new interior partitions and finishes, new structural openings in the existing roof and structural walls, and new plumbing and electrical work.

    Issued Permit 201901079715 $$$ $1,251,000

Seven of the 140 filings on this parcel are shown — the largest by stated cost, plus the earliest that carries a description. The rest are fire-alarm and sprinkler permits, sign and street-space permits, fee revisions to the permits above, and the filings DBI holds under the parcel's Cyril Magnin Street numbers. The earliest filing on file, from February 1983, has no description recorded.
