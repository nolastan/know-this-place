  1. 1930

    Built as the Woodson Apartments, apartment building with 53 1- and 2-room units, to the design of the architect H.C. Baumann. Five storeys, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
