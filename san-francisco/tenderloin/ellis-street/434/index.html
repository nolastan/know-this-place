  1. 1926

    Built as the Janice Mirikitani - Glide Family Youth and Child Care Building, transfer and storage building, industrial, to the design of the architects O'Brien Brothers. Three storeys, reinforced structure with stucco facade, two-part commercial composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
