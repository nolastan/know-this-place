Nine of the 147 filings on this parcel are shown — the largest by stated cost, plus the earliest on file. The rest are sprinkler, fire-alarm and hood permits from the culinary-academy fit-out, renewals of expired permits for final inspection, and sign and street-space permits.

Earlier record

  1. 1984

    The building was sold and converted to a culinary academy.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 1, Summer 2020
  2. 1912–1984

    The German House was the social center of San Francisco's German community, housing dozens of clubs and societies.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 1, Summer 2020
  3. Dec 1912

    The German House was completed, following a cornerstone-laying ceremony at Polk and Turk Streets attended by German Consul General Bopp, Mayor James Rolph and Governor Hiram Johnson.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 1, Summer 2020
