  1. 1912

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. The survey dates the work to 1912–1913. Designed by John Galen Howard as the Pierce-Arrow factory branch. It was the finest of the showrooms built away from Van Ness Avenue, and sold Pierce-Arrow and Reo cars into the 1930s.
