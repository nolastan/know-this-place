  1. 1909

    Built as the King Edward Apartments, 65 2-room apartments, to the design of the architect J.R. Miller. Five storeys over a basement, brick structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
