  1. 1911

    Built as the Glenn Hotel, mid-priced hotel with 122-rooms and 115 baths - one bath per room or suite, to the design of the architect William H. Weeks. Nine storeys over a basement, steel frame with brick-clad concrete walls, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
