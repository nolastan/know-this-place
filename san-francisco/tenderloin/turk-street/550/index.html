  1. 1924

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a public garage and shop. Built by Joseph Pasqualetti as the Admiral Garage: two storeys, two bays, reinforced concrete, with both classical and Gothic detail. Its capacity was about 120 to 125 cars.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  2. 1926

    The Admiral Garage, a public garage for 23 years from 1926; an auto repair shop worked here for 21 years as well.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
