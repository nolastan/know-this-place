  1. 1927

    Built as the Chaumont Apartments, apartment building with 25 2- and 3-room units, to the design of the architect Benjamin Schreyer, built by the contractor Isadore M. Somer. Seven storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
