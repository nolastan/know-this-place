  1. 1913

    Built as the Hotel Senate, storefronts intact, to the design of the architect Charles J. Rousseau, engineered by Pierre Zucco Company. Seven storeys over a basement, steel frame and reinforced concrete structure with stucco facade, three-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
