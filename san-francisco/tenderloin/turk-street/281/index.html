  1. 1923

    Built as the Padre Apartments, 49 unit apartment building, mostly 2-room units, to the design of the architects Baumann and Jose. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure with stucco facade, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.

    National Register of Historic Places nomination, National Park Service
