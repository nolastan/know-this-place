  1. 1911

    Built as the Hotel Schwartz, rooming house with 123 rooms and 38 baths, dining room, to the design of the architects George Streshly and Company. Six storeys over a basement, reinforced concrete structure, two-part vertical composition, Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation.
