Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

Survey findingSignificant for industrial associations, not architecture
StyleMission Revival
Year built as surveyed1911

The Klockars Blacksmith Shop of 1911: a Mission Revival building with a simple shaped roof parapet, no other ornament and, unusually for the style, wood cladding rather than stucco. Its significance rests on its association with industry in the city rather than on its architecture.
