  1. 1924

    Pacific Telephone and Telegraph Building, designed by Alexander Aimwell Cantin. In collaboration with Miller & Pflueger.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  2. 1925

    Completed as the Pacific Telephone Building, a 26-storey office tower then the tallest building in San Francisco and the foremost example of Art Deco architecture in the Central SoMa area. Its design, like the Chronicle building's, shows the influence of Eliel Saarinen's 1922 drawing for the Chicago Tribune Tower.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
