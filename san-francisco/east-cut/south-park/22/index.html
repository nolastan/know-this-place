Earlier record

  1. 1910s

    Run as the Eimoto Hotel, described in a published history of San Francisco's Japantown as a first stop in the United States for many Japanese arrivals, who landed at the nearby Piers 30–32 used by Japanese steamship companies.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
