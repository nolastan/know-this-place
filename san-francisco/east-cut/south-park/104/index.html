Earlier record

  1. 1920s

    One of the prominent Japanese establishments at South Park, alongside the Higoyo Hotel, the Biwako Baths and the Hotel Bo-Chow, in an enclave that formed after 1910 and dispersed to Japantown in the 1930s when the Japanese steamship lines moved to piers north of the Ferry Building.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1930s

    The former Omiya Hotel was purchased by the Gran Oriente Filipino Masonic Lodge, and served as a meeting place and boarding house for members working in San Francisco and for farm workers from the Central Valley who visited at weekends. It is among the longest-held Filipino-owned cultural assets in San Francisco, and was put forward for San Francisco Landmark designation.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
