Public art

  • Boy III — Thomas Houseago
    Cast bronze with white patina finish; building lobby; Artwork is accessible during normal business hours, 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.; artwork is visible from Howard Street.
    Thomas Houseago, born in Leeds, England in 1972, cast Boy III and its companion Sleeping Boy in white-coated bronze, a departure from the plaster he traditionally worked in.
  • Sleeping Boy — Thomas Houseago
    Cast bronze; building lobby; artwork is accessible during normal business hours, 8:00 a.m to 7:00 p.m; artwork is visible from Howard Street.
    Sleeping Boy is the reclining companion piece to Boy III; both are cast in white-coated bronze with casting marks left intentionally unsmoothed by the artist.
