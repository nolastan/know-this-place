Public art

  • Not Out of the Woods Yet — Richard Deacon
    Aluminum tread plate; under arcade of the plaza (Foundry Square); open space and artwork are always accessible.
    Installed 2003. Made of aluminum tread plate, the sculpture sits behind columns at the entrance to 500 Howard Street; San Francisco Chronicle critic Kenneth Baker wrote it 'nests muscularly in a tight spot.'
