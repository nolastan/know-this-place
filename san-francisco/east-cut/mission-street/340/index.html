    A commercial building, heavily altered since.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
  2. 1923

    Built in 1923 to the design of George Applegarth for the Cebrian Company, a family real estate firm, as a speculative venture; textile firms such as the pattern-maker Butterick Company and publishers' offices such as the MacMillan Company took space in it. It was cleared for the office tower of 2015.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 350 Mission Street Office Project: Final Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 2006.1524E, 2011.

Virtual Depictions

Refik Anadol's media wall was dedicated on 30 September 2015 and runs a twelve-chapter, ninety-minute programme with eight-channel sound on a screen 20 by 40 feet. On 6 September 2024 a pane of glass fell from the roof of the building and damaged the Millennium Tower next door.
