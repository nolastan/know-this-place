Public art

  • Moonrise Sculptures: March, October and December — Ugo Rondinone
    Aluminum; plaza; open space and artwork are always accessible.
    Installed 2008. Three mottled aluminum sculptures of oversized heads stand in the plaza outside 555 Mission Street.
  • Human Structures — Jonathan Borofsky
    Steel; plaza; open space and artwork are always accessible.
    Installed 2008. 62 life-size steel figures, water-jet cut and bolted hand-to-hand and head-to-toe, form five interconnected towers in a dozen coded colors. The dataset's title 'Human Sculptures' should read 'Human Structures.' The city's inventory titles it “Human Sculptures”.
