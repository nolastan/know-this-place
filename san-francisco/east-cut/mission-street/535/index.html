Public art

  • Applique da Parete — Gordon Huether
    Glass mounted to stone backing; lobby wall, visible from street (Shaw Alley); Artwork is accessible from 8am - 6pm (M-F); it is visible from Shaw Alley.
    Installed 2014. A 140-foot-long, 18-foot-tall installation of 355 individual glass panels on Corten steel, commissioned by Boston Properties along Shaw Alley.
  • The Band — Anton Josef Standteiner
    Bronze, copper, steel; In POPOS, corner of Minna & Shaw; artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 2014. Four bronze, copper and steel sculptures, each about 10 feet tall, representing members of a music group at Minna Street and Shaw Alley.
