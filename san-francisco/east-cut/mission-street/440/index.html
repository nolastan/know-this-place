  1. 1920

    Built as the C.C. Moore Building, a masonry commercial loft with Gothic-inspired detail.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
  2. November 1934

    The State Emergency Relief Administration consolidated its scattered San Francisco offices into the C.C. Moore/Terminal Plaza Building here in November 1934, and the Works Progress Administration made the same building its first San Francisco headquarters in 1935.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
