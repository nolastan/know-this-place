  1. 1939

    Built as the Transbay Terminal to designs by Timothy Pflueger and Arthur Brown Jr., linked to the new Bay Bridge by a looping concrete viaduct that carried Key System trains into downtown. It was planned for 35 million passengers a year and handled 26 million at its wartime peak.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
  2. 1959

    Turned into a regional bus depot, after the lower deck of the Bay Bridge was given over to cars in 1958 and the Key System dismantled.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
