  1. 1904

    Erected as a ten-story office building with ornate Renaissance and Baroque detail.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
  2. April 18, 1906

    One of a handful of buildings left standing in the Central SoMa area after the earthquake and fire, most of them steel-framed.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  3. 1913

    Architect Warren C. Perry established his own practice here, in the building that also housed John Galen Howard’s firm.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  4. 1930s

    Remodelled during the Depression-era 'Modernize Main Street' campaign.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  5. 1931

    Refaced to designs by the architect John V.D. Linden, the failing terra-cotta ornament stripped away and replaced with fluted terra cotta and pared-back Art Deco detail.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
