Public art

  • Annular Eclipse — George Rickey
    Aluminum; urban garden; open space and artwork are always accessible.
    Installed 2000. George Rickey (1907-2002), an American kinetic sculptor, built the work from two circles that remain in contact and move only by wind and gravity, without motors. Sources differ on the work's exact title: one names it 'Annular Eclipse V', while the George Rickey Foundation's public collection page names the 560 Mission Street work 'Annular Eclipse Sixteen Feet II' (1999-2000).
