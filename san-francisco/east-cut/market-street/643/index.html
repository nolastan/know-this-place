  1. October 5, 1875

    The Palace Hotel opened here to designs by the New York architect John P. Gaynor, the largest and best-appointed hotel in the country at the time.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
  2. 1909

    The shell of the 1875 hotel was pulled down after the earthquake and a new hotel built in its place to designs by the New York firm Trowbridge & Livingston.

    Transit Center District Survey, adopted 2012
