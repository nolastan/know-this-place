The survey prints this building's address two ways: 215 Market Street in one table and 245 Market Street in another.

Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

StyleChicago School
Year built as surveyed1922–1924 (215 Market Street); 1923 (245 Market Street)
Address as surveyed215 Market Street and 245 Market Street

Two buildings on this parcel, each named as a Chicago School example: the Matson Building at 215 Market Street, designed by Bliss & Faville, and the PG&E General Office Building at 245 Market Street, designed by Bakewell & Brown.

Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. The dates on record for the Matson Building disagree: this page carries 1921, the 2008 Transit Center survey dates the building on the parcel to 1922, and the city's statement on Progressive Era and early revival styles gives 1923 in its text and 1922-1924 in its illustration caption.
