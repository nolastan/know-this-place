Two open spaces, two street numbers

The city's inventory lists a second space at this parcel under “14 Fremont Street” — a pedestrian walkway with café tables, planting beds with sitting ledges and a travertine-clad colonnade along First Street. The address registry has no 14 Fremont; the parcel is numbered 28 Fremont on that side.
