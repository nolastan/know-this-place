Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StylePostmodernism
Architect as surveyedSkidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM)
Year built as surveyed1986

A flatiron office tower by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill - a circle enveloped by a triangle - in slick polished granite with a tripartite composition. Held up as the building where the firm's shift from stalwart Modernists to willing Postmodernists is clearest, and as appearing eligible under California Register Criterion 3. Its design took a Progressive Architecture award in 1983.
