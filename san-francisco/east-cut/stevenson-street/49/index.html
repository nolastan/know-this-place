Public art

  • Escalieta I — Manuel Neri
    Ordinario marble; 49 Stevenson entrance; open space and artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 1985. Escalieta I is a marble sculpture by Manuel Neri (1930-2021), an American sculptor and a leading member of the Bay Area Figurative Movement's "second generation," installed at 49 Stevenson Street through San Francisco's 1% Public Art Program.
