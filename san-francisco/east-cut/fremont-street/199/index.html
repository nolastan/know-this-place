Public art

  • Big Bertha — Paul Kos
    Granite; center of sun terrace; artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 2000. The granite boulder sits in a POPOS between the office towers that also displays poetry by U.S. Poet Laureate Robert Hass. Paul Kos, born December 23, 1942, is an American artist.
