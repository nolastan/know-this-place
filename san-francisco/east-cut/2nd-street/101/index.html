Public art

  • Sumer #24 — Larry Bell
    Bronze; indoor park; artwork is available from 8 am - 6 pm.
    Larry Bell, born in 1939 in Chicago and associated with the Light and Space movement, cast Sumer #24 in bronze as part of a series referencing the ancient civilization of Sumer.
  • Core — Charles Arnoldi
    Canvas, acrylic; indoor park; artwork is available from 8 am - 6 pm.
    Charles Arnoldi, an American painter, sculptor and printmaker, was born in 1946 in Dayton, Ohio, and had his first solo exhibition at Los Angeles's Riko Mizuno Gallery in 1971.
