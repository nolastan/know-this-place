Earlier record

  1. 1909

    Built by the Pile Driver, Bridge and Structural Iron Workers No. 77 as a mixed-use building holding the union's hall. City directories of the 1920s show it also used by the Labor Carpenter's Union No. 22 and the Warehouse and Cereal Workers No. 38. It is among the oldest surviving union halls in San Francisco and is recognized as individually eligible for the California Register.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1934

    One of at least two labour halls in the Central SoMa area during the 1934 Waterfront Strike.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
