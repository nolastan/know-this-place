Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

2AES - the Art and Architecture Exhibition Space - a non-profit gallery started here in the late 1980s by architect Mark Horton and later run with a group of architects and a landscape architect. It showed visionary and experimental work, much of it unbuilt 'paper architecture', and marked a turn away from Postmodernism among younger Bay Area designers.
