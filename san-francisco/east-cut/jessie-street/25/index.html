One Ecker Square

Perini Land & Development built the tower on speculation in 1982–83. The state historic-resource form records a hybrid of Corporate Modern and Brutalist manner, and reads the two-storey, south-facing open-air atrium as a response to the city's 1971 Urban Design Plan — the thinking that produced the open-space requirement this page's garden satisfies.
