San Francisco Seacliff

Seacliff

An affluent residential enclave on the bluffs above the Golden Gate, laid out from the 1910s, much of it within the Sea Cliff Historic District.

Streets documented so far

  • El Camino Del Mar
    An eighteen-room 1926 house above the Golden Gate; a part-time Robin Williams residence.
  • 24th Avenue
    One 1903 house across two lots, tied to Ansel Adams.
