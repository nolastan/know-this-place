# 540 El Camino Del Mar

An eighteen-room house of 1926 on a 17,149-square-foot lot above the Golden
Gate — the largest lot documented on this site, inside the Sea Cliff Historic
District, and in the middle of the largest run of permitted work recorded here.

## The building

- Built 1926 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1926)
- 1 unit, 18 rooms, 7 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 9,310 sq ft; lot 17,149 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-1(D); Supervisorial District 1

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A — a historical resource, by virtue of district membership
- Sea Cliff Historic District, period of significance **1913–1935**
- **Eligible** for the California Register, not listed; not on the National
  Register; not an Article 10 or 11 district, so the parcel carries no local
  landmark protection

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records this as a
part-time residence of Robin Williams, and mentions two benches inscribed with
his parents' names. No dates are given and no underlying source is cited.

## Permit history

Fifteen permits are on file, 1991–2026. They fall into three episodes. The
first, in 1991–92, opens with a $400,000 horizontal addition filed three months
after the recorded sale; it expired, as did its 1992 revision, and only the
interior demolition was completed. Nothing substantial follows for a quarter of
a century. Then, between May 2025 and June 2026, five permits were filed in
thirteen months — a remodel whose estimate was revised from $650,000 to
$2,343,508, plus bathrooms, a basement excavation, solar with battery storage,
and a landscape scheme.

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **9 July 1991**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $2,652,567 and improvements at $3,006,246 — $5,658,813 total.
The roll predates the 2025–26 work.
