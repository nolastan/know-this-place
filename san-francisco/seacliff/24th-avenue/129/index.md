# 129–131 24th Avenue

One house of 1903 spanning two contiguous Sea Cliff lots, numbered 129 and 131.
Published guides tie the address to Ansel Adams, and its permit record is
dominated by repeated foundation work.

## The building

- Built 1903 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1903)
- 1 dwelling, 9 rooms, 3 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 3,240 sq ft; combined lot 8,999 sq ft (lot 002: 5,754;
  lot 002A: 3,245)
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-1(D); Supervisorial District 1
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — lot 002 carries a survey result, lot 002A
  is unevaluated; neither a designated landmark nor in a historic district

## One building, two lots and two numbers

The city treats 129 and 131 as one structure on two parcels. Lot 002A (the 131
side) is classed "Single Struct on Multi Lot" and reports zero building area —
the building is carried entirely on lot 002 — and both parcels' assessor
records give the location as 129 24th Avenue. This site documents them as a
single building, which is why the page is titled with the range.

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records 129 as Ansel
Adams's childhood home, giving his birth on 20 February 1902, and 131 as a
later property with a studio that the guide says was destroyed in a sinkhole.
The city data does not distinguish the two numbers as separate buildings, so
the guide's split is carried here as its own account rather than as fact. No
underlying source is cited for either claim.

## Permit history

Twelve distinct permits are on file, 1995–2024. DBI files the shared foundation
permits under both lots, so a raw query returns several twice; the duplicates
are collapsed here. The record's dominant theme is the ground: emergency
underpinning of two corners in 1995, larger underpinning of the whole building
in 1996, and a stem-wall foundation along the north side later that year. The
most recent and largest work is a 2024 interior remodel with an elevator added.

- **2024** — interior remodel: remove and rebuild the lower-level stair,
  remodel the sunroom, kitchen, bath and upper bedrooms ($1,500,000) —
  [permit 202407166594](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202407166594);
  revised to add an elevator and equipment room ($30,000) —
  [permit 202410082508](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202410082508)
- **2020** — emergency repair of the deck over the garage: structural beams,
  guardrails, waterproofing, deck surface ($56,000 revised) —
  [permit 202010287644](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202010287644)
- **2004** — build a 7-foot fence along the north property line and remove a
  shed ($900) —
  [permit 200404010238](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200404010238)
- **1998–2000** — matching horizontal additions on each lot ($11,000 and
  $10,000), both expired, and a 2000 renewal filed for final sign-off —
  [9826157S](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9826157S),
  [9826156S](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9826156S),
  [200010173261](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200010173261)
- **1998** — reroofing ($5,000), expired —
  [permit 9822911](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9822911)
- **1996** — stem-wall foundation along the north side ($11,750) —
  [permit 9622101](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9622101)
- **1996** — underpin the building to provide stability, filed on both lots
  ($72,000 and $42,000) —
  [9607813](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9607813),
  [9607812](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9607812)
- **1995** — emergency underpinning of two corners of the house ($8,000) —
  [permit 9521143](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9521143)

The 1990s permits nearly all expired rather than completing; the ground work
that finally held is not clearly recorded.

## Value

The 2025 roll carries the two parcels separately: lot 002 at $92,773 land plus
$109,934 improvements, lot 002A at $72,055 land plus $82,789 improvements —
$357,551 across both. No sale appears on the current roll for either, and the
low base reflects a long-held Proposition 13 base year.

## Not yet documented
