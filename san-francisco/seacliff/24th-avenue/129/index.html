One building, two lots and two numbers

The city treats 129 and 131 as a single structure on two parcels. The 131 lot is classed “Single Struct on Multi Lot” and reports zero building area — the building is carried entirely on the 129 lot — and both parcels' assessor records give the location as 129 24th Avenue. This site documents them as one building, which is why the page is titled with the range.

The guide's account

A published guide records 129 as Ansel Adams's childhood home, giving his birth on 20 February 1902, and 131 as a later property with a studio it says was destroyed in a sinkhole. The city data does not separate the two numbers into two buildings, so the guide's account of two houses is set out here rather than adopted.

Not yet documented: the architect and builder; whether the studio the guide places at 131 was ever a separate structure and what became of it; what the "sinkhole" claim refers to; which of the lapsed 1990s foundation permits was actually built; and any primary-source basis for the Adams residency.
