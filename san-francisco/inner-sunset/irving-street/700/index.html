Earlier record

  1. 1902

    Built 1902. Builder: J. B. Ogborn (contractor). Built for owner John Meierdierks; northwest corner of Ninth/Irving; held Dessel's Pharmacy then Rexall Drugs for decades.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  2. 1922

    Built 1922. Six stores; Old Home Pie Shop occupied the corner store ca. 1945-1951.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
