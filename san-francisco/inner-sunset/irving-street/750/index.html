  1. 1930

    Built 1930. Architect: Albert Schroepfer. Dessel's Pharmacy; Frank W. Dessel moved his pharmacy business here in 1931 from 800 Irving Street, owned it through 1949, Dessel's continued under other owners into the 1990s.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
