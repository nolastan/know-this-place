Earlier record

  1. 1920-1921

    Built 1920-1921. Architect: James R. Miller. Jefferson School; gymnasium added 1928 by City Architect Charles H. Sawyer; main building destroyed by arson 1959 and replaced; 1928 gymnasium survives.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  2. 1917

    Built 1917. Architect: G. Albert Lansburgh. San Francisco City Landmark. Sunset Branch Library, one of four SF branch libraries Lansburgh designed (with Mission 1915, Presidio 1921, Chinatown 1921); one of the Inner Sunset's three City Landmarks.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
