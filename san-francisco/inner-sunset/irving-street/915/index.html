Stylehistoricist pastiche
Architect as surveyedOptimal Design Group
Year built as surveyed1985

A mock Edwardian mixed-use building by Optimal Design Group, with bay windows, wood spandrels and window surrounds and cornice details like those of true Edwardian buildings, but in modern materials, with less ornament and lesser workmanship.
