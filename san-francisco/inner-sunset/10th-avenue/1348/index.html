  1. 1899

    Built 1899. Architect: Charles R. Wilson. Contractor: C. F. Sheibley. San Francisco Landmark No. 29 (designated 1970). SFFD Engine Co. No. 22 firehouse; company moved to 1290 Sixteenth Avenue in 1962; one of ~7 surviving 19th-c. SF firehouses; one of three Inner Sunset City Landmarks along with the Sunset Branch Library (1305 Eighteenth Avenue) and 320 Judah Street.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
