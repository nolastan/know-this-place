  1. 1932

    Built 1932. Architect: Charles O. Clausen. San Francisco City Landmark No. 265. The Henry Doelger studio: planning office, sales office, architectural studio and warehouse of builder Henry Doelger, 1932-1950; addition to the west in 1940; from here Doelger planned construction across the Sunset district and his Westlake, Daly City development.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024

Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleStreamline Moderne

Named as one of two good San Francisco examples of the Streamline Moderne style, alongside the Aquatic Park Bathhouse of 1939 — a comparison the statement draws to place the single Streamline Moderne building in Duboce Triangle. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.

Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. The 2013 Sunset District builders context statement dates this building to 1932; the assessor dates the building on this parcel to 1940. Submit an update
