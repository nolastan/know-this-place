# Inner Sunset

The blocks between Golden Gate Park and Mount Sutro, laid out along the Muni N-Judah line that reached them through the Sunset Tunnel in 1928. Coverage begins with a single building named in the 2022 Duboce Triangle context statement as a citywide example of the Streamline Moderne style.

## Streets documented so far

- [Judah Street](judah-street/) — 1 building, built 1940.
