Earlier record

  1. 1910

    The Park View Hotel, at Lincoln Way; a residential hotel with an attached saloon in 1910, 31 residents that year, many employed leveling the neighborhood's sand hills.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  2. 1905

    Built 1905. Contractor: Munster and Company. Corner building with four stores, for owner George Kammerer; replaced by a new building in 1930.

    Inner Sunset Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
