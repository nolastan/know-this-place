# Presidio

The former army post at the city's northwest corner, now federal parkland,
filed as its own neighborhood because the city's analysis neighborhoods treat
it as one. Its places are the ones the National Park Service and the Presidio
Trust list inside it — batteries, beaches, overlooks, parade grounds,
cemeteries, the post's historic buildings — each with a page of its own; the
land under them is one parcel, whose assessor and permit record is its page on
Moraga Avenue.

## Streets documented so far

- [Moraga Avenue](moraga-avenue/) — The one parcel under the whole Presidio, filed under the lowest of its Moraga Avenue numbers.
