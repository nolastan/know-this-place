San Francisco Golden Gate Park

Presidio

The former army post at the city's northwest corner, now federal parkland, filed as its own neighborhood because the city's analysis neighborhoods treat it as one. Its places are the ones the National Park Service and the Presidio Trust list inside it — batteries, beaches, overlooks, parade grounds, cemeteries, the post's historic buildings — each with a page of its own; the land under them is one parcel, whose assessor and permit record is its page on Moraga Avenue.

Streets documented so far

Adjacent neighborhoods

Seacliff adjoins to the south-west and the Inner Richmond to the south, along the Presidio wall; Presidio Heights and Pacific Heights are south-east, and the Marina is east across Lyon Street.
