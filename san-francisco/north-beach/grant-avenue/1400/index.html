  1. 1909

    An apartment building the city's survey uses to illustrate the type in North Beach.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1912

    Built for the Italian-American Paste Company; later the Old Spaghetti Factory.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1950–1965

    The city's survey names the Old Spaghetti Factory at this address among the bars, cafes, bookshops and clubs of the North Beach Beat scene, whose period of significance it sets at about 1950 to 1965.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  4. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Upper Dupont Street / Grant Avenue District.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  5. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a listed resource. San Francisco City Landmark No. 127.
