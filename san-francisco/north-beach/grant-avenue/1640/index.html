  1. 1852

    Third Baptist Church founded

    Nine Black Christians gathered to begin the religious and social-cultural activities of the congregation, on Dupont — now Grant Avenue — between Greenwich and Filbert.

    Philip M. Montesano, “San Francisco Black Churches in the Early 1860s: Political Pressure Group,” The Argonaut: Journal of the San Francisco Historical Society, vol. 31, no. 2 (Winter 2021), pp. 54–61
  2. 1854

    First church building

    The First Colored Baptist Church of San Francisco moved into its first church building, on Dupont Street between Greenwich and Filbert. The State of California's historical landmark registration application for the congregation gives the address as 1642–44 Grant Avenue.

    SF Planning Department, Article 10 Landmark Designation Report, Third Baptist Church Complex, 1399 McAllister Street (Case 2017-004024DES)
  3. 1855

    The congregation took the name Third Baptist Church, as the third Baptist communion founded in the city and to be an inclusive church without a racial designation.

    SF Planning Department, Article 10 Landmark Designation Report, Third Baptist Church Complex, 1399 McAllister Street (Case 2017-004024DES)
  4. 1896

    The congregation left this site for a building at Bush and Powell Streets, which the 1906 earthquake and fire destroyed.

    SF Planning Department, Article 10 Landmark Designation Report, Third Baptist Church Complex, 1399 McAllister Street (Case 2017-004024DES)
