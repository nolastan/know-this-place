  2. 1950–1965

    The city's survey names the Co-Existence Bagel Shop at this address among the bars, cafes, bookshops and clubs of the North Beach Beat scene, whose period of significance it sets at about 1950 to 1965.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
