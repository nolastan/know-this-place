  1. 1937

    The Li Po Cocktail Lounge, one of the last intact Chinatown nightclubs, dating to 1937, in a building of about 1906. Its neon sign in the shape of a Chinese lantern was added about the 1940s and restored in 2018; it is a fully expressed example of neon in the Asian Eclectic manner, and belongs to the tourist-facing signage installed across Chinatown after 1906.

    Neighborhood Commercial Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1939

    The Li Po cocktail lounge, advertised in a 1939 tourism guide, remains in operation here.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
