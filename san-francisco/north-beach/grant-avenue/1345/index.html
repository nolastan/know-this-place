  1. 1907

    Built.

  2. 1923

    A photograph in the city's collection records Fiorio and Figone Hardware trading here.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1950–1965

    The city's survey names the Coffee Gallery at this address among the bars, cafes, bookshops and clubs of the North Beach Beat scene, whose period of significance it sets at about 1950 to 1965.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  4. 1954

    Miss Smith's Tea Room operated here between 1954 and 1960, one of the North Beach bars and restaurants the city's survey identifies as significant in San Francisco's LGBTQ history.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  5. 1960

    The poet Bob Kaufman read his work at the Coffee Gallery here.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  6. March 1966

    Edward Stanton photographed the property, showing the Coffee Gallery window.

    Edward Stanton Photographs (SFP 55), San Francisco Public Library
  7. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Upper Dupont Street / Grant Avenue District.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
