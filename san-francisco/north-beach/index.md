# North Beach

The blocks between Telegraph Hill and Russian Hill, around Washington Square
and the upper reaches of Grant Avenue, which was formerly Dupont Street.

## Streets documented so far

- [Grant Avenue](grant-avenue/) — Three 1907 flats on the Dupont Street site of the First Colored Baptist Church's first building.
- [Greenwich Street](greenwich-street/) — 6 buildings, built 1907–1933.
- [Lombard Street](lombard-street/) — 1 building, built 1926.
- [Pfeiffer Street](pfeiffer-street/) — 1 building, built 1956.
- [Powell Street](powell-street/) — 1 building, built 1906.
- [Union Street](union-street/) — 1 building, built 1900; 1 in the Telegraph Hill Historic District.
