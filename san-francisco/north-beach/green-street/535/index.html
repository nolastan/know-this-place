  1. 1926

  2. 1946

    Built as an industrial building for Buon Gusto Sausage Factory, to the design of Martin Rist, one of the modern architects the city's survey identifies as working in North Beach.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Upper Dupont Street / Grant Avenue District. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places (SG100006073).

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
