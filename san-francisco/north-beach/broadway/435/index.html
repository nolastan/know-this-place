  1. 1913

    The 1913 Sanborn map shows Garibaldi Hall here as a two-story wood building with a hall on the second floor and lodge rooms. The city's survey attributes the building to the architect Henry Pizzigoni, who designed it for the Compagnia Garibaldina di Mutuo Soccorso.
