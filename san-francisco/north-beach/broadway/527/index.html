  2. 1950–1965

    The city's survey names Specs' 12 Adler Place at this address among the bars, cafes, bookshops and clubs of the North Beach Beat scene, whose period of significance it sets at about 1950 to 1965.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1952

    Tommy's Place operated here between 1952 and 1955, one of the North Beach bars and restaurants the city's survey identifies as significant in San Francisco's LGBTQ history.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  4. 1952

    Tommy's Place, entered from Broadway and connected to the bar at 12 Adler Place, opened here in June 1952; after the September 1954 police raid the liquor licence passed to the building's owner and the bar was renamed Frank's.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  5. 1956

    12 Adler Place operated here from 1956, one of the North Beach bars and restaurants the city's survey identifies as significant in San Francisco's LGBTQ history.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  6. 1968

    Spec's Twelve Adler Museum Cafe traces its business here to 1968; the city's survey lists it among North Beach's legacy bars, restaurants and businesses, a concentration it records as the highest in the city.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  7. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as an individually listed resource.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
