  1. 1906

    Built to the design of Paul J. DeMartini, whose North Beach work the city's survey documents.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1912

    Built.

  3. 1913

    The Italian Club occupied this building — a reconstruction of Washington Square Hall — on the 1913 Sanborn map; by 1949 the hall had been taken over by a restaurant.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  4. 1937

    Original Joe's traces its business here to 1937; the city's survey lists it among North Beach's legacy bars, restaurants and businesses, a concentration it records as the highest in the city.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  5. 1954

    Interior of Fior d'Italia, designed by architect Mario Gaidano; the restaurant left the 1912 building after 2005.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  6. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Washington Square Historic District.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
