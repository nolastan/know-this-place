  1. 1913

    The 1913 and 1949 Sanborn maps show a bocce ball alley in a long one-story wood building behind this address, on the south side of Cadell Alley.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1949

    The Paper Doll operated here between 1949 and 1961, one of the North Beach bars and restaurants the city's survey identifies as significant in San Francisco's LGBTQ history.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Upper Dupont Street / Grant Avenue District.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
