  1. 1934

    Mona's, the city's first lesbian establishment operated here between 1934 and 1935, one of the North Beach bars and restaurants the city's survey identifies as significant in San Francisco's LGBTQ history.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1934

    Mona's, the city's first lesbian bar, opened here in 1934 as a hangout for artists and writers and moved on the following year.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
