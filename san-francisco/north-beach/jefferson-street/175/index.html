Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StyleNew Formalism
Architect as surveyedEdward Wong

Built about 1964 as A. Sabella's Restaurant by architect Edward Wong, an example of New Formalist design in a neighbourhood commercial building. Substantially altered since - ground floor, signage, awnings, and a new balcony and replacement windows breaking the fenestration pattern - so that the statement finds it no longer retains overall integrity.
