A residence by Jim Jennings, shown in model form in SFMOMA's 1991 'In the Spirit of Modernism' exhibition and later named a 1998 Record House. Stuccoed boxy massing and gridded metal windows refer back to the International Style; inside, an exposed concrete cylindrical circulation core is lit from above.
