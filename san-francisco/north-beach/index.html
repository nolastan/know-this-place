San Francisco North Beach

North Beach

The blocks between Telegraph Hill and Russian Hill, around Washington Square and the upper reaches of Grant Avenue, which was formerly Dupont Street.

Adjacent neighborhoods

Chinatown adjoins south along Grant Avenue and Broadway and the Financial District south-east below Telegraph Hill. Russian Hill rises south-west up the Vallejo Street grade.
