  1. 1907

    A new Neighborhood House designed by Bernard Maybeck was completed here for the settlement organisation displaced in 1906; it held homemaking classes and a health clinic and housed the Telegraph Hill Neighborhood Association until 1954.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1968

    Listed in the 1968 citywide survey of San Francisco's architectural heritage as Telegraph Hill Neighborhood Association.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as an individually listed resource.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
