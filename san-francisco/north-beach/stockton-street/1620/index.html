The city's survey dates the Italian Athletic Club hall to 1936 in its account of halls, while its architect appendix gives 1935 in the text and 1927 in the figure caption for the club at 1630 Stockton Street. The hall's construction year is given as 1935 by the city's 2026 statement on clubs and social halls, 1936 by its 2022 North Beach statement, and 1936 by the assessor.
