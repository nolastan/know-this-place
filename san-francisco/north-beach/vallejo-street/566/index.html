  1. 1955

    Built as an apartment building to the design of Lloyd Conrich, one of the modern architects the city's survey identifies as working in North Beach.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1955

    Two narrow apartment buildings separated by an outdoor pool and courtyard, sitting almost entirely inside the block. Parking is reached from two alleyways and the courtyard from a street frontage of just nineteen and a half feet, making it among the most camouflaged infill projects of its period.

    Large Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
