  1. 1860

    The church of St Francis of Assisi, founded in 1849 as the city's first Roman Catholic parish after Mission Dolores, was dedicated in this building in 1860; it holds the first parochial school in San Francisco and the first ordination of priests.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1968

    Listed in the 1968 citywide survey of San Francisco's architectural heritage as Church of St. Francis of Assisi.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Upper Dupont Street / Grant Avenue District.
