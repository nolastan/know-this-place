  1. 1905

    The settlement house moved down the hill to this address, running a dispensary at 536 Green Street alongside it; the buildings on both sites were destroyed in 1906.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1924

    The church of Saints Peter and Paul, the parish founded in 1884 for the city's Italians, was rebuilt on Washington Square from 1912 and completed in 1924; its decorated facade was added in 1940.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as a contributor to the Washington Square Historic District and an individually listed resource.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
