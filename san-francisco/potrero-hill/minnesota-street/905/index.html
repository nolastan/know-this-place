One of a row of Stick/Eastlake cottages built to designs from Pelton's Cheap Dwellings, each keeping a parapeted roofline, faux gables over the windows, elongated brackets and wooden cladding. The document's caption year of 1900 is the assessor's roll figure, not a researched date.
