Earlier record

  1. 1905

    A grocery store opened here, kept by one of the first Italian families to settle at the edge of Dogpatch — the start of an influx that made Italian-born residents the second-largest group in the neighborhood by 1920.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001

Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

Address as surveyed1204 19th Street

Named in the survey's account of Dogpatch, though it lies west of Indiana Street and so outside the 2001 survey boundary. The assessor dates the building to 1907, two years after the grocery opened.
