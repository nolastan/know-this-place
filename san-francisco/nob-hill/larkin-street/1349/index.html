  2. 1909

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a garage, auto repair shop 1, 3. Dated 1909–1910. Designed by Ward and Blohme as a private garage; it became the Alaska Garage, a public garage, in 1913. Brick-built, with a symmetrical façade.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  3. 1913

    The Alaska Garage, a public garage for fourteen years from 1913, run for most of them together with the Graystone Garage next door.
