Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StylePostmodernism
Architect as surveyedJames Ingo Freed of Pei Cobb Freed & Partners with Cathy Simon of SMWM
Year built as surveyed1996

The new main library by James Ingo Freed of Pei Cobb Freed & Partners with Cathy Simon of SMWM: a Postmodern grey granite face to the Civic Center in keeping with its Beaux-Arts neighbours, and side and rear elevations closer to New Modernism, stripped of ornament and worked in rotated volumes and punched openings. It won an AIA and American Library Association award for library architecture in 1997.
