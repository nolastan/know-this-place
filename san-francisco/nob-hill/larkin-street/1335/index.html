  1. 1913

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a public garage. Dated 1913–1914. Designed by John H. Powers as the Graystone Garage. For most of its 27 years as a public garage it was run together with the Alaska Garage next door at 1349 Larkin Street.
