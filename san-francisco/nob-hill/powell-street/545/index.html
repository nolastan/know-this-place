The clubhouse's completion year is given as 1912 by the city's 2026 statement on clubs and social halls; the assessor's roll and the city's survey record give 1909. The club itself was founded in 1901, by members who had broken away from the Bohemian Club. Not yet documented: the developer, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale.
