  1. 1906

    The St. Francis Hotel, designed by the firm Bliss & Faville. A first hotel of the same name stood here from 1904 until the 1906 earthquake destroyed it; this is the second.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  2. 1950s

    The St. Francis Hotel, whose Orchid Room and Oak Room were among the bars sustaining Union Square's long use as a gathering area.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
