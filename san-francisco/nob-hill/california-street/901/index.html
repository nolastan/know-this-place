  1. 1954

    The Stanford Court Hotel, a men-only residential hotel with community showers, recorded in a 1954 journal at fifty dollars a month; the building is partially extant.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  2. 1971

    A residential garden laid out in 1971 by the landscape architect Thomas D. Church, who designed more than 150 gardens in San Francisco and is widely counted the founder of Modern California landscape design.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
