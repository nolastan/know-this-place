  1. 1878-1880

    Designed by William F. Curlett. With Maxwell Bugbee, Samuel Bugbee and Walter Cuthbertson. The building was demolished in 1906.

  2. 1965

    Grace Cathedral, picketed on 26 September 1965 by about thirty demonstrators protesting the Episcopal Diocese's curtailment of a canon's duties over his support for gay and lesbian rights; in 1976 it hosted part of the Conference on Violence Against Women.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
