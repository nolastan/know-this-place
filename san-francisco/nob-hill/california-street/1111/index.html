  1. 1956

    Designed by artist and architect Millard Sheets.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  2. 1958

    Built as the California Memorial Masonic Temple, to house the Masonic Lodge that had been at 25 Van Ness Avenue. One of the last purpose-built clubs and social halls of its kind in the city, put up as fraternal membership peaked after the Second World War.

    Citywide Historic Context Statement: Clubs and Social Halls (1848–1989), 2026

    The Masonic Auditorium, 1958, by Albert F. Roller.
