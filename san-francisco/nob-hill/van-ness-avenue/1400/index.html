  1. 1912

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. The survey dates the work to 1912–1913. Designed by Herman Barth. Reo, Packard, Chevrolet and Ford were sold here over the years.
