  1. 1912

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a tire store, auto showroom 1, 3. Dated 1912–1913.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  2. 1913

    Built for the Firestone Tire and Rubber Company, which took half the building; the rest was an auto showroom. Vehicles moved between floors by elevator rather than the usual ramp. The Firestone Tire and Rubber Company's San Francisco factory branch, 1913–1923, occupying half the building. The rest was an auto showroom, and later the whole of it was.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
