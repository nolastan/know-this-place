  2. 1913

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. The building held auto showrooms, used-car salesrooms, an auto body building shop and auto supplies and tire shops. Modern grilles now cover its large window openings.
