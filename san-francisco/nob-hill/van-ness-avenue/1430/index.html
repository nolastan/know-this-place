  1. 1912

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto showroom. New and used car dealers, tire shops, auto supplies stores and auto finance offices held the building; tire shops were here 1915–1924 and again in 1927, and auto supplies for eighteen years.
