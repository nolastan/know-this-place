999 Green Street is a condominium tower whose 112 apartments are held as 112 separate parcels — lots 029 through 140 on block 0127 — numbered 901 through 3202: five to a floor from the ninth through the thirty-first, with no thirteenth, and two more on the thirty-second. The roll lists no unit at this address below the ninth floor.

This page's parcel is lot 076, unit 1903, so the assessed value and last sale shown below are that unit's and not the building's.

112Residential units
  1. 1964

    The Summit, Joseph Eichler's last San Francisco project: a thirty-two-storey luxury tower designed by Tibor Fesces of Neill Smith Architects, with Claude Oakland as interior architect. Its cantilevered floors end in balcony edges rather than structural members and are carried on two concrete piers. Its scale on the crest of Russian Hill was so unpopular that it led to zoning reform limiting the height and bulk of later hilltop development.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
