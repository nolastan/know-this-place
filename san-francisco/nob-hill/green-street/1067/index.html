Victorian Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleSecond Empire, remodelled from an Octagon house

Built as an octagon house and remodelled into the Second Empire style in the 1880s, when a mansard roof was added. It carries little applied ornament, but nothing appears to have been stripped from it.

Early Settlement Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2025)

Year built as surveyedc. 1858

The Feusier Octagon House, San Francisco Landmark No. 36 — an eight-sided house built around 1858, one of the two octagon houses left in the city.

The city's statement on Victorian era styles dates the house twice and disagrees with itself: its narrative gives 1856 and its illustration caption gives 1858. The city prints two different street numbers for this landmark: the statement on early settlement era styles gives 1607 Green Street, while the statement on Victorian era styles gives 1067 Green Street. Both name San Francisco Landmark No. 36 and the same house. The nomination dates the house to 1857 or 1858; the assessor's 2025 roll gives 1900 for the building on this parcel.
