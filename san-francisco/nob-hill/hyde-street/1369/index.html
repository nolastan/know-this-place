  1. 1910

    The Keystone Apartments, designed by Oliver, Foulks & Kaiser — a $500,000 building advertised with a roof garden and children's play annex, a billiard room, a bowling alley, a library and a ballroom, and one of the earliest high-end large apartment buildings in the city. A metal and glass marquee marks its entrance.

    Large Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
