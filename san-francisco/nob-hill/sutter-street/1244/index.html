  1. 1934

    The Scottish Rite Temple Annex here was one of the halls the Russian community hired for large events; a Day of Russian Culture was held here in 1934.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
  2. 1966

    The Avalon Ballroom, programmed from 1966 with electronic bands and blues players including Howlin' Wolf, Muddy Waters and Lightnin' Hopkins. About a third the size of the Fillmore, with a low stage that put performers and audience close together under the light shows.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
