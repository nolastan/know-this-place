  1. 1911

    1461 Pine Street

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a public garage. The Pine Garage, a public garage for 28 years. Its brick front has been sandblasted and modern metal windows fitted.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  2. 1917

    1465 Pine Street

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto repair shop. Twenty-five feet wide, with a shallow Romanesque cornice formed of brick. It is still in use as an auto repair shop.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  3. 2010

    1465 Pine Street

    Evaluated in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor and given California Register status code 3CS — individually eligible for the California Register, under Criterion 1.

    Van Ness Auto Row Support Structures survey, adopted 2010
  4. 2010
