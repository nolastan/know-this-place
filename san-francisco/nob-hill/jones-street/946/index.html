  1. 1915

    A seven-unit apartment building of the type that reads almost exactly like a mid-block flat from the street — two bays, an off-centre recessed vestibule — but whose vestibule opens onto a shared lobby. Its fire escapes are what tell the two apart.

    Flats and Small Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
