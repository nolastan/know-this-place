  1. between 1907 and 1940

    A commercial or residential-over-commercial building on the Chestnut Street shopping street, which zoning confined to this corridor and which by 1933 held the food and clothing markets, barbers, delicatessens and doctors' offices that made trips downtown unnecessary.
