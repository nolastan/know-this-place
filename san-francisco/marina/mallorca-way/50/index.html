StyleMarina style (Mediterranean Revival)

A flats building in the Marina style — the local variant of Mediterranean Revival adapted to a contiguous row: a garage door at street level, a recessed entry stair to one side, and a band of windows across the first floor under a flat roof edged with red tile.
