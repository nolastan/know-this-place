# 2150 Beach Street

An eight-room house of 1937 in the Marina — the newest building documented on
this site, and one of only two built after the 1930s.

## The building

- Built 1937 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1937)
- 1 unit, 8 rooms, 3 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 2,593 sq ft; lot 3,571 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 2
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records this as where
Joe DiMaggio lived during his marriage to Marilyn Monroe. No dates are given
and no underlying source is cited; the marriage was in 1954. The same guide
gives [2043–2047 Taylor Street](../../../russian-hill/taylor-street/2043/) as
his childhood address.

## Permit history

Three permits are on file; one is a street-space filing at a nominal valuation.

- **2011** — reroofing ($10,500) —
  [permit 201110076346](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201110076346)
- **2005** — expand a room to infill and align with the second-floor exterior
  wall; new bathroom and finishes; relocate the stair ($40,000) —
  [permit 200510064927](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200510064927)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **23 July 1999**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $1,014,382 and improvements at $770,284 — $1,784,666 total.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, and any dates or
primary-source basis for the DiMaggio residency.
