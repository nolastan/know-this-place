Early Settlement Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2025)

Year built as surveyed1861

The McElroy Octagon House, San Francisco Landmark No. 17 — an eight-sided house built in 1861. The statement counts octagon houses among the early settlement era styles but gives them no theme of their own: of at least five once standing in San Francisco, two survive, and both are city landmarks.
