  1. 1872

    Tilton house, since moved here

    Stephen Tilton's house of 1872 stood near the centre of his lot at Union and Steiner Streets, beside the Presidio & Ferries cable train terminal, with a four-storey pump house and windmill on the property. It was later moved to 2460 Union Street.

    Robert Bardell, “The Presidio & Ferries Railroad,” The Argonaut: Journal of the San Francisco Historical Society, vol. 29, no. 2 (Winter 2018), pp. 6–33
