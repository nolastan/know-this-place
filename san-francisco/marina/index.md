# Marina

A low-lying district on filled ground along the northern waterfront, largely built after the 1915 Panama–Pacific International Exposition.

## Streets documented so far

- [Avila Street](avila-street/) — 1 building, built 1940; 1 in the Marina Corporation Residential Historic District.
- [Baker Street](baker-street/) — 2 buildings, built 1926–1928; 2 in the Baker Street Historic District.
- [Beach Street](beach-street/) — An eight-room 1937 house recorded as Joe DiMaggio's home during his marriage to Marilyn Monroe.
- [Broderick Street](broderick-street/) — 1 building, built 1925; 1 in the Cow Hollow First Bay Tradition Historic District.
- [Chestnut Street](chestnut-street/) — 4 buildings, built 1931–1950.
- [Divisadero Street](divisadero-street/) — 1 building, built 1912; 1 in the Cow Hollow First Bay Tradition Historic District.
- [Filbert Street](filbert-street/) — 2 buildings, built 1908–1913.
- [Green Street](green-street/) — 1 building, built 1916; 1 in the Pacific Heights Historic District.
- [Lombard Street](lombard-street/) — 1 building, built 1960.
- [Mallorca Way](mallorca-way/) — 1 building, built 1950; 1 in the Marina Corporation Residential Historic District.
- [Marina Boulevard](marina-boulevard/) — 1 building, built 1938.
- [Scott Street](scott-street/) — 2 buildings, built 1914–1915.
- [Union Street](union-street/) — The address to which Stephen Tilton's house of 1872, built beside the Presidio & Ferries cable terminal, was moved.
- [Webster Street](webster-street/) — 1 building, built 1900.
