Marina

A low-lying district on filled ground along the northern waterfront, largely built after the 1915 Panama–Pacific International Exposition.

Adjacent neighborhoods

The bay closes these blocks to the north and the Presidio to the west. Pacific Heights rises south up the slope from Union Street, and Russian Hill is east beyond Van Ness Avenue.
