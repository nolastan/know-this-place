  2. 1937

    A two-flat building designed by Richard Neutra with Otto Winkler — a radical departure from the Eclectic Revival buildings around it, trading historicist ornament and punched upper-storey windows for broad bands of glazing, while still reading as a side-entrance flat.

    Flats and Small Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  3. 1937

    The Schiff duplex, 1937, designed by Richard Neutra with Otto Winkler and the only one of his San Francisco buildings on level ground. Its steel and glass front, rows of steel-framed ribbon casements and two roof decks made it the closest San Francisco came to the Machine Aesthetic on a narrow city lot.

    Modern Architecture and Landscape Design Context Statement, adopted 2011
