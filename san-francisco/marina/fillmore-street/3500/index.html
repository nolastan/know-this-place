  1. 1935

    The Marina district had no public school when it was built out in the 1920s and 1930s, and this $400,000 junior high school was in the first batch of projects San Francisco put to the Public Works Administration in 1934. George W. Kelham, John Bakewell Jr. and William P. Day designed the reinforced-concrete campus in the PWA Moderne style with Art Deco motifs: academic building 1935, shops building and heating plant 1937, auditorium 1939.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  2. 1936

    Marina Middle School, designed in 1936 by George W. Kelham of Kelham and Day and paid for with New Deal federal funds.

    The PPIE and the Development of the Marina District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
