Survey findingCould be considered for individual significance
StyleDutch Colonial Revival

A Dutch Colonial Revival residence with the trademark gambrel roof, brick, sash windows and a pediment over the entry; its massing is somewhat unusual, with a central bay, and it could be considered for individual architectural significance. The document's year for this building is 1900, which is what the assessor's roll gives the parcel, so it is not an independent date.
