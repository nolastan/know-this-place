  1. 1923

    A sixteen-unit residence court begun in 1923 — each unit of four or five rooms with its own entrance, heated by steam from a central plant, stucco over a wood frame, at a projected cost of about $75,000. Ben Liebman built it to Richard Irvine's design.

    The PPIE and the Development of the Marina District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
