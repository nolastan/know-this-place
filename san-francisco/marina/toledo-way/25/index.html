  1. 1936

    A single-family house built in 1936 by Sbarboro, Detjen & Jorgensen, who advertised themselves as the “Pioneer Builders of the Marina District” and had put up a hundred and fifty buildings in it by 1929. No member of the firm was a licensed architect; they designed their own buildings and worked with architects including John Ahnden, Reuben Irvine and Laurence Ebbets.

    The PPIE and the Development of the Marina District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
