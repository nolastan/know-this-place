  1. 1923

    Barrel-front flats with a double-height entrance portal — the centred, many-faced projecting bay that became common in the Marina, Richmond and Sunset districts in the 1920s.

    Flats and Small Apartment Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  2. 1925

    One of a row of contiguous flats buildings built in 1925 by Sbarboro, Detjen & Jorgensen, who advertised themselves as the “Pioneer Builders of the Marina District” and had put up a hundred and fifty buildings in it by 1929. No member of the firm was a licensed architect; they designed their own buildings and worked with architects including John Ahnden, Reuben Irvine and Laurence Ebbets.

    The PPIE and the Development of the Marina District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
