2856 Scott Street is a three-story building of 1926 divided into six condominium units, each its own parcel — lots 034 through 039 on block 0537 — numbered 1 through 6. The assessor records the same three stories and the same 1,479 square feet against every one of the six.

This page's parcel is lot 034, unit 1, so the assessed value and last sale shown below are that unit's and not the building's.
