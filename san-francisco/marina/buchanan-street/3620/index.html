  1. 1893

    The San Francisco Gas Light Company put up its headquarters here in 1893, alongside two large gas holding tanks that stood until the Marina was built out. The tanks are gone; the office building is San Francisco City Landmark No. 58.

    The PPIE and the Development of the Marina District Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
