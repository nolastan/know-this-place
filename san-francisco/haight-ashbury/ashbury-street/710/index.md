# 710 Ashbury Street

A two-unit building of 1900 near the corner of Ashbury and Waller, identified
by published guides to notable residences as the house the Grateful Dead
shared in the second half of the 1960s.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 2 units, 10 rooms, 2 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwellings – Apt 4
  units or less"
- Building area 2,680 sq ft; lot 2,652 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 5
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — carries a historic survey result but no
  formal evaluation. Not a designated landmark; in no historic district

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Jerry Garcia and
the Grateful Dead as sharing this house from 1965 to 1968. The claim is
second-hand; the guide gives no underlying source for the dates.

## Permit history

Seven permits are on file with the Department of Building Inspection,
1981–2006. Most expired without being completed, including a 1992 proposal to
convert the front of the ground-floor apartment into a garage.

- **2006** — reroofing ($13,000), expired —
  [permit 200604249674](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200604249674)
- **1997** — two renewals of the lapsed 1981, 1992 and 1993 permits —
  [9717669](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9717669),
  [9717593](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9717593)
- **1993** — replace a rotten wood stair from the third floor to the ground
  with folding metal ($800), expired —
  [permit 9304327](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9304327);
  revised the same year to delete a door —
  [permit 9313020](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9313020)
- **1992** — convert the front of the ground-floor apartment to a garage and
  remodel the studio ($35,000, revised to $50,000), expired —
  [permit 9214600](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9214600)
- **1981** — work of $1,000, completed 1983; no description recorded —
  [permit 8107226](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8107226)

## Value

The 2025 roll assesses the land at $41,554 and improvements at $165,817 —
$207,371 in total. No sale appears on the current roll, and the assessed base
is consistent with a long-held Proposition 13 base year rather than with
present-day market value.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, and any primary-source
confirmation of the 1965–1968 residency.
