  1. 1966

    The poster designers of Mouse Studios lived here, across the street from the Grateful Dead's house. In spring 1967 the building also held the temporary offices of the Kiva, a community space project run by a tribal council drawn from the Oracle, the Diggers, the Straight Theater and the Family Dog.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
