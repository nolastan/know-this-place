One of three addresses put forward for Janis Joplin, and the one known as “the pink house”.

Three addresses, one tenancy

The same guide places Joplin at 122 Lyon Street from June 1967 to April 1968, citing her driver's licence, and records a competing claim for 112 Lyon Street. Three buildings are in circulation for one period of one person's life; this page does not adjudicate between them.
