# 635 Ashbury Street

An eight-unit apartment building of 1905, four storeys on a 2,500-square-foot
lot — one of three Haight addresses that published guides attach to Janis
Joplin.

## The building

- Built 1905 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1905)
- 8 units, 30 rooms, 8 baths, 4 stories; assessor class "Apartment 5 to 14
  Units"
- Building area 7,900 sq ft; lot 2,500 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 5
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — carries a historic survey result but no
  formal evaluation. Not a designated landmark; in no historic district

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences names this as a Janis
Joplin address, describing it as "the pink house". No dates are given. The same
guide separately places Joplin at 122 Lyon Street from June 1967 to April 1968,
and records a competing claim for 112 Lyon Street — three addresses for one
tenancy, none of them documented against a primary source.

occupancy permits carrying a nominal $1 valuation and are omitted below. The
remainder are apartment-by-apartment kitchen and bath rehabilitations, filed in

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **26 August 2022**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $2,465,748 and improvements at $1,643,832 — $4,109,580 total.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, when the building was divided
into eight apartments, and any primary-source basis for the Joplin claim.
