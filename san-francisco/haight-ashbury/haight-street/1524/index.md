# 1524–1528 Haight Street

Flats over a ground-floor store, on the block of Haight between Ashbury and
Clayton. The parcel runs 1524 to 1528 and sits inside the Haight Ashbury
Counterculture Historic District.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 3 units, 16 rooms, 3 baths; assessor class "Flat & Store 4 units or less"
- Building area 7,940 sq ft; lot 3,781 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning: Haight Street Neighborhood Commercial District; Supervisorial
  District 5
- The assessor reports 0 stories — a gap in the record, not a measurement. A
  1988 permit legalised fourth-floor occupancy, so the building has at least
  four levels

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A — a historical resource, by virtue of a survey result and
  district membership
- Haight Ashbury Counterculture Historic District, period of significance
  **1966–1975**; **eligible** for the California Register, not listed
- Not on the National Register; not an Article 10 or 11 district, so the
  parcel carries no local landmark protection

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences lists this as a Jimi
Hendrix address, then qualifies its own claim: newer research, it says,
indicates a stay of only a few months, if any. The association is recorded here
as disputed and nothing more.

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **17 July 2001**. The 2025 roll
totals $2,289,802, but reports land and improvements at the identical figure of
$1,144,901 each — the total is usable, the split is not.
