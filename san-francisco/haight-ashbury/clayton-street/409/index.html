  1. 1967

    The Haight Ashbury Free Clinic leased a second building here for psychiatric services and a publishing arm. The ground floor became Happening House, and from 1967 the Ecumenical Ministry to the Haight — the 409 House, described as the country's first drop-in centre — worked from the first floor. A meeting here in October 1967 produced the proposal for the Death of the Hippie ceremony.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
