  1. 1904

    Built.

  2. 1965

    In Gear, one of the earliest shops selling clothes to hippies.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
  3. 1967

    The Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic opened here in 1967, offering substance-abuse and medical services on a model of non-judgemental volunteer care that was adopted nationally.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  4. June 7, 1967

    The Haight Ashbury Free Clinic opened in a second-floor suite here on 7 June 1967, after many prospective landlords had turned it down. Around 12,000 patients used it in the first month, for infections, pneumonia and venereal disease as well as drugs. Happening House shared the space at the outset.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
