  2. 1954

    Home of Frances R. Miller, who secured the 1954 charter for the local chapter of the Association for the Study of Negro Life and History that became the San Francisco African American Historical and Cultural Society; the chapter most likely operated from this house. Identified in 2024 as eligible for local landmark, California Register or National Register designation.
