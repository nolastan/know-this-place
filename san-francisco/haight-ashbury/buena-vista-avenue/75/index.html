  2. 1931

    Designed by Albert H. Larsen, built as infill on what was left of a lot once occupied by the stables and garage buildings of the former Buena Vista Sanitarium. Apartment buildings above neighbourhood scale are rare in the Haight Ashbury, and this is one of them.
