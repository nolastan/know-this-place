Two Spreckels houses, one city

The same SF Planning dataset that names this the Floyd Spreckels Mansion names 2080 Washington Street the Spreckels Residence. They are different buildings in different neighbourhoods with different protections: 2080 Washington is an Article 10 individual landmark, while this parcel carries Category A status from a survey result alone and no local landmark protection. What links the two names has not been established here.
