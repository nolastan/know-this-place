# 737 Buena Vista Avenue West

A nineteen-room house of about 1900 on the west side of Buena Vista Park.
SF Planning's historic-resource survey records it under a name — the Floyd
Spreckels Mansion — which most parcels in the city do not have.

## The building

- Built 1900 per the assessor; SF Planning records no build year
- 1 unit, 19 rooms, 5 baths, 3 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 8,407 sq ft; lot 7,496 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 8
- Named in the SF Planning survey as the **Floyd Spreckels Mansion**
- Historic status: CEQA Category A — a historical resource on the strength of
  the survey result. Not a designated landmark; in no historic district

This is not the Spreckels house at [2080 Washington
Street](../../../pacific-heights/washington-street/2080/), which the same
Planning dataset names the Spreckels Residence and which is an Article 10
individual landmark.

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records both Jack London
and Danny Glover as having lived here at different times. Neither claim carries
dates or an underlying source in that guide. London was born at 601 Third
Street and died in 1916.

## Permit history

**No building permits are on file.** The DBI dataset reaches back to the
mid-1970s and returns nothing for this address — one of only two addresses
documented on this site with an empty permit record.

## Value

The most recent secured roll carrying this parcel is **2018**, not 2025 — the
dataset gives no reason. On that roll the land was assessed at $4,541,943 and
improvements at $3,696,930, $8,238,873 total, following a sale recorded on
**20 October 2014**. The figures are seven years stale.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder; the build year (assessor and Planning disagree, one
saying 1900 and the other nothing at all); who Floyd Spreckels was and what
connects the name to this house; why the parcel drops off the assessor roll
after 2018; and dates for either reported resident.
