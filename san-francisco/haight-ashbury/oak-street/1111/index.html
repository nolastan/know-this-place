Early Settlement Era Styles Historic Context Statement (2025)

StyleGothic Revival / Carpenter Gothic
Year built as surveyedc. 1850

The Abner Phelps House, San Francisco Landmark No. 32 — a premier example of Carpenter Gothic, and generally considered the oldest residence in San Francisco. Built in a semi-rural setting before the surrounding city; by some accounts bought in New Orleans in 1850, shipped in sections and re-erected here around 1850-1851.
