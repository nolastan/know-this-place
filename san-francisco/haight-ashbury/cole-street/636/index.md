# 636–638 Cole Street

A two-flat building of 1903. One parcel carrying both street numbers, a block
south of Haight Street.

## The building

- Built 1903 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1903)
- 2 flats, 12 rooms, 2 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Flats & Duplex"
- Building area 3,000 sq ft; lot 3,063 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 5
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district
- The parcel spans 636 and 638 Cole Street; permits are filed against both
  numbers together

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Charles Manson
and members of his group as living at this address during 1967, arriving in the
Summer of Love. The guide cites no underlying source.

## Permit history

Seven permits are on file, 1994–2018. The 1996 pair is the notable sequence:
the building's front parapet was removed as an emergency falling hazard in
April, and a permit to repair and reinforce it, filed a month later, expired
without being acted on.

- **2018** — reroofing ($12,000) —
  [permit 201807195025](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201807195025)
- **2010** — comply with physical inspection report CC-5817: remodel both
  flat kitchens, repair the rear stairs under 50 percent, upgrade kitchen
  lighting ($23,000 revised) —
  [permit 201009090503](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201009090503)
- **2009** — repair holes in the garage ceiling with fire-rated materials,
  per notice of violation 200908781 ($800) —
  [permit 200906150404](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200906150404)
- **1999** — repair wall covering and backing per a termite report ($2,025) —
  [permit 9918838](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9918838)
- **1996** — repair the parapet wall and reinforce the structure ($2,500),
  expired —
  [permit 9608362](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9608362)
- **1996** — emergency removal of the front parapet, a falling hazard
  ($5,000) —
  [permit 9606621](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9606621)
- **1994** — repair lower siding, framing bases and perimeter foundation
  ($8,850) —
  [permit 9419775](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9419775)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **2 November 1999**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $699,677 and improvements at $565,707 — $1,265,384 total.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, whether the front parapet
removed in 1996 was ever rebuilt, and any primary-source basis for the 1967
residency.
