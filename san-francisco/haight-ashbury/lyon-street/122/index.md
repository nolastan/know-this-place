# 122 Lyon Street

Five apartments over three storeys on the block of Lyon between Page and Oak,
facing the Panhandle. The parcel falls inside two overlapping historic
districts.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 5 units, 14 rooms, 5 baths, 3 stories; assessor class "Apartment 5 to 14
  Units"
- Building area 3,814 sq ft; lot 2,278 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 5

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A — a historical resource, from a survey result and district
  membership
- Inside **two** overlapping districts: Buena Vista North and Panhandle, both
  with a period of significance of 1870–1915
- Both are **eligible** for the California Register, not listed; neither is a
  National Register or Article 10 or 11 district, so the parcel carries no
  local landmark protection

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences places Janis Joplin here
from June 1967 to April 1968, and says the dates come from her driver's
licence. The same guide records competing claims for
[112 Lyon Street](../112/) and
[635 Ashbury Street](../../ashbury-street/635/). The apartment number the guide
gives is not repeated here.

## Permit history

Three permits are on file, 2000–2023 — two roofs and a stair rail. The building
has no recorded alteration history before 2000.

- **2023** — reroofing ($12,540) —
  [permit 202306270960](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=202306270960)
- **2003** — raise the outer top rail on the back stairs to 36 inches above
  the treads ($2,500) —
  [permit 200301275950](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200301275950)
- **2000** — reroofing —
  [permit 200010274315](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200010274315)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **11 February 2014**. The 2025
roll assesses land at $1,250,375 and improvements at $833,583 — $2,083,958
total.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, when the building was divided
into five apartments, and any primary-source basis for the Joplin dates beyond
the guide's account of her driver's licence.
