# 112 Lyon Street

A single-family house of 1900, five doors down from 122 Lyon and inside the
same two historic districts. Eleven rooms, one bath, and a rear addition
attempted three times and never finished.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 1 unit, 11 rooms, 1 bath, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 2,546 sq ft; lot 2,413 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-3; Supervisorial District 5

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A — a historical resource, from a survey result and district
  membership
- Inside both the Buena Vista North and Panhandle historic districts, each
  with a period of significance of 1870–1915
- Both are **eligible** for the California Register, not listed; neither is an
  Article 10 or 11 district, so the parcel carries no local landmark
  protection

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences gives this address as an
alternative to its own main claim for [122 Lyon Street](../122/), saying only
that some accounts place Janis Joplin here instead during the 1960s. It is the
weaker of the two claims in that source's own telling.

## Permit history

Five permits are on file, 1987–1991, and nothing since. Three of the five are
successive attempts at the same horizontal addition — enclosing part of the
rear-yard deck — filed in August 1990, April 1991 and July 1991. All three
expired.

- **1991** — horizontal addition, third filing —
  [permit 9113410](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9113410)
- **1991** — horizontal addition, second filing ($11,147) —
  [permit 9106481](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9106481)
- **1990** — horizontal addition, enclosing part of the rear-yard deck
  ($45,000) —
  [permit 9016528S](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=9016528S)
- **1988** — remove a kitchen sink and cap off the water supply ($1,000),
  complete —
  [permit 8805636](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8805636)
- **1987** — replace the front stairs and tear out fungus-damaged wood
  ($10,000), complete —
  [permit 8706305](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8706305)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **31 May 1990** — the oldest
recorded sale among the addresses documented here, and the reason the assessed
base sits well below neighbouring parcels. The 2025 roll assesses land at
$533,532 and improvements at $321,951, $855,483 total.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, whether the rear addition was
ever built without a permit, and any basis for the Joplin claim.
