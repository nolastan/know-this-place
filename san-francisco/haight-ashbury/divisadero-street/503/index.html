  1. 1919

    A Stick/Eastlake house of the 1890s. Between 1905 and 1919 a one-storey commercial space was built out into its front-yard setback and the single-family house was divided into flats; the new shop held a tailor's in 1919. By 1950 every building on the block carried a shop of the same kind.
