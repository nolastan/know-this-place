  1. 1896

    Built as Divisadero Hall, a meeting place for the Olympus Parlor of the Native Sons of the Golden West and, over the years, for many other fraternal and social groups. One of the few purpose-built fraternal lodges and social halls in the city to predate the 1906 earthquake and survive it.

    Citywide Historic Context Statement: Clubs and Social Halls (1848–1989), 2026
  2. 1953

    A Chekhov festival was staged here in 1953, jointly sponsored by the Russian American Society and the American Russian Institute.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
