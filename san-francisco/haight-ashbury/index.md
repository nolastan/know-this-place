# Haight Ashbury

The neighborhood at the east end of Golden Gate Park, at the centre of San Francisco's 1960s counterculture. Its housing is largely late-Victorian and Edwardian, and several blocks fall inside California Register historic districts.

## Streets documented so far

- [Ashbury Street](ashbury-street/) — Two buildings — the house guides give the Grateful Dead, and one of three Janis Joplin addresses.
- [Ashbury Terrace](ashbury-terrace/) — 1 building, built 1912.

- [Buena Vista Avenue](buena-vista-avenue/) — 1 building, built 1931.

## Adjacent neighborhoods

Golden Gate Park closes these blocks to the west and the Panhandle runs
along the north. [Hayes Valley](../hayes-valley/) is east beyond Divisadero
Street, [the Castro](../castro/) south-east across Buena Vista Park,
[Corbett Heights](../corbett-heights/) south up Clayton and Ashbury Streets,
and [the Inner Sunset](../inner-sunset/) south-west over Mount Sutro.
