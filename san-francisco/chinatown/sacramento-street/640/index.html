  1. 1907

    Built with entrances on both Commercial and Sacramento Streets. The Commercial Street front has a ground floor of four window bays divided by pilasters, with five irregularly spaced windows on each of the two floors above. It was one of two buildings on its block corner kept and refurbished when the rest were cleared for a tower.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, 505 Montgomery Street: [Draft] Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 82.463E, 1983.
