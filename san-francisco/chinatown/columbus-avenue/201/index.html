  1. 1913

    The photography studio of J. B. Monaco, one of at least six Italian-owned studios on the 1913 Sanborn map, occupied the upper floor here; the city's survey records the building's large windows and skylights as still visible.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1967

    Mr. Bing's traces its business here to 1967; the city's survey lists it among North Beach's legacy bars, restaurants and businesses, a concentration it records as the highest in the city.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
