  1. 1913

    The Drexler–Colombo Building, a reinforced concrete building with stores on the ground floor and offices above, to the design of the Reid Brothers.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as an individually listed resource. San Francisco City Landmark No. 237. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places (07001469).
