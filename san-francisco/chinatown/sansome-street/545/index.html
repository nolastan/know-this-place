  2. 1930

    A nine-storey reinforced-concrete building with a penthouse went up here in 1930, holding about 56,600 square feet of offices and used at one time for printing and publishing. It stood empty from November 1987, and the 1991 proposal to replace it recorded San Francisco Heritage's 1979 rating of "C" — contextual importance — with no rating under the Downtown Plan.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Transamerica III: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 89.489E, 1991.
  3. 1975

    A single-storey retail building of about 2,000 square feet was built on the corner of Washington and Sansome Streets in 1975.

    San Francisco Department of City Planning, Transamerica III: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 89.489E, 1991.
