# Chinatown

The oldest Chinese community in North America, on the slopes above Portsmouth Square, extending east into the brick blocks of Jackson Square.

## Streets documented so far

- [Jackson Street](jackson-street/) — The Presidio & Ferries Railroad's horse car barn, in the Jackson Square Historic District.
- [Stockton Street](stockton-street/) — 1 building, built 1911; 1 in the Chinatown Historic District.
