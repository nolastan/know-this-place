  1. 1909

    François Louis Alfred Pioche, whose 1867 subdivision with Robinson laid out much of Corbett Heights, kept a mansard-roofed house at 806 Stockton Street until his death in 1872. The alley behind it was renamed from Pioche Alley to Pagoda Place in 1909.

    Corbett Heights Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
