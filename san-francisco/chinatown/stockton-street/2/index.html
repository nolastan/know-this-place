Year built as surveyed1907

Built in 1907 to designs by Albert Pissis in the Beaux Arts style, with terra cotta cladding, and remodelled twice since — in 1937 and again in 1990. Only the upper terra cotta cladding survives of the original design, which leaves it ineligible as an individual resource though it stands within the Article 11 Kearny-Market-Mason-Sutter Conservation District.
