  1. 1913

    The Children's Day Home, run by the Sisters of the Holy Family as a place where working mothers could leave their children, operated from a two-story and basement building here; the Sanborn maps of 1913 and 1949 both show it in the same building.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1970s

    Designed by Roslyn Lindheim, a leading figure in the design of modern healthcare buildings and founder of Planetree, whose work sought healthier and more humane settings for people in institutional care. Built as the On Lok Senior Day Care Center, to house elderly people who would otherwise have been institutionalised.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
