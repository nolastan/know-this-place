  1. 1913

    The Knights of Pythias Hall stood here on the 1913 Sanborn map; by 1949 it had become the Cathay Hall of the American Legion, a Chinese organisation.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1930s

    The Gran Oriente Filipino lodge, the most prominent Filipino fraternal organisation of the 1930s, met here from that decade until after the Second World War. Fire insurance maps show the building as a Knights of Pythias hall in 1913 and as the Cathay American Legion Post in 1948; it now houses the Chinese Baptist Church.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
