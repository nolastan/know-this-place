Year built as surveyed1972

The Transamerica Pyramid, designed by William L. Pereira & Associates with Gin Wong as design architect, unveiled in 1969 and completed in 1972. A touchstone of the anti-Manhattanization argument and now read as one of the city's defining examples of Corporate Late Modernism, with highly repetitive fenestration and precast panel cladding. Redwood Park, the landscape designed by Anthony Guzzardo beside the Transamerica Pyramid, offered as a concession for approval of the tower and an early example of the trade of public open space for downtown height and bulk.

The Modern design context statement gives the Transamerica Pyramid three dates — designed 1969, and completed in 1971 or 1972. Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants.
