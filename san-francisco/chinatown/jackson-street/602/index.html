  1. 1919

    The jazz composer Jelly Roll Morton played a residency at the Dixie Hotel Bar and Cafe here in early 1919.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  2. 1950s

    Three Manilatown businesses traded from this corner in the late 1940s and 1950s — Mango's Smoke Shop, the Bagong Sikat Restaurant and the Manila Café — among the row of Filipino cafés, barber shops, pool halls and smoke shops that ran along Kearny Street from Pine to Pacific.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
