Earlier record

  1. Apr 18, 1906

    Cable service on the Presidio & Ferries ended when the earthquake and fire destroyed the railroad's infrastructure.

    The Argonaut, vol. 29 no. 2, Winter 2018
  2. 1882

    The Presidio & Ferries Railroad ran a horse car section along Washington and Jackson Streets, connecting the ferry terminals to its cable railway terminal. Its horse cars were stabled in the car barn here.

    The Argonaut, vol. 29 no. 2, Winter 2018
