  1. 1908

    Built as the Seattle Saloon and Dance Hall and renamed the Dash in 1908 when its ownership changed, this was one of the largest dance halls raised on the Barbary Coast after the 1906 earthquake and is identified as the only known surviving Barbary Coast establishment that featured female impersonators.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
