  1. 1920

    Cole Hardware traces its business here to 1920s; the city's survey lists it among North Beach's legacy bars, restaurants and businesses, a concentration it records as the highest in the city.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1932

    Built as a commercial building for Rossi Market building, to the design of Peter D. Canali, one of the modern architects the city's survey identifies as working in North Beach.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1932

    Built as a supermarket for Rossi's Market, in the Streamline Moderne style, to the design of Peter D. Canali.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
