  1. 1912

    Fugazi Hall, formally the Casa Coloniale Italiana John F. Fugazi — a steel-framed building with a theater and rooms for Italian community organisations, commissioned by the banker John F. Fugazi and designed by Italo Zanolini.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. 1968

    Listed in the 1968 citywide survey of San Francisco's architectural heritage as Fugazi Building.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. 1975

    Club Fugazi, a 400-seat theatre to which the revue Beach Blanket Babylon moved in 1975 and where it was still running in 2015 - reportedly the longest-running musical revue in the country. Identified as potentially eligible for landmark designation.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  4. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as an individually listed resource.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
