Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StyleBrutalism
Architect as surveyedClement Chen & Associates with T.Y. Lin, Chen-chi Kwan and John Carl Warnecke
Year built as surveyed1971

A hotel tower at Portsmouth Square, opened as a Holiday Inn, by Clement Chen & Associates with structural engineer T.Y. Lin, Chen-chi Kwan and John Carl Warnecke. Held up as the clearest Brutalist high-rise downtown: board-formed concrete carrying the grain of its formwork, a broad base for the lobby, restaurant and Chinese Cultural Center, and a narrow shaft of hotel rooms. Found eligible for the California Register.

Not yet documented: the developer, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale. The city's Modern & Postmodern styles context statement dates this building 1971; the assessor's roll records it as built 1970. Submit an update
