  1. 1905

    Built.

  2. 1925

    The Grand Lodge Caballeros de Dimas-Alang, a Filipino mutual aid society founded in San Francisco in 1921 and named for the pen name of José Rizal, was listed here in 1925.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
  3. 1950s

    A Filipino employment agency advertising “competent domestic and industrial help” traded here in the Manilatown of the late 1940s and 1950s.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
  4. 1952

    Opus One operated here between 1952 and 1958, one of the North Beach bars and restaurants the city's survey identifies as significant in San Francisco's LGBTQ history.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  5. June 18, 1964

    Photographed, showing the Columbus Tower.

    San Francisco Subjects Photograph Collection (SFP 162), San Francisco Public Library
  6. 1982

    Recorded in the city's 1982 survey of North Beach, adopted by the Board of Supervisors in 1999, as an individually listed resource. San Francisco City Landmark No. 33.

    North Beach Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
