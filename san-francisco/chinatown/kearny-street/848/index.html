  1. 1907

    The International Hotel was built in 1907 and became a fixture of Manilatown, popular with Asian immigrants and particularly with Filipino bachelors. Its tenants were evicted after a decade-long campaign and a police standoff in August 1978, and the building was demolished in 1981. The site stayed empty until 2004, when a new I-Hotel of 105 senior apartments opened, with a community centre and a historical display on the ground floor. The Kearny Street Workshop was founded in the old hotel's basement in 1972 and opened a gallery on the Jackson Street side in 1974.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
  2. 1979

    The International Hotel was demolished, and the smaller Victory Hotel on the parcel beside it went at the same time. The site was still vacant in 1996.
