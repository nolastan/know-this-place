A Home Savings & Loan Association branch bank by Millard Sheets and his collaborative design studio, with a sculptural fountain and a mosaic mural, in the 'Home Savings Style' of New Formalism - monumental geometric massing, white travertine, integrated artworks and gold gilding. Most former Home Savings branches are now Chase Bank.
