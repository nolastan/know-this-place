  1. 1943

    The Graysons clothing store here was fitted out to a design by Victor Gruen and Elsie Krummeck, the partnership whose storefront prototype Pittco distributed free to merchants and whose senior partner went on to invent the shopping mall; it was photographed about 1943, soon after completion. The store has since been divided into four shops, and its generous side show windows survive.

    Neighborhood Commercial Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
