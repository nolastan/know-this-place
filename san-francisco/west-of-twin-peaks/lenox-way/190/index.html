  2. 1940

    The West Portal branch library was designed by Frederick H. Meyer in the same Classical Revival–Mission Revival manner as its Bernal Heights twin, built by the Works Progress Administration for $106,359 and completed in 1940 with an adjoining playground. Its main reading room keeps a decorative stencilled ceiling frieze, painted in 1939 under the Federal Art Project.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
