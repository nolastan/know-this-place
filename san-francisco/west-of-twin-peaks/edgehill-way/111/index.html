A shingled Third Bay Tradition residence by Backen Arrigoni & Ross, its round side adapted to a curved corner site. The J-plan building has projecting shed-roofed glass bays hung like saddlebags, a common feature of Third Bay Tradition and Sea Ranch architecture.
