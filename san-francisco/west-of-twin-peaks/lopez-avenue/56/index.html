A Colonial Revival residence of 1928 with a side-gabled roof, a raised and accentuated entry, sash windows and overall facade symmetry, standing within the California Register eligible Forest Hill Historic District. Its detailing is intact but standard, which suits it better as a district contributor than as an individually significant resource.
