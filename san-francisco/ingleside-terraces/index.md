# Ingleside Terraces

A curvilinear residence park platted in 1912 on the site of the former
Ingleside Racetrack, developed by Joseph Leonard's Urban Realty Improvement
Company around the loop of Urbano Drive and the Sundial Park at Entrada
Court.
