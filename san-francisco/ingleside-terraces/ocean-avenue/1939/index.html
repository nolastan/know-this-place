  1. 1924

    Built in 1924 with three storefronts, each behind a deeply recessed zigzag arcade vestibule. The wood-sash transom and cornice survive largely intact and one storefront keeps high integrity; the other two were substantially renovated about the 1980s and their vestibules partly filled in.

    Neighborhood Commercial Buildings Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
