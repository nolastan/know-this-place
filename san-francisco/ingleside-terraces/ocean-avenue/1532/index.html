Earlier record

  1. 1917

    One of the long, multiple-storefront buildings (1524, 1532 and 1700–1720 Ocean Avenue) built beside the Westwood Park residential development as its first houses were occupied in 1917, in a Craftsman style with Colonial and Tudor elements.
