StyleStreamline Moderne
Architect as surveyedHarold Stoner
Year built as surveyed1941
Address as surveyed2501-2515 Ocean Avenue

The Lakeside Medical Center, designed by Harold Stoner in 1941 — a futuristic Streamline Moderne commercial building with glass blocks, a vertically projecting tower, curved and stepped surfaces, coping and a sign of individual letters. A rare intact example of the style, eligible for individual consideration under Criterion C/3.
