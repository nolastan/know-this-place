  1. 1968

    San Francisco State University, where the 1968-69 student strike produced the country's first College of Ethnic Studies and its Black Studies programme. Identified in 2024 as eligible for designation, with the caveat the College no longer occupies its original building, which it says should be identified and examined.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  2. 1976

    San Francisco State University's Student Union hosted the first annual gay and lesbian Alcoholics Anonymous conference, Living Sober, in 1976.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015

Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StyleBrutalism
Architect as surveyedPaffard Keatinge-Clay
Year built as surveyed1969-1975

The San Francisco State University Student Center by Paffard Keatinge-Clay, a unique local expression of Brutalism: massive enamel panel pivot doors, concrete downspouts and stairways, a projecting pyramid housing a study area, and projecting stadium seating. A later roof-deck addition sits awkwardly, but the original design remains legible and the building appears eligible.
