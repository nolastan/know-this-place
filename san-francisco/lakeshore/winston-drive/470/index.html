  1. 1982–2012

    Inadequate environmental controls in the building caused mold problems in the collection.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 1, Summer 2020
  2. 1982

    The Sutro Library moved to Winston Drive, into two older buildings cobbled together, and was dedicated as its new home.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 1, Summer 2020
  3. 2012

    The Sutro Library's collection moved out to the J. Paul Leonard Library on the university's main campus, which was completed in July 2012.

    The Argonaut, vol. 31 no. 1, Summer 2020
