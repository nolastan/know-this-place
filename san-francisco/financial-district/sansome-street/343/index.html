Public art

  • Four Seasons — Joan Brown
    Tile; roof garden; artwork and open space are accessible from 9-5.
    The tiled obelisk stands at the center of the 15th-floor roof terrace and was funded through San Francisco's 1% for Art program combined with the POPOS requirement.
  • L'Octagon — Pol Bury
    Stainless steel, marble; Sacramento St. lobby; artwork and open space are accessible from 9-5.
    Belgian sculptor Pol Bury (April 26, 1922 - September 28, 2005) began his career as a painter associated with the Jeune Peinture Belge and CoBrA groups before becoming known for kinetic sculpture.
