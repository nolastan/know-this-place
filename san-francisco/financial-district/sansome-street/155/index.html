  1. 1930

    Miller and Pflueger built the office tower here alongside their conversion of the old U.S. Treasury building into the Pacific Coast Stock Exchange — a traditional three-part vertical composition with a giant order at the capital, its limited ornament a blend of classical and Moderne focused in a sculptural group over the door.
