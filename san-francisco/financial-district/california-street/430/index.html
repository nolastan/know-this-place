Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StyleModernism
Architect as surveyedAnshen & Allen
Year built as surveyed1968

A Modernist office tower added behind the 1906-08 Bank of California by Anshen & Allen, its concrete spandrels echoing the fluting of the older bank's columns. Cited as an early example of a sensitive Modernist addition to a historic building.
