  1. 1909

    The Alaska Commercial Building stood here from 1909 until the 1970s — designed three years earlier by Henry H. Meyers and Clarence Ward and built at a cost of about $500,000. Fourteen storeys on an L-shaped plan with its long side to Sansome Street, steel and reinforced concrete, its California, Sansome and Halleck Street elevations faced in granite and terra cotta and carved with walruses and marine motifs for the Alaska Commercial Company, whose offices it held.

    Bank of Tokyo of California Building, California and Sansome Streets: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1975
  2. 1975

    By 1975 the Alaska Commercial Building stood vacant on the northeast corner of California and Sansome, with an eighty-space surface parking lot beside it, and both were cleared for the Bank of Tokyo of California's twenty-three-storey tower.

    Bank of Tokyo of California Building, California and Sansome Streets: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1975
