Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

StyleClassical Revival
Year built as surveyed1906-1908

The Bank of California built 1906-1908 to designs by Bliss & Faville — a three-storey Classical temple with a large colonnade and Corinthian ornamentation, and a prime example of the Classical Revival style.
