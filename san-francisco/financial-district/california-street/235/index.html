The John Hancock Building, also known as the Mutual Life Insurance Company or Industrial Indemnity Building, designed by Chuck Bassett of Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. Modernist overall, it carries features later associated with New Formalism: a tripartite configuration, an arched podium base and polished granite cladding.

Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. The city's Modern & Postmodern styles context statement dates this building 1959; the assessor's roll records it as built 1958. Submit an update
