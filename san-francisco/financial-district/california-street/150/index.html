Public art

  • Arbor Arch — Ed Carpenter
    Aluminum pipe, stainless steel cables and hardware, aluminum channel, dichroic glass; rooftop terrace of the sixth floor; artwork available from 9:00 a.m to 6:00 p.m.
    Installed 2000. The sculpture, measuring 54 by 28 by 19 feet, was commissioned to disguise a large air vent and diesel exhaust stack emerging into the sixth-floor roof garden while adding to the garden's ambience.
