Public art

  • Hawaiian — Gwynn Murill
    Bronze; Public right-of-way; open space and artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 2002. Gwynn Murrill (born 1942), an American sculptor, made this bronze work under San Francisco's 1% for Art program; Wikipedia captions the same sculpture 'The Hawaiians (Mother and Child).' The city's inventory credits Gwynn Murill; the source cited here gives Gwynn Murrill.
