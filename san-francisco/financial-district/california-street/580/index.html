  1. 1982

    The four-storey Fireman's Fund Insurance Building stood on this rectangle opposite the Bank of America headquarters, with frontages of 124 feet on California Street and 128 feet on Kearny and Spring. Heritage's downtown survey listed it but left it unrated, because it did not rate anything built after 1945.

StylePostmodernism
Architect as surveyedJohnson & Burgee
Year built as surveyed1984

A downtown tower by Johnson & Burgee capped with a mirror-glass faux mansard and a ring of draped figures - the 'corporate goddesses' commissioned from artist Muriel Castanis - and one of the most extreme of the articulated tops that followed the 1985 Downtown Plan.

The statement dates this tower 1986 on p. 43 and 1984 on pp. 128-129. Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. Submit an update
