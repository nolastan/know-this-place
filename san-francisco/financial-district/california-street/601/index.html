The International Building by Anshen & Allen, named as the firm's iconic Modernist work, characterised by light and glass.

The Modern design context statement prints the International Building's address as 601 California Street in two places and 610 California Street in a third, and dates it variously 1957, 1960, 1961 and 1962. Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. Submit an update
