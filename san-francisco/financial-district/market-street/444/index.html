Why the number changed

The tower opened as Shaklee Terraces and was later renumbered from 444 Market Street to One Front Street, because four is an unlucky number in several Asian cultures. Its facade closely follows Emil Fahrenkamp's Shell-Haus, built in Berlin in 1931. The Shaklee Corporation left for Pleasanton in 1999.

Shaklee Terrace, also known as 444 Market Street, by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill: a smooth, slick facade plane, a highly repeating pattern of fenestration and a uniquely undulating facade profile. Lobby interiors have been altered but the exterior is intact, and the statement finds it eligible under Criterion C/3 and notable within SOM's portfolio.
