  1. 1979

    The Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage rated the Shasta Hotel "C" in the 1979 downtown survey it published as Splendid Survivors — the third of its four grades, for buildings that count for their scale and materials and for the way they hold a streetfront together. The Department of City Planning's 1976 Architectural Inventory did not rate it.
