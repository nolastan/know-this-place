  2. 1908

    A six-storey steel and concrete building of 1908, 88 feet tall, remade by successive alterations from 1951 and clad in white masonry tile with red-painted window frames. It served as offices and shops and stood empty from about 1997.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 185 Post Street: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 2000.272E, 2001.
