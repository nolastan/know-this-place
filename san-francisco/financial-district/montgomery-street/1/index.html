  1. Sep 1871

    Men who had come with Japan's contribution to the Mechanics' Institute fair of 1871 stayed in San Francisco as permanent residents and set up a mercantile firm, leasing a store in the Masonic Temple at 1 Montgomery Street.

    The Argonaut, vol. 30 no. 1, Summer 2019
  2. Sep 1871

    The firm would deal in the staple products of Japan — tea, coal, flax and wax — rather than knick-knacks, and had the means to do business on a large scale, the Bulletin reported.

    The Argonaut, vol. 30 no. 1, Summer 2019
  3. Jul 2015

    Ground floor and mezzanine — heating and ventilation remodelled with new duct distribution, three fan coil units, two rooftop condensing units and a new mechanical room on the mezzanine, with lighting revisions and structural support for the plant.

  4. Feb 2020

    Selective interior demolition of finishes and partitions in the basement and on the second floor, with the structure, the exterior and the historic features to remain.

    Revision to the February 2020 permit, to remove the vaults in the basement.

Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

StylePostmodernism
Architect as surveyedSkidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM)
Year built as surveyed1982

A roof terrace with historicist columns and pergolas added by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill atop the historic bank here, as part of the Crocker Tower and Galleria project — the point at which the firm’s design shifts fully into Postmodernism.

Not yet documented: the architect and builder of the present building; and whether the Masonic Temple whose store the Japanese firm leased in 1871 stood on this parcel — street numbers changed and the block was rebuilt after 1906, and nothing consulted here establishes the correspondence.
