  1. September 1871

    Japanese mercantile firm takes a store

    Men who had come with Japan's contribution to the Mechanics' Institute fair of 1871 stayed in San Francisco as permanent residents and set up a mercantile firm, leasing a store in the Masonic Temple at 1 Montgomery Street.

    Taryn Edwards, “Before the Midwinter Fair: The Mechanics' Institute's ‘Pacific Rim' Industrial Exhibitions of 1869 and 1871,” The Argonaut: Journal of the San Francisco Historical Society, vol. 30, no. 1 (Summer 2019), pp. 8–23
  2. September 1871

    What the firm would trade in

    The firm would deal in the staple products of Japan — tea, coal, flax and wax — rather than knick-knacks, and had the means to do business on a large scale, the Bulletin reported.

    Taryn Edwards, “Before the Midwinter Fair: The Mechanics' Institute's ‘Pacific Rim' Industrial Exhibitions of 1869 and 1871,” The Argonaut: Journal of the San Francisco Historical Society, vol. 30, no. 1 (Summer 2019), pp. 8–23

Three of the 792 filings under this address and parcel are shown. DBI files work in the neighbouring 38-storey office tower under the same address, so only filings whose description identifies the two-storey banking hall — its basement vaults, its mezzanine, its historic features — are listed here. Business addresses are usually deferred on this site; this page exists because the address was named in the prompting source. The parcel carries the two-storey banking hall only — the 38-storey tower behind it on the same block is a separate parcel (0292004, 68–82 Post Street), though DBI files its permits under this address.
