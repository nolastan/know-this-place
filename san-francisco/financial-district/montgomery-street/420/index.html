420 Montgomery Street

Financial District · San Francisco, CA 94104

  • Built 1959
  • Commercial office
  • 12 stories
  • Class-A construction
  • Historic status: unevaluated

Samuel Brannan put up the second of a pair of Montgomery Street buildings on this site in 1853; the building standing here replaced it.
