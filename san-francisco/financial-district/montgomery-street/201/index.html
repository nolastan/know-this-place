  6. 1979

    The Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage rated the Russ Building "A" in the 1979 downtown survey it published as Splendid Survivors — the top of its four grades, which it reserved for the buildings it judged individually most important downtown. The Department of City Planning's 1976 Architectural Inventory coded it 4-D5-4.

    333 Bush Street draft initial study, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1981
  7. 1980s

    The Russ Building, where the Golden Gate Business Association - known as the first LGBTQ chamber of commerce, founded in 1974 - had its offices by the early 1980s; it had 500 members by 1980.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
