  1. 1930

    The Title Insurance Co. Building of 1930, a privately funded building in the PWA Moderne style, which was otherwise used mostly for public work.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  2. 1930

    The Title Insurance Company Building, designed by architect Wilbur Peugh, who over his career designed roughly two thousand buildings.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  3. 1977

    A narrow six-storey building stood here, with a tall masonry facade in the art deco style. Its entrance was dramatically treated and flanked by bas reliefs of muscular figures, and its copper or bronze spandrels were designed with zig-zag motifs; the Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage called it one of the finest of the very few buildings in that style in San Francisco, and asked that whatever was built next to it should respect it.

    180 Montgomery Street DEIR, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1977
