A 43-storey downtown office tower by John Graham & Associates, part of the sharp increase in height and bulk downtown through the 1960s. Found eligible for the California Register in a Planning Department historic resource evaluation.

The statement dates this tower 1967 on p. 9 and 1968 on p. 36. Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. The city's Modern & Postmodern styles context statement dates this building 1968; the assessor's roll records it as built 1966. Submit an update
