  1. 1979

    The Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage rated the building here "C" in the 1979 downtown survey it published as Splendid Survivors — the third of its four grades, for buildings that count for their scale and materials and for the way they hold a streetfront together. The Department of City Planning's 1976 Architectural Inventory did not rate it.

    333 Bush Street draft initial study, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1981
  2. October 6, 1982

    The building here was named as one of six buildings in the Hallidie Building Block - Retail Historic District, which the Landmarks Preservation Advisory Board recommended to the City Planning Commission for designation as a city historic district. The district was centred on the Hallidie Building at 130 Sutter Street and covered that side of the block; it also lay within the larger Retail-Shopping Historic District that Heritage had identified as eligible for the National Register.

    222 Kearny Street DEIR, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1983
