  5. 1979

    The Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage rated the Hunter-Dulin Building "A" in the 1979 downtown survey it published as Splendid Survivors — the top of its four grades, which it reserved for the buildings it judged individually most important downtown. The Department of City Planning's 1976 Architectural Inventory rated it "5" on a scale running from 0 to 5. Buildings rated A or B by the Foundation, or 3 or above by the city survey, went onto the List of Architecturally and/or Historically Significant Buildings in the Downtown, adopted by the City Planning Commission on 29 May 1980 in Resolution No. 8600.

    222 Kearny Street DEIR, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1983
