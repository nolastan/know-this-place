Two rooftops

The open space is two sun terraces rather than one, and the further of them sits on the historic bank building at Montgomery and Post, reached by stairs or a lift.

Crocker Galleria by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill, its arched glass atrium part of the first major Postmodernist project downtown - and a shift signalled by one of the stalwart Modernist firms.
