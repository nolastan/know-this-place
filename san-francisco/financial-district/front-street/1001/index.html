  1. 1905

    A glass warehouse and mirror factory built for W. P. Fuller and Co. — the one important building to come out of George Alexander Wright's short-lived partnership with Willis Polk.

    The West Slope of Russian Hill: A Historical Context and Inventory of Historic Resources, 2006, revised 2009
  2. 1907

    Built.

  3. April 1967

    KMPX, the first underground radio station — playing whole albums rather than top-forty singles — began broadcasting from here in April 1967 and influenced the founding of dozens of stations across the country. Its March 1968 staff walkout, announced on air over low wages, drew crowds outside and a flatbed-truck performance by sympathetic musicians.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
