Twenty San Franciscans

Called to Rise shows twenty figures from the city's history — among them Juan Bautista de Anza, Eadweard Muybridge, Phoebe Apperson Hearst and Amadeo Peter Giannini — with two bronze panels explaining what each did. The city's inventory credits Thomas Marsh and Qiliu Pan; the published account of the work names only Marsh.
