Public art

  • Untitled — Fritz Koenig
    Bronze; spiral staircase on Commercial St.; artwork and open space are always accessible.
    Fritz Koenig (1924-2017), a German sculptor born in Wurzburg, Bavaria, made this bronze sphere with black etchings, sited on Commercial Street between the Old Federal Reserve Bank Building and the former Le Meridien hotel, encircled by a spiral stairway.
  • Creazione — Dimitri Hadzi
    Bronze; Commercial St.; artwork and open space are always accessible.
    Dimitri Hadzi (1921-2006) cast this bronze sculpture, inspired by the music of Mozart, in bronze with a black patina on a marble base; it was commissioned for Embarcadero Center and stands on Commercial Street at Sansome Street.
  • The Universality of Wisdom — Arman
    Bronze; Federal Reserve steps Battery St.; artwork is always accessible.
    Arman, born Armand Pierre Fernandez on November 17, 1928 in Nice, France and died October 22, 2005 in New York, became a U.S. citizen in 1973 and worked as a French-American Nouveau Realiste sculptor.
  • Hermes and Dionysos: Monument to Analysis — Arman
    Bronze; Front steps at sidewalk along Sansome Street; artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 1990. The two bronze figures Dionysos and Hermes were added to the Sansome Street entrance in 1990; other casts of the same 1986 composition spell the title "Hermes and Dionysos," differing from the on-file "Dionysus." The city's inventory titles it “Hermes and Dionysus - Monument to Analysis”.

The Old Federal Reserve Bank

An Ionic colonnade in the Beaux-Arts manner carries an upper section in the Moderne manner. The building was designated a San Francisco landmark in 1983 and added to the National Register on 31 July 1989. Jules Guerin's mural Traders of the Adriatic, in the main lobby, shows Venetian shipping merchants taking receipts for goods on deposit.

Published sources spell the Arman sculpture's title “Dionysos”; the city's inventory gives “Dionysus”.
