Architect as surveyedLawrence Halprin
Year built as surveyed1982

The Levi's Plaza landscape by Lawrence Halprin, completed in 1982: a geometric 'Hard Park' with a stepped, interactive fountain set against a naturalistic 'Soft Park'. Widely published and shown as one of Halprin's most significant landscapes in his 1986 SFMOMA retrospective.
