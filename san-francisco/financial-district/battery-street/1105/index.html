Year built as surveyed1981

Levi's Plaza, a three-block brick mid-rise office complex by HOK with interior architect Gensler and landscape architect Lawrence Halprin - effectively the only corporate office park in San Francisco, scaled and coloured to sit among the historic brick warehouses at the foot of Telegraph Hill. Called one of the most significant examples of Late Modernist landscape design in the city, eligible under California Register Criterion 3 and likely to meet the National Register's exceptional-significance threshold.
