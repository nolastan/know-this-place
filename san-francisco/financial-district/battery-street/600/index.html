  1. 1913

    In its natural state the ground lay under the shallow water of Yerba Buena Cove; by 1913 a single narrow rectangular building housing a paper manufacturer stood on the lot.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 640 Battery Street Office Building Expansion: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 97.678E, 1998.
  2. 1926

    A reinforced concrete and glass light-industrial building put up in 1926 to house the Traung Lithography and Label Company, designed by Maurice Couchot and Jesse Rosenwald, with Gothic Revival detail at the Battery Street entrances and industrial sash windows.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 640 Battery Street Office Building Expansion: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 97.678E, 1998.
  3. 1937

    An abutting addition of the same height and style was completed in 1937 to Rosenwald's design.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 640 Battery Street Office Building Expansion: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 97.678E, 1998.
