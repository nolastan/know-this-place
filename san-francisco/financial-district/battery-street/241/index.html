Public art

  • Torso with Raised Arm — Stephen de Staebler
    Bronze; Side of building, midblock, along Sacramento Street; artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 1990. De Staebler's own curriculum vitae lists this bronze work at the Portman Building, Embarcadero Center West, dated 1990; another source dates an installation of the same title to 1987. The city's inventory titles it “Torso with arm raised”.
  • Baile Merengue — Bill Barrett
    Bronze; Embarcadero West lobby; artwork is always accessible.
    Bill Barrett (born December 21, 1934), an American sculptor, is the artist of this bronze work on view in the Embarcadero Center West lobby.
