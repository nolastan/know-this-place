  1. 1908

    Built in 1908 for the White Investment Company to the design of Lewis P. Hobart: brick with concrete supports, faced in stucco, stone and metal, a shell-and-fish moulding running through the frieze and a large cartouche above the cornice at the entrance.

    Daon Building, Battery and Sacramento Streets: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1980
  2. 1920

    A two-storey brick building with wood posts, built in 1920 to the design of B. G. McDougall, fronting Halleck Street as well as Battery, and Sacramento Street at 335-337.

    Daon Building, Battery and Sacramento Streets: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1980
  3. 1922

    Six storeys built in 1922 for H. & W. Pierce, Inc. to the design of B. G. McDougall — steel frame, reinforced concrete floors and roof, and a grey brick curtain wall.

    Daon Building, Battery and Sacramento Streets: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1980
