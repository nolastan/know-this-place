Public art

  • Untitled — Pepo Pichler
    Concrete, granite, stone, cast bronze; Empire Park; open space and artwork is accessible during daylight hours.
    Pichler was born in 1948 in Klagenfurt, Austria, trained at the Vienna Academy of Fine Arts, and moved to San Francisco in 1975; his fountain sculpture is the park's central focal point.
