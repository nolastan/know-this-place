Earlier record

  1. 1980

    Completed as the Dimasalang House, a HUD-funded joint venture between the Tenants and Owners Development Corporation and the Filipino fraternal organization Caballero de Dimasalang, which lobbied at the same time to rename the surrounding streets — Bonifacio, Lapu Lapu, Mabini and Rizal — after historic Filipino figures. It carries the Lipi Ni Lapu Lapu mural on its north side.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
