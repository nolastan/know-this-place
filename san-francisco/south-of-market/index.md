# South of Market

The wide blocks west of Fourth Street, between Market and Harrison. Coverage here begins with the buildings named in the city's public-art and privately-owned-public-open-space inventories.

## Streets documented so far

- [Market Street](market-street/) — 1 building, built 2006; 1 in the Kearny-Market-Mason-Sutter Conservation District.
- [Mission Street](mission-street/) — 2 buildings, built 2015; 1 in the Mint-Mission Conservation District.
- [Natoma Street](natoma-street/) — 1 building, built 2014.
- [Ninth Street](9th-street/) — 2 buildings, built 2011–2014.
