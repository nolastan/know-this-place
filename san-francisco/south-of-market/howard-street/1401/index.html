  1. 1913

    Saint Joseph's Church, dated 1913. Its congregation, largely Irish in the early 20th century, became increasingly Mexican and Filipino by the middle of the century. The parish moved to Saint Patrick's after the 1989 earthquake and the church is now closed.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
  2. 1913

    The existing St. Joseph's Church complex — two churches, a school, convent, and rectory — was under construction in 1913 and rededicated in 1914, designed by architect John J. Foley, replacing the parish's earlier churches (1861, 1865) destroyed in the 1906 Earthquake and Fire.

    South of Market Area Historic Context Statement, adopted 2011
