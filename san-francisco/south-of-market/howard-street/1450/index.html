Address as surveyed1420 Howard Street

One of the 148 industrial buildings the survey inventoried as standing in its Industrial Employment Study Area, the mixed industrial and residential district between Market, Howard, 16th and 9th streets. The report's own appendices give this building more than one build year: 1900, 1906.
