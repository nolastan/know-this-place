  1. April 18, 1906

    One of the handful of buildings left standing in the Central SoMa area after the earthquake and fire.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1940

    The Kamm/Morris Plan Company Building, designed by Bliss & Faville in 1905, was remodelled.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  3. 1950s

    Remodelled again with Art Moderne influences by Hurt, Trudell & Berger. A building altered wholesale into a later style, it has lost architectural association with its original construction but may be significant for the period of the alteration.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
