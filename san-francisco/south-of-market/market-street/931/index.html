Earlier record

  1. 1907

    Built to a design by architect Albert Pissis as a two-storey brick masonry commercial building with a saloon and pool hall.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1939

    Connected internally to 929 Market Street and the two façades unified with Art Deco influences by architect Otto A. Deichmann, for the National Dollar Store. Both buildings keep their brick rear cladding.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
