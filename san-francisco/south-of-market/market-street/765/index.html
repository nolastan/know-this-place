The San Francisco Marriott Marquis, designed by Anthony Lumsden of DMJM after the firm took the project over from Eberhard Zeidler of Zeidler Partnership Architects. The critic Allan Temko called its fanned top a 'hallucinatory jukebox'; it is the most exaggerated of the city's Postmodern hotel towers.
