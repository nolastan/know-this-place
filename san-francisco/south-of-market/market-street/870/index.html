  2. 1917

    The acting Russian consulate had an office in the Flood Building here from 1917 until the consulate nominally closed in November 1924.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
  3. 1925

    Russian émigré doctors kept surgeries in the Flood Building here in 1925, and the acting Russian consulate had an office in the building from 1917.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
