Year built as surveyed1988

San Francisco Centre by Whisler/Patri, opened in 1988 as a downtown shopping centre competing with the suburban malls, finished in polished granite and applied ornament. Expanded onto the adjacent historic Emporium building site in 2006 by Kohn Pedersen Fox.
