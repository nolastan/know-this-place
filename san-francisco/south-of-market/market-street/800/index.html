  1. 1908

  2. 1908

    The West Bank Building, designed by architect Charles F. Whittlesey.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  3. 1928–1932

    The downtown office of the Castle Building Company. In its four years the firm built the earliest known group of split-level houses in the Sunset District.

    Sunset District Residential Builders Historic Context Statement, adopted 2013
  4. 1930

    The Filipino Federation of America, which counted the largest Filipino membership of any organisation outside the Philippines, was headquartered here in 1930.

    San Francisco Filipino Heritage: Addendum to the South of Market Historic Context Statement, 2013
