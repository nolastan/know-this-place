  1. 1872

    The new St. Patrick's church was dedicated, serving a predominantly Irish immigrant population of about 30,000 and intended as the Irish national church of San Francisco and the West Coast. The parish had been established in 1851 in a rented hall at 4th and Jessie streets, and its lot on the north side of Mission Street was bought after the Civil War.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. April 18, 1906

    Heavily damaged in the earthquake and fire; the church was reconstructed.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  3. 1939

    The church rectory adjoining it was constructed.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  4. 1965

    Yerba Buena Center redevelopment plans showed only 15 percent of the roughly 400 buildings in the project area being retained, with the central blocks razed save for St. Patrick's Church.
