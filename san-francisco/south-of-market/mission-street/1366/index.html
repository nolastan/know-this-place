  1. 1980s

    The Lesbian Rights Project, formed in 1977, operated from an office here by the early 1980s, advising women on donor insemination, parental rights and employment; it published the Lesbian Mother Litigation Manual in 1982. Identified as potentially eligible for landmark designation.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
