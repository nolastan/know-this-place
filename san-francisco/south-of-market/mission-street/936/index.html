  1. 1915

    Built as the Land Hotel and later renamed the Chronicle Hotel for its proximity to the newspaper: five storeys of brick masonry with patterned polychrome brickwork and terra cotta beltcourses, strongly representative of the mixed-use residential hotels put up in the Central SoMa area after 1906.
