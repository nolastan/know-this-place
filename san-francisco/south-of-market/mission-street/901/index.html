  1. 1924

    Built for the San Francisco Chronicle and, unlike the paper's earlier building at 690 Market Street, devoted entirely to producing and printing the newspaper. Gothic Revival in style and since altered; its design shows the influence of Eliel Saarinen's 1922 drawing for the Chicago Tribune Tower.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1960s

    The building exterior was remodelled.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  3. 1976

    Offices of the San Francisco Chronicle, where Armistead Maupin wrote the daily instalments of Tales of the City.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
