Public art

  • Linking Hand Veil — Ball-Nogues Studio
    Acrylic beads; 10th and Mission stair tower; visible from street right-of-way.
    The acrylic-on-glass mural, commissioned through San Francisco's 1% for Public Art Program, is made of hundreds of colored translucent plastic hand shapes strung in catenary chains, screening the SOMA Teen Center storefront on 10th Street between Market and Mission while coloring the sunlight passing through it. The city's inventory credits Ball-Nogues Studio; the source cited here gives Ball-Nogues Studio (Benjamin Ball and Gaston Nogues).
  • Linking Hand Cloud — Ball-Nogues Studio

  • Linking Hand Veil — Ball-Nogues Studio
    Acrylic; 10th street between Market and Mission; artwork is always accessible.
    Benjamin Ball previously worked at Gehry Partners and in film production design on the Matrix films; Gaston Nogues, from Buenos Aires, also worked at Gehry Partners specializing in fabrication.
