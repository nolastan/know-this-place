  1. 1914

    The Mission Revival style Lincoln School stood here, the only public school in the Central SoMa study area at the time. It was demolished in the 1960s.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1972

    The Filipino Education Center, opened on 1 May 1972 at 390 4th Street with contributions from the San Francisco Unified School District and the State of California, moved here. It taught non-English-speaking children from kindergarten through twelfth grade and referred families to educational, health and social services.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  3. 2004

    With the Bessie Carmichael School/Filipino Education Center rebuilt as a K–5 campus at 375 7th Street, this building became home to the school's middle school grades.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
