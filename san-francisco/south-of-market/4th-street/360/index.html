  1. 1907

    A wood-frame Mission Revival style police station stood on the site.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1925

    Replaced by the Spanish Colonial Revival Southern Police Station, perhaps the finest Spanish Colonial Revival building in the South of Market. It has been found eligible for the National Register, and was put forward for San Francisco Landmark designation.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  3. 1936

    Works Progress Administration renovations to the station were completed — one of only two WPA projects in the Central SoMa area.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  4. 1970s

    Portions of the ornamental wings were removed before the 1970s, and the building was eventually converted into a senior centre.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
