  1. 1972

    The Folsom Street Barracks, a bathhouse, occupied this address from 1972 to 1976. Fire destroyed it in October 1976, and the 1981 Folsom fire destroyed the site again.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
  2. 1972

    Casey's, a bar, occupied this address from 1972 to 1973. It operated as part of the Folsom Street Barracks.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
  3. 1973

    The 1145 Saloon, a bar, occupied this address in 1973. It adjoined the Folsom Street Barracks.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
  4. 1973

    The Red Star Saloon, a bar, occupied this address from 1973 to 1976. It connected to the Folsom Street Barracks.

    Damon Scott for the Friends of 1800, Sexing the City: The Development of Sexual Identity Based Subcultures in San Francisco, 1933–1979, historic context statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 2004
