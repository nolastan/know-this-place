  1. 1910s

    Run as the Pullman Hotel, home to a number of African American railroad porters who lived close to the Southern Pacific depot.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
  2. 1920

    The Townsend House, later the New Pullman Hotel, housed many of the Black railroad porters who worked out of the Southern Pacific depot at Fourth and Townsend. It was listed in the Negro Motorist Green Book year after year and is a designated city landmark.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
