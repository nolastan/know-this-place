Earlier record

  1. 1923

    Built as the Pickwick Hotel, one of two Gothic Revival buildings at 5th and Mission streets. A parking garage for the hotel was completed in 1927. Proposed for addition to the Kearny-Market-Mason-Sutter Article 11 conservation district.
