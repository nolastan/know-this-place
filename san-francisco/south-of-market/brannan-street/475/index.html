  1. 1908

    An unreinforced brick and heavy-timber warehouse of two storeys over a basement, 275 by 258 feet, designed by Washington J. Miller and built for the hardware firm Baker & Hamilton on the site of the California Wine Association building destroyed in 1906.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 475 Brannan Street: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 97.470E, 1998.
  2. 1918

    Baker & Hamilton occupied the building for ten years, leaving in 1918 after merging with the Pacific Hardware & Steel Company.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 475 Brannan Street: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 97.470E, 1998.
