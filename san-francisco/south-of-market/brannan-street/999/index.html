The California DataMart, later the Diamond and Jewelry Mart, by Tanner & VanDine - the firm that became Tanner Leddy Maytum Stacy. Shown in SFMOMA's 1991 'In the Spirit of Modernism' exhibition and named as one of the projects that signalled the local shift from Postmodernism to New Modernism. It took a Progressive Architecture award in 1984.
