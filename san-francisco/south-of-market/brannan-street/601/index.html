  1. 1924

    Built for Grinnell Company of the Pacific, successor to the General Fire Extinguisher Company, and photographed for the March 1935 issue of Architect & Engineer; the UMB survey noted its 'robust red' brick, and it carries roofline plaques bearing the letter G and the image of a fire sprinkler. Added to the Bluxome and Townsend Warehouse Historic District, correcting an apparent omission in the South of Market survey, whose DPR 523 A form gave no year of construction and photographed the company's separate steel-framed pipe shed instead. The same architects designed another contributor to the district, 650 5th Street.

    Central SoMa Historic Context Statement, adopted 2016
