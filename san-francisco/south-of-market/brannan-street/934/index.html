  1. 1906

    Built by the Western Pacific Railway as one of the corrugated steel structures the railroads put up cheaply for new tenants after the earthquake; designed by the company's chief engineer V. G. Bogue and built by Thomas H. Day & Sons. Long the home of the Union Machine Shop, and the only one of these buildings known to survive in the survey area. Engineered by the Western Pacific engineering office.

    Showplace Square / Northeast Mission Historic Resource Survey, adopted 2011
