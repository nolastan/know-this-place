  1. 1935–1941

    The only wholly new firehouse the Works Progress Administration built in San Francisco, put up for $83,000 to replace a dilapidated South of Market station. SFFD Engine No. 8 is in the Streamline Moderne style and still stands nearly unaltered.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
  2. 1939-1940

    Fire Station No. 8, designed by architect Dodge A. Reidy, who was listed as City Architect in 1946.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
