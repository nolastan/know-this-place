Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

StyleClassical Revival

Recorded as the only Romeo flat known in the plan area — a San Francisco type built mostly after 1906, three bays wide, with a winding central staircase dividing the facade and stacks of narrow flats in the outer bays, usually four or six of them. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
