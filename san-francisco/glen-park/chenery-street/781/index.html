Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

StyleCraftsman
Address as surveyed763–791 Chenery Street

One of the row of Craftsman houses built in 1921 and 1922 along the south side of Chenery Street between Lippard Avenue and Brompton Avenue — gabled roofs with exposed rafter tails and prominent brackets, wide eave overhangs, and double-hung windows with multi-light upper sashes. The evaluation calls the row five houses; the printed range 763–791 covers six parcels on the assessor's roll. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
