Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

Address as surveyed664–676 Chenery Street

Falls inside the range 664–676 Chenery Street that both photograph captions give for the 1917 Spanish Eclectic commercial building the text places at 664–670; 664, 670 and 676 are three separate parcels, and the assessor dates this one to 1908. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study. The evaluation examined the commercial core at Diamond and Chenery as a potential historic district and found it did not qualify; the city's current record puts this parcel in the California Register-eligible Glen Park Center Historic District.
