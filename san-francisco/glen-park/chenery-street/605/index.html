Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

Pictured in the property-type chapter as an example of the plan area's one- and two-story commercial buildings, most of them extensively modified but keeping rectangular plans, flat roofs, and storefront windows and entrances on the facade. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
