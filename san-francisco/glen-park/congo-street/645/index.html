Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

Named as the address at which the 1924 city directory lists Christopher Kernan, a carpenter — the Christopher J. Kernan whom building permits give as architect of 2852–2862 Diamond Street. The house stands outside the plan area and was not surveyed.
