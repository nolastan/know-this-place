Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Year built as surveyed1880

Named as the first house Fernando Nelson built, in 1880, after working as a carpenter's apprentice in the Mission and Noe Valley. It sold for $800. Nelson went on to build more than 4,000 houses in San Francisco, dozens of them in Eureka Valley.
