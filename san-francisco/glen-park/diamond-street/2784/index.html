Survey findingAppears eligible for the California Register under Criterion 3 (architecture)
Address as surveyed2784–2786 Chenery Street

The finding is the San Francisco Planning Department's, from its own survey of the parcels west of Lippard Avenue and inside the neighborhood commercial zoning, which this evaluation reprints. Printed in the list of eligible buildings as 2784–2786 Chenery Street, a street whose numbers do not run that high; the archived copy carries a hand-written correction to Diamond.
