  1. 1910–1912

    The Glen Park Grammar School was built on this parcel for the families who settled Glen Park after 1906 — a three-story Mission Revival building with shaped parapets and a detached auditorium reached by a one-story passage. It taught first through eighth grade and was replaced in 1934.

    Glen Park Historic Context Statement, adopted 2011
  2. 1939

    Chain link fences were added to the concrete retaining walls, five years after the school was completed.

    Glen Park Historic Context Statement, adopted 2011
  3. 1965

    A concrete pedestrian ramp was built over Bosworth Street, after the city widened it to four lanes in 1964; small sections of the retaining wall at the northern boundary were removed for it.

    Glen Park Historic Context Statement, adopted 2011
  4. 1986

    Accessibility upgrades added entrances, drinking fountains, bathrooms and handrails, the largest exterior change being a new ramp and entrance on the east elevation at the northern playground.

    Glen Park Historic Context Statement, adopted 2011

One of eight parcels the evaluation surveyed intensively. It was funded by a 1933 Public Works Administration loan and grant, alongside George Washington High School, Marina Junior High School, Lawton Elementary School and Visitacion Valley School, and built in three phases under PWA project numbers 7942.04, 7942.12 and 1578.0. Its terra cotta detailing follows Bliss & Fairweather's post office at Stockton. The original wood-sash multi-light awning and hopper windows have been replaced with metal sash in the same openings, on a date the school district's drawings do not record.
