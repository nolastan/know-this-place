# Glen Park

The valley around Glen Canyon, south of Diamond Heights and west of Bernal
Heights, laid out on the southern reaches of the Rancho San Miguel and settled
in earnest once the streetcar and later the freeway reached it. Coverage begins
on 30th Street with the first house Fernando Nelson built, named in Elaine
Stiles's 2017 Eureka Valley context statement as the start of a career that put
more than 4,000 houses across the city.
