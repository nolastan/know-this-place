Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

Survey findingNot eligible for the National Register, the California Register or City Landmark designation
Year built as surveyedNot identified; late 1910s or early 1920s by appearance
Address as surveyed23–25 Brompton Avenue
Parcel as surveyedBlock 6744, Lot 030

One of eight parcels the evaluation surveyed intensively, and one of five buildings the proposed Diamond/Bosworth infill site would demolish. Surveyed with 25 Brompton Avenue as one property on Lot 030, which has since been split; this is the smaller building, standing behind the one on the street, with a rear shed-roofed addition. No original building permit was found and it is not on the 1913–1915 Sanborn map, so the evaluation dates it only by appearance; the assessor dates it to 1915.
