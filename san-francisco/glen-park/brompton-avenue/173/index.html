Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

Recorded as an early wood-frame cottage of 1910, later raised to insert a garage at the ground story, with a staircase added to reach the entrance. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
