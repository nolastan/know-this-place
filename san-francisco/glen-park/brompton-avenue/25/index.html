Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

Survey findingNot eligible for the National Register, the California Register or City Landmark designation
Address as surveyed23–25 Brompton Avenue
Parcel as surveyedBlock 6744, Lot 030

One of eight parcels the evaluation surveyed intensively, and one of five buildings the proposed Diamond/Bosworth infill site would demolish. Surveyed with 23 Brompton Avenue as one property on Lot 030, which has since been split. Built as a single-family home and described as a late example of the false-front vernacular wood-frame building put up in Glen Park from the turn of the century, altered by replacement cladding, a garage cut into the middle of the facade and boarded windows.
