Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

StyleQueen Anne

Named as an elaborate Queen Anne house of 1909 and photographed about that year and again in 2009; the evaluation records the reconfigured front staircase and the garage added at the first story as the only major alterations. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
