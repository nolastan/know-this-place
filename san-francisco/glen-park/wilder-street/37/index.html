Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

StyleSpanish Eclectic

Named as an example of the "Junior Five", the standardized plan built to Federal Housing Administration guidelines across San Francisco — a garage at the first story, and a living room, kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom above — here with Spanish Eclectic detailing. It is also pictured among the plan area's Spanish Eclectic houses. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
