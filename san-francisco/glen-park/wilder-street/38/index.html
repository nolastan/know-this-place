Glen Park Historic Resources Evaluation (adopted 2011)

StyleModern

Named as one of the Modern residences built in the plan area in the 1950s and 1960s: flat roof, wide facade overhang, smooth stucco cladding, metal-sash casement windows and plain box bays. Recorded in the reconnaissance survey of 110 parcels but not evaluated individually; buildings the evaluation did not evaluate are treated as potential historical resources pending further project-level study.
