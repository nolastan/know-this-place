# 87 Harrington Street

A seven-room single-storey house of 1908 on a short street off Mission,
carrying one of the lowest assessed bases documented on this site.

## The building

- Built 1908 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1908)
- 1 unit, 7 rooms, 1 bath, 1 story; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 1,232 sq ft; lot 2,321 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-1; Supervisorial District 11
- Historic status: CEQA Category B — never formally evaluated. Not a
  designated landmark; in no historic district

The assessor files the parcel under Mission Terrace; the city's analysis
neighbourhood layer places it in Outer Mission. This site follows the analysis
neighbourhood.

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Jerry Garcia as
living here with his grandparents for the five years following his father's
death in 1947. The same guide places the family at [121 Amazon
Avenue](../../../excelsior/amazon-avenue/121/) before that.

## Permit history

Two permits are on file.

- **2013** — replace the water heater, install five recessed light fittings,
  repair hardwood flooring, and close out an electrical permit left open since
  2005 ($5,000) —
  [permit 201312184523](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=201312184523)
- **2000** — reroofing ($9,000 revised), expired —
  [permit 200003224999](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200003224999)

## Value

The 2025 roll assesses land at $36,011 and improvements at $29,999 — $66,010 in
total, and no sale on the current roll. That is among the lowest bases
documented here, and the signature of a parcel held since before Proposition
13 rather than any statement about the house.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, and any primary-source
confirmation of the Garcia years.
