Earlier record

  1. 1901

    Built 1901 as the San Francisco and San Mateo Railway's company car house and office building at San Jose and Geneva avenues; by the 1920s it housed more streetcars than any other barn in the city's electric-car era.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
  2. 1944

    Transferred to the Municipal Railway (MUNI) in 1944 along with the rest of the Market Street Railway system.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
  3. 1985

    Designated a San Francisco landmark as the Geneva Office Building in 1985.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
  4. 1989

    Severely damaged in the Loma Prieta earthquake, prompting MUNI to abandon the building for operations.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
  5. 2004

    Transferred to the San Francisco Recreation and Park Department for use as an arts and history center, pending rehabilitation funding.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
