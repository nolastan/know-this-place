# 720 Steiner Street

One of the Painted Ladies facing Alamo Square — the row photographed as
"Postcard Row" or the "Seven Sisters" — built around 1900 and inside the
Alamo Square Historic District. Published guides record Alice Walker as living
here until 1996.

## The building

- Built 1900 (assessor roll and SF Planning both record 1900)
- 1 unit, 9 rooms, 2 baths, 2 stories; assessor class "Dwelling"
- Building area 2,875 sq ft; lot 1,744 sq ft
- Wood-frame construction (assessor construction type D)
- Zoning RH-2; Supervisorial District 5
- SF Planning records the building under the name **Postcard Row (part)**

## Historic status

- CEQA Category A / A* — a historical resource, and within an **Article 10
  historic district**
- Alamo Square Historic District, period of significance **1890–1915**;
  California Register eligible, and Article 10 **listed**
- Because the district is Article 10, this parcel carries local landmark
  protection — unlike the California-Register-only districts elsewhere on this
  site

## Notable residents

A published guide to notable San Francisco residences records Alice Walker as
living here until 1996, and describes the house as one of the Painted Ladies of
Alamo Square. No underlying source is cited.

## Permit history

Only three permits are on file, and the building has no substantial recorded
alteration history.

- **2002** — renewal of the 2001 permit for final sign-off —
  [permit 200209035476](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200209035476)
- **2001** — remodel a bathroom and install a skylight at the stairs, kept
  3.5 feet off the property line ($15,000), expired —
  [permit 200110029763](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=200110029763)
- **1981** — the oldest record on file, created during job-card conversion;
  no scope recorded ($10,000) —
  [permit 8106844](https://dbiweb02.sfgov.org/dbipts/default.aspx?page=Permit&PermitNumber=8106844)

## Ownership and value

The assessor records the most recent sale on **24 August 2001**. The 2025 roll
assesses land at $1,072,513 and improvements at $715,007 — $1,787,520 total.

## Not yet documented

The architect and builder, the original permit, which of the seven houses in
the row this is by its position, and any primary-source confirmation of the
Walker residency and its end in 1996.
