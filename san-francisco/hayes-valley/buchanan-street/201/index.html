The Nightengale House, San Francisco Landmark No. 47 — a Gothic Revival house that, although architect-designed, carries the lacy bargeboards and pointed-arch windows of the earlier Carpenter Gothic buildings, and shows Victorian-era features alongside them.

The city's statement on early settlement era styles dates the house twice and disagrees with itself: its landmark list and illustration caption give 1882, its narrative 1878. The assessor's roll gives 1878. The house's style is recorded both as Carpenter Gothic and as San Francisco Stick/Eastlake. Not yet documented: the developer and the early residents.
