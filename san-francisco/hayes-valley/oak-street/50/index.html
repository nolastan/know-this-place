Public art

  • Exultadagio — Daniel Winterich
    Glass; facade of the addition/ left of the main entrance; artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 2006. The 74-by-40-foot glass curtain wall uses prismatic glass fins with dichroic glass strips that project colored light onto the facade and into classrooms, fulfilling San Francisco's 1% for public art requirement; architect for the addition was Perkins + Will and the glass was fabricated by Lenehan Architectural Glass.
