  4. 1988

    The Western Addition Cultural Center hosted the 1988 kickoff of the San Francisco chapter of the National Coalition of Black Lesbians and Gays, and its Sargent Johnson Gallery mounted the 1989 exhibition The Dynamics of Color: Lesbian Artists Respond to Racism; the building later housed the Queer Cultural Center.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  5. 1989

    A building put up for the Acme Brewing Company, used as the San Francisco Redevelopment Agency's Western Addition field office through the redevelopment years and turned over to the community in the late 1980s. The African American Art and Culture Complex moved in in 1989 — one of the city's six cultural centres, with galleries, a 206-seat theatre, dance studios and a conference room — and the San Francisco African American Historical and Cultural Society is housed here. Murals were painted at the centre in 1984 and 1996. Identified in 2024 as eligible for local landmark, California Register or National Register designation.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
