The 2025 secured roll carries no row for this parcel, or for any of the six beside it: sf-parcels puts seven active parcels on map 0817070 — 453, 455, 457, 459, 461, 463 and 465 Hayes Street — and the assessor has yet to report any of them. The page therefore states no year built, use, floor area or assessed value. EAS and sf-parcels disagree by one lot over which parcel carries which number along this frontage: EAS files 465 Hayes Street on 0817076, which sf-parcels records as 463 Hayes Street. The Department of Building Inspection files every permit at 465 Hayes Street under lot 070, and a 2014 permit on that lot reads “new address at block 0817, lot 070 as; 465 hayes street”, so the page follows sf-parcels and DBI. A 2014 permit on this lot describes itself as a revision to an approved application filed under “original address, 350 linden street, block 0817, lot 089”. sf-parcels maps 350 Linden Street as its own parcel, 0817069, so whether the Hayes Street frontage and the Linden Street building are one structure is not settled by the city's records.
