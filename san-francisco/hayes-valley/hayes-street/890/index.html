  2. 1983

    The Pride Center; Gay American Indians took offices here in 1983 and hosted dances and events honouring American Indian women, and the Shanti Project listed this as its address from 1983 while running grief and support groups, a volunteer Buddy Program and the first housing programme for people with AIDS. Identified as potentially eligible for landmark designation.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
