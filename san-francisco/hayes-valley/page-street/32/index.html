  1. 1975

    A Gay Community Center operated here in the 1970s, hosting Bay Area Gay Liberation meetings, a Black Gay Conference in July 1976 and the biweekly Black Gay Caucus that followed, the offices of the Gay Latino Alliance, meetings of the Gay Asian Support Group and Gay American Indians, and in 1977 the first formal gay film festival in the United States. Identified as potentially eligible for landmark designation.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
