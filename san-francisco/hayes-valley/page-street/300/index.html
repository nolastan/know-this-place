  1. 1960s

    The San Francisco Zen Center, which grew out of the Japantown temple and became a major Buddhist institution in the Bay Area.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
  2. 1969

    The San Francisco Zen Center bought the Julia Morgan-designed Emanu-El Sisterhood building here in 1969 for its City Center. Built to shelter single Jewish women, it could house seventy-five residential students practising zazen four times a day.

    Donna Graves for San Francisco Heritage, Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965–1975), San Francisco Planning Department, adopted 17 July 2024
