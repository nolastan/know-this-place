  1. 1924

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as a garage and repair shop. Designed by the O'Brien Brothers as the Grand Central Garage, close in form to their Kay's Garage on Pacific Avenue. The Grand Central Garage, a public garage for thirteen years.
