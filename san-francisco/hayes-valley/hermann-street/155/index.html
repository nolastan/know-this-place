  1. 1900

  2. 1937

    Built as the San Francisco Mint on the crown of Reservoir Hill, where the Spring Valley Water Company's reservoir had been, to a Moderne design by Gilbert Stanley Underwood, Supervising Architect of the U.S. Treasury. It took over the functions of the Old Mint at Fifth and Mission.

    Market & Octavia Historic Context Statement, adopted 2007
  3. May 15, 1937

    The Treasury Department picked this undeveloped serpentine bluff above Upper Market Street on 2 August 1934 because vaults could be sunk into the rock. The Office of the Supervising Architect under Gilbert Stanley Underwood designed the four-storey granite-faced building in the PWA Moderne style, massed to look forbidding, its only ornament a row of parapet medallions showing the coins to be struck inside. Clinton Construction Co. built it for about $1 million from August 1935 to March 1937; it was dedicated on 15 May 1937 and began minting coins on 1 July.

    San Francisco New Deal Historic Context Statement, adopted 2023
