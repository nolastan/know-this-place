  1. 1920

    Recorded in the city's survey of the Van Ness Avenue automobile corridor as built for use as an auto repair shop. Designed by A. Lacy Worswick. Brick-built, with arched openings defined by courses of brick and a corbelled brick cornice; almost all its wooden window sash survives. Auto repair shops worked here for 38 years.
