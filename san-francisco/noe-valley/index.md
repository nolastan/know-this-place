# Noe Valley

The valley south of the ridge that divides it from Eureka Valley, on land José
de Jesús Noé worked as part of the Rancho San Miguel before the Horner brothers
bought and subdivided it. Coverage begins on 21st Street with a house named in
Elaine Stiles's 2017 Eureka Valley context statement, just over the boundary of
that study area, as an example of the Second Bay Tradition.
