  8. 1991

    An office building completed in 1991 for the St. George Pathfinders, a Russian youth scouting organisation, replacing the single-storey wooden house that had held their headquarters since at least the 1960s. Its glass façade was designed to reflect the cathedral across the street. It now holds a dance academy and a small All-Cossacks Union museum.

    Nina Bogdan, Russian American Historic Context Statement, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission October 15, 2025
