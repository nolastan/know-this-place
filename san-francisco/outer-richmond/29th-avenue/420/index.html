  1. 1913

    St. Peter's built a Gothic Revival brick church here after the 1906 earthquake and fire destroyed its wooden sanctuary downtown. The building is unreinforced masonry and was later found individually eligible for the National Register of Historic Places.

    420-430 29th Avenue DEIR, SF Planning Department, 2008

    A parish hall was built onto the church as a Sunday school, according to the building permits.
