Listed among the properties the statement puts forward as meriting further study; it is a context statement rather than a survey, and says the list is conservative rather than exhaustive. The 1968 Here Today survey was adopted by the Board of Supervisors under Resolution No. 268-70, so Planning counts the buildings it lists as individual historic resources. The 1976 city architectural survey rated buildings 0 to 5, 5 highest, and was never adopted as a register of historic resources.
