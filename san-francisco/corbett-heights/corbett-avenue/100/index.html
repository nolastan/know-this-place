Post-war infill at the corner of Ord and Corbett, on a lot created when the parcel the Cassin family had held since the 1870s was divided.

2,294 sq ftLot area

  1. 1953

    Lot created by subdividing the Cassin parcel at Ord and Corbett, both sisters having died by 1951.

    Building completed by the contractor Elis J. Ekstrom, who two years later built 385 Corbett Avenue.
