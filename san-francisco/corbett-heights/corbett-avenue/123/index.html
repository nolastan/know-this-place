  1. 1880

    William Magner's grocery, saloon and basement meeting hall opened on this corner; it was the district's only hall until 1895, and the neighbourhood improvement clubs met in it.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "June Neighborhood News", Vol. VIII, No. 6 (1 June 2026)
  2. July 7, 1890

    Magner's store and hall burned to the ground, taking two neighbouring houses with it. The corner stayed empty until 1937.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "June Neighborhood News", Vol. VIII, No. 6 (1 June 2026)
  3. 1937

    House completed. Contractor Arthur Quinn (A & B Quinn); no architect.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "June Neighborhood News", Vol. VIII, No. 6 (1 June 2026)
  4. February 1937

    Building permit filed; stated cost $4,000.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "June Neighborhood News", Vol. VIII, No. 6 (1 June 2026)
  5. 1940

    Owner valued the house at $6,000 (federal census).

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "June Neighborhood News", Vol. VIII, No. 6 (1 June 2026)
  6. 1954
