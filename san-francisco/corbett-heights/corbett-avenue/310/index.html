  1. April 20, 1962

    Plans drawn by the architect Jonathan D. Buckley: a house of irregular shapes and angles, avoiding squares and rectangles down to the pool in the rear garden.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "April Neighborhood News", Vol. VII, No. 4 (1 April 2025)
  2. 1967

    Bought after the fire by Wilt Chamberlain (1936–1999) of the San Francisco Warriors, who repaired it and held it until 3 December 1975. City directories never list him at either of its two flats.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "April Neighborhood News", Vol. VII, No. 4 (1 April 2025)
  3. February 16, 1967

    An extensive fire left the house uninhabitable; the repair permit was filed on 16 February 1967, and the shingles it gained were not part of the original design.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "April Neighborhood News", Vol. VII, No. 4 (1 April 2025)
