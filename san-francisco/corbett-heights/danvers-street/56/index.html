56–58 Danvers Street

Corbett Heights · San Francisco, CA 94114

  • Two-flat
  • 2 stories
  • Zoned RH-2
  • Historic status: unevaluated

When the house was photographed in 1909, Falcon Street ran past it to a grocery and saloon. The Market Street extension later took the street, the store and the buildings around it.
