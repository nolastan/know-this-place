  1. 1976

    The three-storey brick warehouse then on the corner was given an architectural rating of "0" and a summary rating of "1" in the city's 1976 architectural survey. It was typical of what was built south of Market Street early in the twentieth century, and one of the last two- and three-storey brick buildings left on the block.

    101 Mission Street Office Building DEIR, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1981
  2. 1981

    The brick building on the corner had been pulled down by late 1981, after the City Planning Commission approved a twenty-storey tower for the site in August of that year.

    135 Main Street Building DEIR, SF Dept. of City Planning, 1981
