DBI holds 42 permits for this parcel, most of them tenant improvements on individual floors. The timeline shows the 24 largest by stated cost, plus the earliest on file; the DBI query in the sources below returns all 42. 6 of them are nominal $1 street-space or sidewalk permits. 3 expired or cancelled permits are not shown. This is a condominium building: the assessor's roll holds no record of the building itself, only 202 parcels on map 3794077 — 174 condominiums, 24 below-market-rate condominiums and 4 ground-floor commercial store condominiums, all recorded as built in 2007. The unit count here is the residential parcels among them, and the page's own parcel number is the map's key parcel, one of the ground-floor shops. The city's address registry files 170 King Street and 177 Townsend Street under parcel 3794004, which the city has retired; the numbers were not carried onto the condominium parcels that replaced it.
