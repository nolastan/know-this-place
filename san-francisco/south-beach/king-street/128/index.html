    The Garcia-Maggini Warehouse, a good example of the American Commercial-style warehouses built along the South End's rail lines.

  3. July 2, 1934

    During the 1934 Waterfront Strike, the Garcia & Maggini Warehouse was rented by strikebreakers as the dummy drayage company Atlas Drayage; on July 2-3, 1934, trucks loaded with coffee were driven here from Pier 38 under police protection, triggering a clash between strikers and police that spread west along Townsend Street.

    South of Market Area Historic Context Statement, adopted 2011
