  1. 1976

    The De Lano Building stood here: two storeys, twenty-three feet wide, entered in the 1976 city architectural inventory as a commercial or utilitarian building in the modern root style.

    Federal Reserve Bank: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1979
  2. 1976

    A five-storey building then held the corner of Market and Spear, entered in the city's 1976 architectural inventory in the classical root style with vernacular variations.

    Federal Reserve Bank: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1979
  3. 1979

    The Bay Building stood here, graded C in the Foundation for San Francisco's Architectural Heritage downtown survey — a background building supporting the character of more significant neighbours.

    Federal Reserve Bank: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1979
  4. 1979

    The Lincoln Hotel Building stood here, graded C in the Heritage downtown survey.

    Federal Reserve Bank: Draft Environmental Impact Report, 1979
