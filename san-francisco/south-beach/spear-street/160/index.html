Public art

  • Tau — Roger Berry
    Steel; open space in front of building; artwork is always accessible.
    Installed 1984. This stainless-steel sculpture, 96 inches in diameter and 14 inches deep, forms four intersecting cones that each trace the sun's path through the solar year.
