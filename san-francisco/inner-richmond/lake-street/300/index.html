The Little Sisters of the Poor complex by Anshen & Allen, almost bunker-like - evidence of the shift to heavier form and material in Late Modernism, and a long way from the light, glassy Modernism the same firm was known for.

Not yet documented: the developer, the early tenants and the date of the last recorded sale. The city's Modern & Postmodern styles context statement dates this building 1982; the assessor's roll records it as built 1981. Submit an update
