  1. 1938

    The Town Club opened here in 1938, across the street from Jack's Tavern, the third of the Fillmore's Big Three prewar nightclubs. Identified in 2024 as eligible for local landmark, California Register or National Register designation.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
  2. 1940s–1950s

    The buildings that housed the Binford Hotel and Town Club stand at this address, among the surviving traces of the Fillmore's African American commercial and nightlife district.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  3. 1949

    The Buford Hotel here was recommended to Black travellers by the 1949 Negro Motorist Green Book.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
