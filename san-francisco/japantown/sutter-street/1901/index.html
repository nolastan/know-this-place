  1. 1933

    Jack's Tavern, known as Jack's of Sutter, opened on this site in 1933 in a mixed neighbourhood of African Americans, Japanese, Filipinos and Jews — the first of the Fillmore's Big Three prewar nightclubs. Club Alabama, run by the impresario Lester Mapp, followed in the same building. Neither remains.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
