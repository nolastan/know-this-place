  1. 1925

    The Uoki K. Sakai fish market moved here from Geary Boulevard by 1925. It shared the block with a drugstore, a maternity hospital and a book and stationery shop, all established by the late 1920s.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  2. 1928

    Gosha-do Books and Stationery traded here, and was photographed at this address in 1928. It was a place where people browsed and played cards whether or not they were buying.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
  3. 1947

    Soko Hardware took over the premises after the war, the bookshop having reopened at 1705 Post Street in 1947.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
