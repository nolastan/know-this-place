    The Japan Center Theatre, one of the venues where drag theatre companies mounted productions in the 1960s and 1970s.

  2. 1967

    The Kabuki theatre, later the Sundance Kabuki, dates from 1967 — a product of redevelopment, in a mixture of Modernist and Japanese-inspired modern styles matching the Japan Center mall.

    Japantown Historic Context Statement, May 2009
