Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named among the study area's civic and institutional resources as the Douglass School, opened to pupils in 1953 and funded through a $48 million school construction bond passed in 1948. It replaced the 1895 Douglass School on the same block, which local parents had been petitioning to have replaced since 1949.
