  1. 1893

    Designed by architect Frederick Rabin.

    Architecture, Planning & Preservation Professionals: A Collection of Biographies, adopted 2021
  2. April 1896

    Henry Peters, a partner in the California Brewing Company a block away on Douglass Street, was listed here in April 1896 while his own house was being finished.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "September Neighborhood News", Vol. VIII, No. 9 (1 September 2026)
  3. 1902

    Built.

  4. 1905

    Shown on the 1905 Sanborn map with two stores and a saloon downstairs and flats above; the only mixed-use building known to survive from the 1892–1906 streetcar era.

    Corbett Heights Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
  5. August 1910

    Stelling's Grocery held the corner store, documented here in August 1910.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "September Neighborhood News", Vol. VI, No. 9 (1 September 2025)
  6. 1949

    Trading as the Douglass Market, a neighbourhood grocery that stayed on the corner into the 1970s.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "September Neighborhood News", Vol. VI, No. 9 (1 September 2025)
  7. 1978

    The grocery closed and a real estate office took the corner store, ending roughly eighty years of food retailing here.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "September Neighborhood News", Vol. VI, No. 9 (1 September 2025)
