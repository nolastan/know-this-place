  1. 1932

    Finnila's Finnish Baths moved to a single-storey brick building at this corner containing the saunas, several storefronts and a family residence. The bath house had begun about 1910 in the basement of 9 Douglass Street and moved to 4032 17th Street from 1919 to 1932. The building was demolished in 1985, after which the bath house moved to the Sunset District; it closed in 2000.

    Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
