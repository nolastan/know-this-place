Earlier record

  1. 1963

    The Missouri Mule, a honky-tonk bar at 2348 Market Street, changed hands; the new owner, John Burgoa, reoriented it toward a Gay clientele with a campy singer billed as Vivacious Vivian. It was among the earliest Gay-oriented bars in the Upper Market area, and is now Beaux.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

The 2017 Eureka Valley context statement calls the Missouri Mule the first gay bar in the Castro, and records that the citywide LGBTQ context statement identified it for further study as a San Francisco Landmark.
