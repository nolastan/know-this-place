Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

2140 Market Street housed the Mind Shaft, a well-known dance club, and most recently Lucky 13. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
