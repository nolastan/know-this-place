Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleMission Revival
Year built as surveyed1909

Illustrated in the statement's survey of Eureka Valley property types and architectural styles. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as apartments and flats over commercial space.

Progressive Era & Early Revival Styles Historic Context Statement (2023)

Survey findingAppropriate district contributor
StyleMission Revival
Year built as surveyed1910
Address as surveyed2317-2335 Market Street

A mixed-use Mission Revival building of 1910. Its ground-storey storefronts have been altered, but it keeps its repeated bay windows, shaped parapets and red tile roof elements with brackets and false rafters beneath them, and would be an appropriate district contributor.
