Here Today (1968)Miller-Joost House
1976 architectural surveyRated 4 of 5

The 1968 Here Today survey was adopted by the Board of Supervisors under Resolution No. 268-70, so Planning counts the buildings it lists as individual historic resources. The 1976 city architectural survey rated buildings 0 to 5, 5 highest, and was never adopted as a register of historic resources. The 2017 Eureka Valley context statement names the house as a surviving property associated with agricultural production, and recommends a thematic survey of the agricultural survivals in the former western neighbourhoods of the city on the strength of it and the dwelling at 225-227 Eureka Street.
