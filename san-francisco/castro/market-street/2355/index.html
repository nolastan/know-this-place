Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named among the study area's extant historic automotive commercial resources as an automobile repair and sales building of 1915, near 17th Street, and put forward with the Hecker Garage at 557 Castro Street as possibly eligible for the California Register as an early example of an automobile-oriented building type in the neighbourhood.
