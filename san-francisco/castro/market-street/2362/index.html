Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Listed as the José Theater / Names Project Building, City Landmark No. 241 — one of four city landmarks in Duboce Triangle and one of the two that stand on Market Street, outside the survey area. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association. The 2017 Eureka Valley context statement lists it among the designated landmarks related to Eureka Valley that stand outside its own study area.
