Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named among the study area's extant historic automotive commercial resources, at 17th, Market and Collingwood streets. The statement dates the building 1933 in its property-type discussion and 1920 in a figure caption, and its industrial production framework concludes that the brief candy-making use and the building's adaptable functionality diminish its association with the history of industrial production in Eureka Valley.
