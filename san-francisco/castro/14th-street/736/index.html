Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Named with its twin at 742–748 as a good example of the larger flats and apartment buildings that went up in Duboce Triangle between 1893 and 1905, whose box-like massing and near-identical facades gave the northeast of the neighborhood a denser, more urban look. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
