Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleClassical Revival

Built 1923–24 as one of a related pair with 106 Sanchez Street, in the already slightly out-of-date Classical Revival style but stucco-clad like its 1920s contemporaries. Neither has integral garages, which the statement reads as a mark of their transitional design. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
