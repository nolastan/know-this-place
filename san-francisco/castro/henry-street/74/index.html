Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleQueen Anne cottage
Address as surveyed74–76 Henry Street

The one cottage on Henry Street that matches the Fernando Nelson row on 14th Street, directly behind it. Inside the proposed Fernando Nelson Fourteenth Street Historic District, one of five local landmark districts the statement recommends, and a contributor to the California Register-eligible Duboce Triangle Historic District. The statement dates the row to 1906 in one passage and to 1902–1906 in another. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
