Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Address as surveyed161 Henry Street

The statement's best example of a Romeo flat in Duboce Triangle — a ten-unit building of the type that puts a pair of units on each floor either side of a central, often unenclosed stair, which is what gives the type its name. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
