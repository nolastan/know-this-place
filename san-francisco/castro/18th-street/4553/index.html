The 1976 city architectural survey rated buildings 0 to 5, 5 highest, and was never adopted as a register of historic resources. Built as two attached dwellings before 1900 and converted to flats by 1905. Shown on the 1900 and 1905 Sanborn maps as a dwelling built for more than one household, still standing but altered.
