Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Address as surveyed4201-4225 18th Street

Illustrated as the former Littleman's grocery store, an early 'big box' retail building. The local grocery chain, founded in 1937 and running about fourteen stores in the Bay Area, replaced its small grocery at the corner of Collingwood and 18th streets with a supermarket-style store here in 1954.
