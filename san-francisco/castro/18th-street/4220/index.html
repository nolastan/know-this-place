Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleQueen Anne

Illustrated in the statement's survey of Eureka Valley property types and architectural styles. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as a two-flat on the end-gable dwelling form, now in commercial use.
