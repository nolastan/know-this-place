  2. 1974

    The Full Moon Coffeehouse opened, collectively owned by a group of lesbian women and the first explicitly women-only establishment in San Francisco. Until it closed in 1977 it served food, hosted poetry readings and organised performances by newly popular women's musicians.

    Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
