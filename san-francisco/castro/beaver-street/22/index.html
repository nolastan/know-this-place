Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleItalianate villa
Year built as surveyedCa. 1870

City Landmark No. 284, and the only one of the half-dozen suburban villas built in Duboce Triangle in the 1870s and early 1880s that survives with any of its original setting: an Italianate villa on a pair of adjoining lots, with an intact carriage house and a mature Victorian garden. The property has been reduced by a third from its original three lots. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association. Dates it gives as “Ca.” are its own estimates.
