Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleMediterranean

One of six Mediterranean flats built on the first block of Beaver Street on the sites of two adjoining Victorian suburban villas; the statement places this group at the rear of an estate at 2273 15th Street. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
