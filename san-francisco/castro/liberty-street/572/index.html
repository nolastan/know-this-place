Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleStick
Year built as surveyed1897

Listed among the houses Fernando Nelson built in Eureka Valley. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as a side hall row house. The statement names Nelson's houses at 550-572 Liberty Street, and the late nineteenth and early twentieth-century dwellings on Liberty Street generally, as concentrations of developer housing worth studying as potential historic districts.
