  1. 1935

    The Finnish Evangelical Lutheran Church opened in a new building here.

    Market & Octavia Historic Context Statement, adopted 2007
  2. 1936

    Built.

  3. 1936

    Completed for $10,135 — a small sum for a building this size, which the statement reads as a sign the congregation volunteered its labour. The First Finnish Evangelical Lutheran Church had bought three adjoining lots here after the State of California condemned its South of Market church to build the San Francisco-Oakland Bay Bridge.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  4. 1958

    The congregation was renamed Gethsemane Lutheran Church.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  5. 1964

    Merged with St. Ansgar Lutheran Church at 152 Church Street to form St. Francis Lutheran Church. The statement dates the merger to 1964 in one passage and 1965 in another.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  6. 1977

    Finnish-language services ended and the building was converted into a daycare facility, its use ever since.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Counted among the six religious and fraternal buildings in the survey area: a two-storey wood-frame church with a parsonage and a social hall, designed by a church elder and former building contractor.
