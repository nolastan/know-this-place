Earlier record

  1. 1923

    Built for the Norwegian-Danish Methodist Church, which moved here from 3324 17th Street for a larger building and to sit inside San Francisco's Scandinavian community. The $50,000 building held a church, offices, a parsonage and several apartments.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  2. late 1930s

    The congregation renamed itself Central Methodist Church, around the time it dropped its Norwegian and Danish language services.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
  3. late 1980s

    Central Methodist Church appears to have closed. The building has in recent years housed Golden City Church.

    Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022
