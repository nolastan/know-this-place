Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

The 1925 convent of the Most Holy Redeemer parish complex, built with the school on the west side of Diamond Street. The citywide LGBTQ context statement also identified the building, as the Coming Home Hospice, for further study as a San Francisco Landmark.
