San Francisco Castro Douglass Street 109

109 Douglass Street

Castro (Eureka Valley) · San Francisco, CA 94114

  • Built 1895
  • 4-unit apartment building
  • 2 stories
  • Zoned RM-1
  • District 8
  • Historic status: unevaluated

The lots immediately north held the California Brewing Company's plant from 1892 until 1915, when the city bought their Douglass Street frontage for the Market Street extension. A service station stood on what was left of the ground from 1932 until it was demolished in 1980, and condominiums took the site in 1982.
