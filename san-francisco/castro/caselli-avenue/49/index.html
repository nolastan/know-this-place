Earlier record

  1. 1913

    One of a row of houses from 49 to 85 Caselli Avenue built by the D. Coffin Company in the Clover Heights subdivision about 1913, advertised as “Homes of Distinctive Charms” with six rooms and bath. The report calls the row seven houses in its text and eight in the builder's own advertisement.

    Corbett Heights Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
