Mission Dolores Neighborhood Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Here Today (1968)Listed

The Chula–Abbey district is defined here: the low-scale, freestanding cottages on Chula Lane — formerly Church Lane — and Abbey Street, which stand out among the larger row houses and flats of the rest of the neighbourhood and suggest a working-class character.
