Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleItalianate
Year built as surveyed1864
Address as surveyed591-593 Noe Street

Illustrated in the statement's survey of Eureka Valley property types and architectural styles. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as the earliest documented dwelling in the study area, a two-storey end-gable side hall form with corner quoins and bracketed window hoods, named in the evaluation framework as possibly eligible for the California Register as a rare survival of nineteenth-century urban fringe development.
