StyleQueen Anne

Listed among the houses Fernando Nelson built in Eureka Valley. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as a single-storey end-gable dwelling with entry porch and bay window, now three units.
