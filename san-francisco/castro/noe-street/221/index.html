Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

One of the best post-quake apartment buildings in Duboce Triangle: twelve units on a mid-block site made by assembling several house lots, with a rusticated concrete water table, a central entrance and four bay windows capped by a flat cornice on acanthus-leaf modillions. A figure caption prints the address as 211 Noe Street. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
