Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Put forward as a potential San Francisco Landmark, with the Phoenix Brewery, for its association with the early history of brewing and industrial production in Eureka Valley. The brewer's family lived on the site from 1878, first in a small 1.5-storey dwelling at the rear of the lot and later in a two-storey house set on Noe Street. The statement gives the residence as 560 Noe Street in its narrative and as 550 Noe Street in a figure caption, and gives 560 Noe Street as the brewery building in its industrial production framework.
