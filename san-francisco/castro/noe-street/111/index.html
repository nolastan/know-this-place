Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Address as surveyed113–115 Noe Street

Named as the statement's example of a post-quake flat erected singly rather than in a row — the most common type of building put up in Duboce Triangle in the years right after 1906. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
