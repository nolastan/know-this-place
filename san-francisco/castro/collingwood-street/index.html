Collingwood Street

Collingwood runs parallel to Castro, one block west, from 18th Street south to Alvarado. It is residential end to end — flats, small apartment buildings and single houses, with a commercial edge only where it meets 18th.

Historic status

No parcel on this street falls inside a historic district. Eight of the nine documented buildings are CEQA Category B — unevaluated and 45 years or older; 395 is Category C.

Not yet covered

Twenty-three condominium parcels on this street are individual units rather than buildings, and are held back until the building each belongs to can be established. Seventeen parcels have no record in the assessor's 2025 roll, and four are business addresses, deferred. The rest of the street remains undocumented.
