# Collingwood Street

Collingwood runs parallel to Castro, one block west, from 18th Street south to
Alvarado. It is residential end to end — flats, small apartment buildings and
single houses, with a commercial edge only where it meets 18th.

## Documented so far

Nine buildings, spread along the length of the street and built between 1900
and 1940. None falls inside a historic district; all but one are unevaluated
for historic significance.

- [23–27](23/) — a 1908 three-unit building on one parcel spanning three
  street numbers, its rear stair and porch rebuilt in 1994
- [59](59/) — a 1938 five-unit apartment building, three of its units
  remodelled together in 2014
- [73–77](73/) — a 1909 parcel carrying two structures, a six-unit building
  at the street and a cottage at the rear, with a permit record running
  1982–2025
- [153–155](153/) — a 1932 two-flat whose rear patio doors were replaced in 2023
- [157–159](157/) — a 1932 two-flat with two kitchens and two bathrooms
  remodelled in 2025
- [212](212/) — a 1900 three-unit building whose basement apartment and rear
  cottage were both converted in 1987
- [259–261](259/) — a 1936 two-unit building with 37 feet of foundation
  replaced in 2020
- [378](378/) — a 1940 house with a permit record running 1996–2026
- [395](395/) — a 1925 house excavated for a new basement level under a
  $563,898 permit in 2018

## Not yet covered

Twenty-three condominium parcels on this street are individual units rather
than buildings, and are held back until the building each belongs to can be
established. Seventeen parcels have no record in the assessor's 2025 roll, and
four are business addresses, deferred. The rest of the street remains
undocumented.
