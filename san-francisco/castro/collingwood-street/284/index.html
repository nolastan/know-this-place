Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleStick
Year built as surveyed1886-7

Attributed to John A. Swenson, a Swedish-born carpenter and ship joiner and the earliest builder the statement identifies in Eureka Valley. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as a cross-gable dwelling, and dates it 1886-7 where it lists Swenson's work and 1886 in its evaluation framework.
