Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleSecond Bay Tradition

A duplex dwelling put forward as possibly eligible for the California Register both for its association with the architecture firm and as a well-developed example of the Second Bay Tradition style in the city. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation.
