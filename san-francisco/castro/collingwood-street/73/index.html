73–77 Collingwood Street

Castro (Eureka Valley) · San Francisco, CA 94114

  • Built 1909
  • 7-unit TIC building
  • 3 stories
  • Zoned RH-3

The parcel carries two permitted structures — a six-unit apartment building fronting the street and a one-unit cottage at the rear, addressed separately in DBI records as apartments and a 1-family dwelling.
