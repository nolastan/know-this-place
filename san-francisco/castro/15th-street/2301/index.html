Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleMediterranean

The 12-unit apartment building in a planned development of six buildings put up between 1927 and 1928 on the site of a small chicken ranch — an unusual rural holdout for this part of the city. The statement calls the group the largest and most intact row of 1920s flats and apartment buildings in Duboce Triangle. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
