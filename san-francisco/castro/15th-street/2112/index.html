Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

One of only two purpose-built commercial buildings put up in Duboce Triangle between 1920 and 1939, and heavily altered since; long a retail paint store, and a branch bank outlet by 2022. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
