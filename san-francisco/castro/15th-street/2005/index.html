Here Today (1968)Listed

Appears eligible for local designation under Criterion 3 (Architecture) as a rare remaining example of Gothic Revival institutional architecture in San Francisco. Character-defining features are the steeply pitched front gable roof, the fish-scale shingles, stucco cladding scored to resemble ashlar masonry, stained-glass lancet windows and a heavy wood panelled door. Drawn from a Planning Department DPR Primary Record of 2007.
