Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleCraftsman / Classical Revival / Mediterranean

One of four two-unit buildings put up between 1923 and 1925 on the grounds of the largest of the Victorian suburban villas on this block, which a now unknown contractor bought and demolished around 1923. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
