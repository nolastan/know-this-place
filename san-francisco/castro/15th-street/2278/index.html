Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleContractor Modern

One of the postwar “dingbat” apartment buildings the statement groups under the Contractor Modern style; it singles this one out for its unusual pebble-cast cladding and angled bay windows. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
