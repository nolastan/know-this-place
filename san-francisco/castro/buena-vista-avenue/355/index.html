355 Buena Vista Avenue is the west building of a condominium pair, its 101 apartments held as 101 separate parcels — lots 117 through 217 on block 2607 — numbered 101W through 711W. The east building at 351 Buena Vista Avenue carries a further 35 parcels, suffixed E, which the roll dates a year later.

The assessor records this building as six stories; its unit numbers run in seven tiers.

This page's parcel is lot 117, unit 101W, so the assessed value and last sale shown below are that unit's and not the building's.
