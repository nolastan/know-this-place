    The Kaposi's Sarcoma Research and Education Foundation - later the San Francisco AIDS Foundation - opened here in 1982, the first agency in the city specifically addressing the new disease. Identified as potentially eligible for landmark designation.

Illustrated in the statement's survey of Eureka Valley property types and architectural styles. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as commercial buildings later restyled in the Mediterranean Revival manner. The citywide LGBTQ context statement identified the San Francisco AIDS Foundation here for further study as a San Francisco Landmark.
