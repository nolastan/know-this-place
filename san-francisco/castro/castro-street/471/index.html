Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named as the building that housed the Nasser Brothers' motion picture theatre, opened 1910, the predecessor of the Castro Theater built up the street in 1922. The statement refers to the building by both of the street numbers the city carries on this parcel: 471 Castro Street in its property-type chapter, and 479 Castro Street in a footnote and in a quotation from the citywide LGBTQ context statement, which describes newly arrived residents of the 1970s buying paint, hammers and tools at Cliff's Variety here to restore run-down Victorians. Its recommendations also give 479 Castro Street as the address of the Castro Theater, which is Landmark #100 at 429.
