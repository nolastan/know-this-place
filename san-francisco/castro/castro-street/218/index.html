Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleMediterranean

One of five flats in a planned development of six buildings put up between 1927 and 1928 on the site of a small chicken ranch, with 210–212, 214–216 and 222–224 Castro Street, 2309 15th Street and the apartment building at 2301 15th Street. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
