Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleQueen Anne and Stick
Year built as surveyed1894

Listed among the houses Charles L. Hinkel and his sons built in Eureka Valley. The statement gives the signature Hinkel details as rounded architrave window mouldings with bevelled keystones and bracket-and-strip architraves at the roofline. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as a side hall row house.
