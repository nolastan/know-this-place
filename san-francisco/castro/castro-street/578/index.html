Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleStick
Year built as surveyed1897

Listed among the houses Fernando Nelson built in Eureka Valley. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records it as a two-flat, now three units. The citywide LGBTQ context statement identified the Castro Rock Steam Baths here for further study as a San Francisco Landmark.
