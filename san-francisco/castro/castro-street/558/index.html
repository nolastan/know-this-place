  1. 1904

    Built.

  2. 1970s

    Paperback Traffic, a bookstore with a well-regarded poetry reading series featuring gay writers, opened in the early 1970s. Valet Men's Wear, one of the first gay-oriented clothing stores in the neighbourhood, was at 564 Castro Street on the same parcel.

    Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
  3. 1974

    Small Press Traffic, formed in 1974 to support experimental, queer and writers of colour, began in a back room rented from the bookstore here.

    Donna J. Graves and Shayne E. Watson, Citywide Historic Context Statement for LGBTQ History in San Francisco, San Francisco Planning Department, adopted by the Historic Preservation Commission November 18, 2015
