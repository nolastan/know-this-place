Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleClassical Revival

Named as one of two major bank branches built on Castro Street in the 1920s and put forward as possibly eligible for the California Register both for its association with the development of a full-service commercial district in Eureka Valley and as a distinctive example of a Classical Revival bank building.
