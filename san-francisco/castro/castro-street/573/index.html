Earlier record

  1. 1973

    Harvey Milk opened a small camera shop at 573 Castro Street and lived above it at 575. He ran for the Board of Supervisors that year, lost, and was elected in 1977 as the first openly gay person to win public office in California.

    Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017
