Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleArt Deco

One of only two Art Deco buildings the statement finds in Duboce Triangle, and its best 1930s apartment building in the style; it replaced an older Victorian-era cottage on the site. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
