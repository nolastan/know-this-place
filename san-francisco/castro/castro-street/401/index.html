Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleMediterranean Revival

Listed among the five designated San Francisco Landmarks in the study area, as Landmark #264. The statement dates the building to 1901 and its Mediterranean Revival restyling to 1923. It gives the tavern's address as 401 Castro Street where it lists the landmarks, and as 410 Castro Street where it quotes the citywide LGBTQ context statement — 410 being the Bank of America branch across the intersection.
