Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Built in 1973 on the site of a Victorian dwelling destroyed by fire in 1959, and read by the statement as one of the later flats sharing more with the “Richmond Specials” of the 1970s than with the dingbats before them. A figure caption prints the address as 282 Church Street. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
