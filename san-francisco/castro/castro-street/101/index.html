Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleLate Moderne remodel of a 1908 mixed-use building

Named as one of several Duboce Triangle buildings remodelled under the New Deal home-improvement programs of the Home Owners Loan Corporation and the Federal Housing Administration, which paid for stripping wood ornament and re-coating exteriors in stucco. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
