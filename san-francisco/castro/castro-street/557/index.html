Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named among the study area's extant historic automotive commercial resources as a masonry automobile repair garage of 1915, and put forward with the car showroom at 2355 Market Street as possibly eligible for the California Register as an early example of an automobile-oriented building type in the neighbourhood.
