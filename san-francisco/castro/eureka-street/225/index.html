  1. 1886

    The Pacific Dairy was one of four dairies still working in Eureka Valley in the mid-1880s, alongside one at 17th and Douglass streets, one at Diamond and 18th with a horse corral opposite the cattle yard, and a milk ranch on 19th Street between Douglass and Eureka. Most of the dairy operations were gone by 1900 as land became more valuable for building than for animal production.

    Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017

Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named as the one identified property in the study area associated with agricultural production: the dwelling associated with the Pacific Dairy, which operated at Eureka and 19th streets. The statement recommends a thematic survey of surviving agricultural properties in the former western neighbourhoods of San Francisco on the strength of it.
