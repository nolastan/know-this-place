  1. 1900

    Eggers & Company ran a soda and mineral water manufactory and bottling works from the rear ell of the house and then a series of small outbuildings, advertising itself as a dealer in natural mineral waters and a manufacturer of soda water and superior ginger ale. The firm had previously sold mineral water from the Tolenas Springs in Solano County and appears to have gone on bottling Tolenas spring water here until about 1903.

    Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement, adopted 2017

Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

Named as one of the most intact surviving properties associated with industrial production in Eureka Valley: the Eggers family home, with perhaps some portion of the soda and mineral water bottling works attached to the dwelling.
