# Castro / Eureka Valley

One neighborhood in central San Francisco, known by more than one name. These
pages follow the city's own boundary — the Castro/Upper Market analysis
neighborhood — which takes in Eureka Valley proper along with Duboce Triangle,
Corona Heights, and the slopes climbing west toward Twin Peaks. Its housing
stock is heavily Victorian and Edwardian.

## Castro or Eureka Valley?

Both — they are the same place. **Eureka Valley** is the older name, for the
valley the neighborhood occupies; **the Castro** is what almost everyone
calls it now, after its main street. This site files addresses under "Castro"
and notes Eureka Valley alongside it.

The city's own records are no more consistent, which is worth knowing when
reading the sources cited on each building's page:

- Addressing system and planning datasets: "Castro/Upper Market"
- Assessor's roll: "Eureka Valley/Dolores Heights"
- In everyday use: "the Castro"

One part of that city boundary is filed elsewhere on this site:
[Corbett Heights](../corbett-heights/), the hillside north-west of the Castro,
which local histories treat as its own neighborhood.

Every parcel the assessor's roll calls residential now has a page, built from
the city's own records. Condominium parcels are held back until the building
each unit belongs to can be established, and parcels missing from the roll are
skipped. What the pages don't yet carry is the history — architects, builders,
who lived there — and that arrives one building at a time.
