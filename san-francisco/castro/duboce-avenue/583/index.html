Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleMission Revival

By far the best of only four Mission Revival buildings the statement finds in Duboce Triangle, and one of the six religious and fraternal buildings in the survey area. The congregation, a Disciples of Christ church that came from the South of Market area after the 1906 earthquake, still owns and operates it. One of the properties the statement puts forward as a potential city landmark candidate, a list it calls 37 properties in one passage and 38 in another and prints with 38 entries; it says further research will be required to confirm any of them. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
