Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleQueen Anne / Classical Revival
Year built as surveyedCa. 1905

Inside the proposed Duboce Park Queen Anne/Classical Revival Rowhouse District, one of five local landmark districts the statement recommends: four related rowhouses built Ca. 1905 by the same, now unknown, builder, which the statement likens to “Postcard Row” opposite Alamo Square. All four are contributors to the California Register-eligible Duboce Triangle Historic District. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association. Dates it gives as “Ca.” are its own estimates.
