Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleArt Deco

One of only two Art Deco buildings the statement finds in Duboce Triangle, and one of two mixed-use buildings put up in the neighborhood itself in the 1930s; it stands in the small Church Street commercial district. Built by a contractor for Emmanuel Stagnaro, who ran an Italian restaurant in the ground-floor unit. The statement dates it 1937 in one passage and, in another, to 1911 with an Art Deco remodel Ca. 1935. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
