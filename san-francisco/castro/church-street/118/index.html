Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Built in 1968 on one of the last vacant lots in Duboce Triangle, and read by the statement as one of the later flats sharing more with the “Richmond Specials” of the 1970s than with the dingbats before them. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
