Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

Address as surveyed401 Duboce Avenue / 100 Church Street

One of only two purpose-built commercial buildings put up in Duboce Triangle between 1920 and 1939, and the anchor of the neighborhood's small business district. Built as a light industrial and commercial loft — a contractors' storage facility with offices on the second floor — by an architect and real estate investor. The statement gives the address as both 401 Duboce Avenue and 100 Church Street. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
