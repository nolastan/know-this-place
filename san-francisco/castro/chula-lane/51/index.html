Mission Dolores Neighborhood Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Photographed as an example of the single-family cottages the statement records on Chula Lane and Abbey Street; the assessor records two dwellings on this parcel. The Chula–Abbey district is defined here: the low-scale, freestanding cottages on Chula Lane — formerly Church Lane — and Abbey Street, which stand out among the larger row houses and flats of the rest of the neighbourhood and suggest a working-class character. The district summaries and map the survey-findings chapter says are attached are not in the adopted file, so it names no contributors.
