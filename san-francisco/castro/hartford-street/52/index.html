Eureka Valley Historic Context Statement (2017)

StyleQueen Anne, some altered
Year built as surveyed1901-1902
Address as surveyed20-64 Hartford Street

Listed among the houses Fernando Nelson built in Eureka Valley. The statement is reconnaissance-level and says inclusion in it is not a judgement on a building's worthiness for preservation. It records the row as single-storey end-gable dwellings with entry porch and bay window, and names Nelson's Hartford Street houses as a concentration of developer housing worth studying as a potential historic district. The recommendations print the range as 2-64 Hartford; the builder table prints it as 20-64.
