# Hartford Street

A single block running between 17th and 18th Streets, one block east of Castro.
Its west side was built as a speculative tract by Fernando Nelson in 1900–1902
and is now the Hartford Street Historic District.

## Documented so far

Eight buildings, 19 through 43, all built in 1900 or 1901 and all inside the
historic district — so all carry CEQA Category A. The district is eligible for
the California Register but is not listed on it, and is not an Article 10
district, so the buildings carry no local landmark protection.

- [19](19/) — a 1901 house reroofed twice in September 2019, three weeks apart
- [20](20/) — a 1901 house with a 1989 horizontal-addition permit that expired
- [24](24/) — a 1900 house remodelled 2015–2020 with a new foundation and
  reconfigured upper floors
- [25](25/) — a 1901 house remodelled inside and extended to the rear from 2015
- [28](28/) — a 1901 house whose rear was rebuilt in 2001 with a breakfast room
  and a roof-deck spa
- [32](32/) — a 1901 house whose brick foundation was replaced and a chimney
  removed in 2022
- [36](36/) — a 1901 house with a 1993 rear-addition proposal that was
  disapproved
- [41–43](41/) — a 1901 two-flat on one parcel spanning both numbers, its
  chimneys removed in 1995

## Not yet covered

Twenty condominium parcels on this street are individual units rather than
buildings, and are held back until the building each belongs to can be
established. Seven parcels have no record in the assessor's 2025 roll. The rest
of the street remains undocumented.
