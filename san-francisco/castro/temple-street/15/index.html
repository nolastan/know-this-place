  1. 1891

    A four-room cottage of 895 square feet went up at number 17 about 1891, and stood in that form until 2014.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "February Neighborhood News", Vol. VII, No. 2 (1 February 2025)
  2. 1910

    Home from about 1910 to 1919 of Alexander Horr (1873–1947), the Hungarian-born anarchist bookseller and author of Fabian Anarchism, secretary of the Auto Bus Operators' Union, who stood for governor of California in 1922 and for mayor of San Francisco in 1931. The cottage was offered for sale in 1919 at $1,800 with $50 down.

    Corbett Heights Neighbors, "February Neighborhood News", Vol. VII, No. 2 (1 February 2025)
