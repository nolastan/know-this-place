Mission Dolores Neighborhood Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Address as surveyed455–457 Sanchez Street
Here Today (1968)Listed

One of a row of Romeo flats on the east side of Sanchez Street between Dorland Street and 18th Street, retaining a good to high level of integrity. The Romeo flat is a San Francisco type built mostly after 1906: three bays, the central one holding a winding staircase with a balcony or landing window at each storey, and narrow flats stacked in the outer two.
