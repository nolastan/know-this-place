Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

The only San Francisco Housing Authority project inside Duboce Triangle: a four-storey wood-frame building of 90 units set aside for elderly residents, put up in 1972 on the site of a gas station and an older Victorian dwelling. It was rehabilitated by Bridge Housing Corporation and rededicated in 2019 as affordable senior housing. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
