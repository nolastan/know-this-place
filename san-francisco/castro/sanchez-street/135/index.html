Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleLate Moderne

The only non-residential building put up in Duboce Triangle in the 1940s, and one of three institutional buildings in the survey area with Davies Medical Center and McKinley Elementary School. Built on a vacant lot under a citywide bond issue to improve fire protection in the neighborhoods, it was the first fire station in Duboce Triangle and, apart from the school, its only local government building. Seismically upgraded in 2000 with no significant exterior alterations. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
