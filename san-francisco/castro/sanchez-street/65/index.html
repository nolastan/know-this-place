Duboce Triangle Historic Context Statement (2022)

StyleMinimal Traditional

One of a pair of four-unit apartment buildings built in 1939, with 71 Sanchez Street, that replaced a row of Victorian flats. Christopher VerPlanck's context statement of 2022 surveyed 552 properties in Duboce Triangle for the Duboce Triangle Neighborhood Association.
