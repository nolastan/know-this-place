Mission Dolores Neighborhood Historic Context Statement, adopted 2022

Survey findingIndividually eligible for the National Register and the California Register

Found eligible under Criterion C/3 (Architecture); with further analysis the property may also qualify under Criterion A/1 (Events) for its association with the reconstruction that followed the 1906 earthquake and fire. The survey's DPR forms are drafts — the California Office of Historic Preservation declined in 2013 to enter the survey's 2010 findings in the State Historic Resources Inventory.
