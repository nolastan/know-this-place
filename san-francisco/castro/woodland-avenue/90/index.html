The Darling House, designed in 1936 by Richard Neutra — named among the notable International Style buildings in San Francisco, a style the statement calls extremely rare here in domestic architecture and generally limited to custom-designed single-family houses of 1935-1950.

The Modern design context statement dates the Darling House to 1936 in two places, and lists a Richard Neutra remodel here in 1939. Not yet documented: the developer and the early residents. Submit an update
