  1. 1883

    700–702 Tennessee Street

    A single-family dwelling of 1883, built by a more affluent skilled laborer, stood at 700–702 Tennessee Street, in a row of Italianate multi-family dwellings put up along the west side of the block between 1883 and 1886. The city's address register puts 700 Tennessee Street and 707 18th Street on this one parcel.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
