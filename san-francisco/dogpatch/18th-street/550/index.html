  1. circa 1930

    Small oil companies at Third and 18th

    A cluster of small oil-company buildings, put up to serve the Central Waterfront's larger industries, stood around the Third and 18th Street intersection; two of them were at 550 18th Street and 2075 Third Street.

    Central Waterfront Survey and Context Statement, adopted 2001
