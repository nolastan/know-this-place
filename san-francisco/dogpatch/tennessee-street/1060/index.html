Earlier record

  1. 1867

    The Outside Lands Committee set this parcel aside for a school, one of several it reserved in outlying sections of the city.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
  2. 1877

    The San Francisco School Department built an eight-room frame school facing Minnesota Street, replacing the Potrero School of 1865 at Napa and Kentucky Streets.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
  3. 1895

    An addition facing Tennessee Street. The school was renamed for the superintendent of Union Iron Works, who gave money towards its construction and equipment.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
  4. 1899

    Schoolchildren from the school escorted President McKinley to the laying of the keel of the USS Ohio at Union Iron Works.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001

Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

One of four public and civic buildings still standing in Dogpatch in 2001. Serving a working-class neighborhood, the school taught practical trades — cooking and homemaking for the girls, manual training for the boys.
