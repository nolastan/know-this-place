Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

Listed as a non-contributor to the proposed Dogpatch Historic District, among 14 buildings the survey dated after the 1867–1945 period of significance, and described as a modern residence. It stands inside the run of addresses — 1002 to 1014 Tennessee Street — that the survey gave for the row of Pelton cottages.
