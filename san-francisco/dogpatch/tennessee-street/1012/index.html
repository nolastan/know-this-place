Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

StyleEastlake
Address as surveyed1002–1014 Tennessee Street

One of thirteen identical workers' cottages — sixteen originally — standing on Tennessee and Minnesota Streets between 20th and 22nd Streets, and built to the four-room design Pelton published in the San Francisco Evening Bulletin on 8 May 1880, which he priced at $854.25 on a 20-foot lot.
