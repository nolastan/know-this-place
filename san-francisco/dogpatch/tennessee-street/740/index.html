Earlier record

  1. 1871

    The Kentucky Street Methodist Episcopal Church was built on a donated 60-by-100-foot lot at the corner of Michigan and Sierra Streets.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
  2. 1881

    The church arrived on this site, on the west side of Tennessee Street between Butte and Solano Streets. The survey says in one place that the building was constructed here in 1881 and in another that the church was moved here that year.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
  3. 1904

    The last city-directory listing for the Potrero Methodist Episcopal Church, whose congregation had shrunk as Irish-born residents replaced the native-born shipyard workers it served.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
  4. 1918

    Converted into a combined pasta factory and multi-family dwelling.

    Dogpatch Historic Context Statement, adopted 2001
