Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

Address as surveyed800–04 22nd Street

The survey names 800–04 22nd Street among O'Donnell's work; the assessor splits those numbers between this parcel and 804–806 next door. One of a cluster of eight multi-family dwellings on the north side of the 800 block, several with stores on the ground floor, in styles including Queen Anne and Classical Revival.
