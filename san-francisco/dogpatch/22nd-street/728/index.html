Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

Address as surveyed726–32 and 726–30 22nd Street

One of two residential hotels in Dogpatch, built after the turn of the century to take the influx of shipyard workers. The survey also lists 726–30 22nd Street among the heavily altered historic dwellings. The city's register carries no 726 22nd Street.
