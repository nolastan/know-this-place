Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

Address as surveyed833 22nd Street

One of two larger stables used by commercial drayage firms still standing in Dogpatch in 2001 — the other, of 1894, is behind 1100 Tennessee Street. Stables at the rear of residential lots were generally not inventoried separately.
