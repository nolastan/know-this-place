Dogpatch Historic District Survey (2001)

Address as surveyed700 and 700–02 22nd Street

Named as a good example of Dogpatch's commercial buildings with residences above — typically two stories on a corner lot — and as the address of Brady's Liquors. The city's register carries no 700 22nd Street.
