  1. 1922

    A single-storey unreinforced masonry building in common bond brickwork was built; its permit gave the intended use as a brick foundry and warehouse. It was classified as offices from 1968.

    2225-2255 Third Street DEIR, SF Planning Department, 2007
  2. 1924

    A three-storey unreinforced masonry building faced in common bond brickwork was built; its permit gave the intended use as light manufacturing and warehouse. It was reclassified commercial in 1988 and converted to offices in 2000.

    2225-2255 Third Street DEIR, SF Planning Department, 2007
  3. 1999

    The scrap metal business that had used both buildings, and most of the open lot around them, closed for good.

    2225-2255 Third Street DEIR, SF Planning Department, 2007
