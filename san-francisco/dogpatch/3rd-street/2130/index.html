Central Waterfront Cultural Resources Survey (2001)

StyleArt Deco

One of a cluster of small industrial buildings in this style along Third Street, dated circa 1938–1946. The street was widened by as much as twenty feet in the late 1930s, forcing either new construction or new facades on what was already there.
