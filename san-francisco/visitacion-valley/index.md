# Visitacion Valley

The city's Visitacion Valley analysis neighborhood, in the south-eastern corner of the city between McLaren Park and the county line. Coverage begins with the parcels the Office of the Assessor-Recorder photographed for tax assessment in 1955 and 1964.

## Adjacent neighborhoods

McLaren Park closes the valley to the west, with [the
Excelsior](../excelsior/) beyond it. [Portola](../portola/) adjoins
north-east and [Bayview Hunters Point](../bayview-hunters-point/) east
across Bayshore Boulevard; the San Mateo County line is the southern edge.
