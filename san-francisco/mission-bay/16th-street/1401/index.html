California Historical Resource Status Codes: a 1 or 2 means listed or formally determined eligible, a 3 means the survey judged it eligible, a 5 means locally significant, a 6 that it was found ineligible, and a 7 that it was not evaluated. The 2009 context statement gives the status code 3CS; the 2011 survey data table gives 6L.

Modern & Postmodern Architectural Styles Historic Context Statement (2024)

Architect as surveyedAlbert Roller (original); Gensler & Associates (renovation)
Year built as surveyed1987

A former industrial building by Albert Roller, leased in 1987 as the first San Francisco home of the California College of the Arts architecture programme and renovated for the school by Gensler & Associates.

The survey's data table dates the building to 1951; the context statement's appendix dates it to 1909. Not yet documented: the developer and the early tenants. Submit an update
