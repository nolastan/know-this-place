  1. 1905

    Built as the Baker & Hamilton warehouse, a heavy timber-framed brick building that became the model for several later warehouses in the district. Designed by Sutton & Weeks.

    Showplace Square / Northeast Mission Historic Resource Survey, adopted 2011
  2. 1918

    Baker & Hamilton moved into larger quarters here in 1918; the building, now Baker Hamilton Square, carries City Landmark No. 193.

    San Francisco Planning Department, 475 Brannan Street: Draft Environmental Impact Report, Case No. 97.470E, 1998.
