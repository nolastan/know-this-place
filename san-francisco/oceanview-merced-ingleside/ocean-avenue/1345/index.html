  1. 1910

    A Sunday school for 1906 earthquake refugees, begun in 1907 in the basement of the nearby Robinson Apartments, moved into the first purpose-built Presbyterian church on this site in 1910 (not extant).

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
  2. 1920

    The 1910 wood-framed Presbyterian church on this site burned down in 1920.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010
  3. 1921

    Ingleside Terraces developer Joseph Leonard donated the land and designed the replacement church, built as a place for various Protestant faiths to worship. The context statement's narrative text dates the building to 1921; a photo caption in the same document dates it to 1923. Both readings are recorded here.

    OMI Neighborhoods Historical Context Statement, adopted 2010

  19. 2024

    The Great Cloud of Witnesses, an indoor collage and mural assembled over three decades inside Ingleside Presbyterian Church, covers its interior with thousands of images of civil rights figures, popular culture and congregation members. The church and mural together are a designated city landmark.

    African American Citywide Historic Context Statement, adopted 2024
