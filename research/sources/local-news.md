- **What:** Context and stories: Hoodline Castro archives, Bay Area Reporter
  archive (ebar.com), SF Chronicle, and syndicated wire coverage that surfaces
  per address — a venue's closing night is often reported outside San Francisco
  and nowhere in it that is still online. No structured APIs — this is
  cite-the-URL browsing territory. Quote sparingly; summarize and link.
- **Use for:** notable events tied to a specific address. Skip for routine
  seeding.
- **Why it rates low on search-invisibility:** these outlets are indexed and
  already rank for the addresses they mention, so mining them adds little that a
  reader couldn't find. Use them to verify and to date an event, not as a corpus
  to pass over.
- **Citation label:** name the outlet, the headline and the publication date,
  and link the article.
