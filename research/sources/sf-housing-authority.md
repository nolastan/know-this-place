- **What:** The city's public housing agency, created in 1938. SFPL scanned
  its publications onto the Internet Archive under the creator "Housing
  Authority of the City and County of San Francisco" (and "Commission of the
  Housing Authority…" for the minutes). The early annual reports
  (`annualreportofho1938hous` binds the 1st to 8th, April 1939 to 1946) give
  each project's architects, landscape architect, contractors, contract price
  and the dates bids were opened, ground was broken and tenants moved in.
  `roadtogoldenager1960hous` (1960) gives the opening year and unit count of
  every project to 1962, with street corners. The commission minutes,
  `minutesofregular<year>comm` (1974-2005; items with odd numbers such as
  `1319comm`, `2720comm` are undated volumes), cover modernization contracts,
  demolitions and the HOPE VI rebuildings.
- **Getting at it:** `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`. Find
  the creator's items with the IA advanced search,
  `creator:("Housing Authority of the City and County of San Francisco")`.
  In the bound annual reports the 5th report (April 1943) has a project table:
  name, architects in parentheses marked `*`, land cost, acres, units,
  construction cost and contractor marked `**`; the OCR keeps the rows in order.
- **Placing:** the reports name projects, not addresses. Match by the street
  corner in the 1960 report and the roll's unit count and build year:
  Holly Courts is lot 5714001 (131-161 Highland, 100 Appleton; 118 units,
  1940); Potrero Terrace is 4220A001 (page 900 Connecticut), 4222A001 and
  4223001; Westside Courts is 1075001 (2501 Sutter); Ping Yuen is 0178001
  (711 Pacific) and Ping Yuen North/Annex 0160030 (822 Pacific); Alemany
  is 5843007 (801 Ellsworth). Sunnydale's land has no parcel in EAS.
  Most projects of 1940-62 have been demolished and rebuilt (Valencia
  Gardens, North Beach Place, Bernal Dwellings, Hayes Valley, Hunters View,
  Alice Griffith, Yerba Buena Plaza): frame those facts as site history.
- **Privacy:** tenants, applicants and staff are private people; commissioners
  and the executive director are public officials but are not page content.
  Architects, contractors and namesakes of projects may be named.
