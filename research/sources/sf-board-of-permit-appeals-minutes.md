- **What:** The minutes of the Board of Permit Appeals, the five-member board
  that heard appeals from City departments' decisions on permits. Each meeting
  is a list of appeals, and each appeal prints its number (`6833` in 1974,
  `84-315` from 1981, `V84-310` for a variance), the parties, the decision
  appealed ("Denial on December 4, 1984, of permit to alter a building at
  499 Alabama Street"), who spoke, and the Board's vote. Four votes were
  needed to overrule a department, so a 3-2 vote to overrule upheld it.
  The address-level facts are **what the Board decided about a building's
  permit**, dated to the day — alterations, additions, demolitions, new
  buildings, changes of use, unit legalisations, variances — and, often,
  **which business or institution was in the building** (a theatre, a bar,
  a church, a school, a garage) and what it was (a two-unit building, a
  residential hotel of 40 rooms). It covers 1981–1993, which the Planning
  Commission minutes do not.
- **Where:** The San Francisco Public Library's volumes on the Internet
  Archive, collection `sanfranciscopubliclibrary`, one item per year:
  `minutesofmeeting1974sanf` … `minutesofmeeting1996sanf`, with
  `minutesofmeeting19871988s` and `minutesofmeeting19931994s` covering two
  years each. Cite `https://archive.org/details/<item>/page/n<N>/mode/1up`.
  `minutesofmeeting1987sanf` is a different body (the Telecommunications
  Policy Committee).
- **How to get at it:** The `_djvu.txt` OCR has no page breaks. For page
  numbers, download `<item>_hocr_searchtext.txt.gz`,
  `<item>_hocr_pageindex.json.gz` and `<item>_scandata.xml` into
  `raw/` and split them into `pages/<item>/n<NNNN>.txt` (viewer page
  numbers skip the scandata pages not added to access formats). Pages are
  dated by the meeting date in their running head ("MINUTES OF THE BOARD OF
  PERMIT APPEALS HEARING OF JANUARY 9, 1985 PAGE TWO").
- **What is actually usable:** Appeals on building, alteration, demolition
  and sign permits from the Department of Public Works (Bureau of Building
  Inspection), the Zoning Administrator's variances and determinations, the
  Fire and Health Departments' occupancy permits, and police permits for
  places of entertainment, dance halls, cabarets and amusement devices, which
  name the business at the address. Low value and declined: continuances,
  rehearing requests, withdrawals, and the many penalty-fee disputes for
  work done without a permit.
- **Cautions:** The 1981 scan crops the first letter of every line. The
  1995–1996 OCR double-spaces words. Some volumes print an appeal's number
  wrongly at one meeting and rightly at the next; the reader recorded what
  was printed and noted the conflict. `minutesofmeeting1980sanf` stops at
  20 August 1980; `1minutesofmeeting1980sanf` (62 images) holds 24
  September 1980 onward and has **not been read**. 1987's minutes are
  mostly missing from the combined 1987–1988 volume (11 meetings).
- **People:** The appellant lines are full of private people — owners,
  neighbours, permit holders ("issuance … to Mr. X"), attorneys, and
  "X dba Y". Never take them. Businesses, institutions and firms may be
  named; for "X dba Y" only Y. The project's architect may be named when
  the minutes give the role; those are kept in the finding's extra
  (`architect_as_recorded`) and not put on pages, because they are the
  architect of an alteration, not of the building. Police revocations and
  suspensions of massage, adult, second-hand and similar permits are not
  published: the reason is an allegation about people, and the business's
  presence is recorded as an occupancy on its own. Permits held by a
  person (masseuse trainee, taxi, peddler) are not facts about a building.
- **Citation label:** `San Francisco Board of Permit Appeals, minutes of
  the meeting of 9 January 1985 (Internet Archive, minutesofmeeting1985sanf,
  page n12)`; on a page, the source name is "San Francisco Board of Permit
  Appeals, minutes of the meeting of 9 January 1985".
- **Coverage:** Every volume from `minutesofmeeting1974sanf` to
  `minutesofmeeting1996sanf` read whole (996 meetings), one findings file a
  year (`appeals-<year>.json`); each file's `coverage` block names the
  meetings not in the scan. **Remaining:** `1minutesofmeeting1980sanf`
  (62 images, 24 September 1980 onward) has not been read. The Board's
  minutes after 1996 are not in the Library's scanned run. 4,715 findings
  were declined on purpose — 2,956 procedural (continuances, rehearings,
  withdrawals, dismissals), 1,031 penalty-fee disputes, 221 police
  revocations, suspensions and vice-permit decisions, 60 personal licences,
  about 150 not confirmed against the OCR (quote or street number not
  found), duplicates and facts about a predecessor building — and 1,402
  stayed unresolved, most of them an address EAS no longer holds.
- **Verified:** 2026-11-10 (first pass, the whole run — read 21 volumes /
  996 meetings, found 8,815, resolved 7,413, published 3,407 on 2,513 pages.
  Every quotation was matched against the volume's OCR: 7,893 verbatim,
  834 near-verbatim (OCR noise), 88 not found and declined; 62 street
  numbers absent from the OCR were declined. 1981 was audited row by row
  before the rest arrived. **What the run learned:** the reader wrote
  person names into reader notes and quotes even when told not to (a
  permit holder "dba" a business, a letter-writer); names were removed on
  conversion by matching the names the minutes themselves print after
  Mr./Mrs./Esq. and in the appellant lines — a capitalised-word filter
  catches street names (O'Farrell, Sansome) and is useless here. The 1974
  and 1975 descriptions carry the reader's notes appended after a double
  space; take the first sentence only. Seeding ~1,650 pages in one
  `seed-list` call outlasts a two-hour background limit; it resumes
  cleanly on the parcels without a page.)
