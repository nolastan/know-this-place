- **What:** Two boards. The Board of Trustees of the Fine Arts Museums of San
  Francisco is the city's board for the de Young and the Legion of Honor
  (`minutesagendas<year>fine`, 1973-82 and 2000-06). The Corporation of The Fine
  Arts Museums (COFAM) is the private board that raised the money and ran the
  building projects (`minutesorderofbu<year>corp`, `minutes<year>corp`,
  1987-2001). The COFAM volumes carry the building history: the architects'
  engagements, master plans, the Legion of Honor renovation's cost reports,
  and the new de Young from conceptual plan to demolition of the old building.
  The 2005 FAMSF volume has the resolutions of thanks at the new de Young's
  opening, which name the design architect, the principal architect, the
  landscape architect and the general contractor.
- **Getting at it:** `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`. Items
  found by searching IA for `minutesagendas fine` and `minutesorderofbu corp`.
  Meetings are dated by the header nearest before the passage
  ("Board of Trustees June 8, 2000", "Executive Committee September 9, 1999",
  or "Thursday, April 12, 2001"); the agenda printed ahead of each meeting
  repeats the item titles, so take a fact from the minutes' own text, and
  check that a resolution was adopted (its "Board Resolution N" line).
  `/mnt/project-files/famsf/q.py REGEX [width]` prints matches with that header.
- **Cautions:** Most of each volume is exhibitions, acquisitions, budgets and
  fund-raising. Plans and timelines (groundbreaking "May 15-19, 2002",
  topping-off "October 18, 2003") are announced and not always reported as
  held; publish only what the minutes report as done. Trustees and staff are
  named throughout; leave them out. A 1990 Barnes master plan for the de
  Young was approved but never built; it is published as a planning decision.
