- **What:** The trustees' yearly letter to the Mayor on the War Memorial Opera
  House and Veterans Building (301 Van Ness), and from 1980 on Davies Symphony
  Hall and Zellerbach Rehearsal Hall (201 Van Ness, also 300 Franklin). Each
  letter has a capital-improvements section: what was built, remodelled or
  certified complete that fiscal year, and who moved in or out.
- **Getting at it:** `https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`; items
  `annualrepofpr19391946wa`, `annualreportofpr1947warm`,
  `sttmntsofact19631978warm`, `annualrprt1978798384warm`,
  `annualreport199899sanf`, `annualreportsanf2011sanf`,
  `reportonrehabili1196publ`. Date a passage by the nearest letter heading
  before it ("Honorable Joseph L. Alioto September 10, 1968"); some headings
  are lost to OCR, so check the fiscal year the text names.
- **Cautions:** Most of each letter is budgets, rentals and season statistics.
  The 1960s letters press for a concert hall "across Franklin Street"; that
  site (blocks 785 and 792) is not where Davies Hall was built. The reports
  disagree on Davies Hall's opening day (6 and 16 September 1980), so it is
  published to the month. Trustees and staff are named; leave them out.
  Curated list and converter: `/mnt/project-files/war-memorial/W1.py`, `convert.py`.
